## Lag / freshness

- Capture at 16:05 ET is **after** the 16:00 ET US
  close, so the session itself is finished. The screener still serves a
  900-second delayed stream: the US `close` column is the tape as of
  roughly 15:45-16:00 ET, not the official settling close.
- Measured against Yahoo's official daily close on 24
  large caps: median |Δ| **0.5679%**,
  max 1.3053%. Names flat into the close match
  exactly; the gap is real price movement in the last 15 minutes.
- Every international region agrees with Yahoo to **<0.001 pp** median
  |Δ change| — their sessions are complete and those values are final.
- Method note: lag is measured by matching the screener close against
  Yahoo 5-minute bars. Closest-bar matching overstates the lag when a
  price is flat (it can land on an earlier, identical print), so quote
  the official-close comparison above rather than the bar delta.

## Caveats

- Extreme movers are dominated by sub-$100M micro-caps and preferred
  series with near-zero volume (single-print ±50-600% swings). Apply a
  market-cap floor before reading the movers list.
- `eu-de` still carries a small tail of London-listed German structured
  products (LSX/LS codes) — they survive the venue collapse because they
  have no domestic listing.
- Cross-listed issuers appear in more than one region file with
  different change_pct (known skill limitation; no cross-file dedupe).
- 4 blank `change_pct` cells were filled from Yahoo Finance; blank
  `pe_ratio` = no/negative earnings, blank `market_cap` = no market cap
  reported.
- No movers-mode forward-fill was applied — every region is a full
  refresh (the screener pull is a few seconds).
