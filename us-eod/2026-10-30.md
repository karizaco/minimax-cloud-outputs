# EOD US market snapshot — 2026-10-30

Full US-listed universe snapshot (OHLCV + change% + market cap + PE) for the 2026-10-30 cash session, assembled in the Cloud runtime between 16:00 and 17:05 ET.

## Headline

**4863 of 4898 US-listed symbols carry a settled 2026-10-30 close, and every one of them is the vendor's own settlement-stamped print** — each close is read from the quote service's "Closed at Oct 30, 2026 4:00 PM ET" block, never from a last-trade approximation. The 35 names with no print are dormant SPACs, preferred-share classes and small funds; their numeric cells are empty by design rather than filled with zeros.

**It was a rotation day, not a uniform sell-off.** The S&P 500 closed -1.77% while the Nasdaq 100 fell -3.47% and the equal-weight S&P 500 fell only -0.78%; technology (XLK -4.89%) was sold hard while staples (XLP +1.41%), energy (XLE +1.88%), utilities (XLU +0.72%) and health care (XLV +0.43%) were bought. Inside health care the split is stark: LLY +3.06%, MRK +2.19%, PFE +1.85% and ABT +1.60% against a **median name of -2.54%** — the sector's mega-caps rallied while the long tail was sold.

The caveat that matters is in `volume`. The cron fires at 16:00 ET, which is the *instant* of the close, and at that moment no end-of-day pipeline had settled the new session: the quote service's own `historical` endpoint was still serving the 10/29 session at 16:22 ET, and the chart feed was still replaying 10/30 in real time for most names. A chart daily bar only becomes the complete session bar once that symbol's feed has ingested through the 16:00 close, which happened at a different moment for every name. **4597 of 4773 symbols with a volume have a fully settled bar**; the remaining 176 carry the feed's cumulative count at read time and therefore understate the session — and they understate it badly, because the heaviest prints landed in the final hour (AAPL's bar read 44.2m shares at 15:54 ET and settled at 67.4m). Those 176 rows are the ones to distrust for relative-volume work.

The third caveat is structural and was fixed rather than inherited. The vendor stamps **every share class of a company with the whole company's market cap** — GOOGL and GOOG both come back at ~$3.44T, and two independent vendors agree to the dollar — so summing that column double-counts the company. 11 duplicate class rows were blanked so each company contributes exactly once. A per-class alternative (shares outstanding x close) was tested and **rejected**: the quote service's share counts are wrong for preferred-share classes (NLY-P-F implied 1.31bn shares, i.e. a $33bn "preferred"), whereas the vendor's company figure is right for every name.

## Method & caveats

- The universe is shibui `general_info WHERE country_iso='US'` = 4898 symbols (common stock, REITs, MLPs, closed-end funds, preferreds, ADRs, royalty trusts). ETFs and indices are **not** in this universe.
- `close` and `change_pct` are the vendor's own settlement-stamped values, not recomputed.
- `volume` is the chart feed's session volume. It equals the official daily volume exactly once the feed has ingested through 16:00 ET — cross-checked against the vendor's `historical` rows for the 10/29 session, where the settled chart bar and the vendor's official volume agreed to the share, and against the vendor's live cumulative counter on the 4597 settled symbols, where the two agree to within a few tenths of a percent on liquid names. The 176 rows still replaying at read time are **partial** and understate the session; the last hour of a -1.8% index day carried a disproportionate share of the volume.
- `high` / `low` are the union of the vendor's session range and the chart bar's range, so the settlement-stamped close always sits inside the range. The two feeds disagree outright on ~90 names, and those rows carry the wider of the two ranges.
- `open` is the chart feed's 09:30 print, kept only when it falls inside the session range.
- `market_cap` is the vendor's company-level market cap, with duplicate share-class rows blanked so each company is counted once. Use it as a coarse size filter; the file's sector totals are cap-aware per company but the sector averages in this report are simple averages over every listed name.
- `pe_ratio` is the quote service's trailing multiple, rescaled from its live mark to the settled close.
- No cross-listed duplication was removed: the same issuer can appear once per US listing.
- Audit log skipped (Cloud rule). No helper script under `scripts/` was executed; the assembler and the notes were re-implemented inline.
