The caveat that matters is provenance, and this session it is not the usual one. The close of record is the consolidated 16:00 ET auction print, taken from the daily-bar feed and **cross-checked against an independent quote service (CNBC/CTA), which matched exactly on 36 of 41 sampled names**. The exchange's own `/info` endpoint was NOT used for the close on this session: its `secondaryData.lastSalePrice` field served a **pre-auction print from ~15:57 ET** for every name checked (AAPL 345.1551 against the true 344.17; MSFT 537.705 against 535.93; NVDA 182.29 against 181.60) while still stamping the cell *"Closed at Nov 5, 2026 4:00 PM ET"*. That field is a settlement-stamped but stale value on this session, and it would have published a wrong close for essentially every row in the file. The Nasdaq feed is still used for the company name and the day range fallback, and its live counter still fills 777 volumes.

Two gaps are real and are left empty rather than filled: 56 symbols did not trade at all today (mostly dormant post-IPO SPACs and preferred classes that the quote services do not carry), and 7 rows have no volume from any source.

## Notes

- Every numeric field is published to 4 significant figures with trailing zeros stripped (values >= 100000 keep full precision); empty cells mean the datum was not available and are never written as `N/A`.
- `close` is the consolidated 16:00 ET auction print. The exchange's `/info` endpoint stamped its cell *"Closed at 2026-11-05 4:00 PM ET"* but carried a ~15:57 ET price, so it was not used for the close; the daily-bar close was cross-checked against an independent quote service instead.
- `volume` is the settled session volume from the daily-bar feed, where the bar's own timestamp is at or after the 16:00 ET close. 777 rows fall back to the exchange's live counter, which for these thin preferred and dormant lines carries no after-hours prints.
- The vendor's valuation table still carries its 2026-11-04 session rows (its EOD ingest lags), so each company cap is rescaled by today's close-to-prior-close ratio; a cap is written on the vendor's **carrier row only**, and sibling class rows carry none, so each company contributes exactly once to a cap-weighted aggregate.
- The vendor's cap was reconciled against two independent share counts on every row. 3810 rows keep the vendor's figure, 671 were re-stated from the quote service and 25 from the exchange screener. Where two independent sources agreed with each other but not with the vendor, the independent figure wins.
- Preferred lines carry the issuer's common-share count in every quote service, so their cap would be the company's rather than the line's; no cap is written on preferred rows.
- The session range always brackets the settled close. Where the daily bar could not bracket it, the exchange's own day range was used; rows with no bracketing range from either source are written with empty `high`/`low`.
- 56 symbols did not trade today and carry empty numeric cells; they are mostly dormant post-IPO SPACs and preferred classes the quote services do not carry. A change % with no close for this session is stale, so it is left empty too.
- Market cap is the vendor's company-level figure; do not build cap-weighted sector aggregates from this file without re-checking them against the sector ETFs.
