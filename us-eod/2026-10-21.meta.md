## Quality control

- **17,936 of 18,578** universe rows priced from Yahoo (96.5%); 642 have no Yahoo equivalent (Nagoya/Sapporo/Fukuoka listings, TSXV/CSE micro-caps, Canadian
  preferred series) and carry empty price fields. 899 of the priced
  rows had a bar from a prior session (no trade today) and were blanked,
  leaving **17,037 rows (91.7%)** carrying a 2026-10-21 price.
- **OHLC sanitizer**: 867 fields (759 `open`, 46 `high`, 62 `low`) were
  blanked across 861 rows where Yahoo's own bar contradicted itself —
  a stale `open` (often the prior close) and, for thin names, a stale
  day high/low. The bar `close` is Yahoo's live
  price and matches `regularMarketPrice` on 17,117/17,936 rows, so
  the contradictory side was dropped, never the close. No value was
  invented or back-filled.
- **Symbol repair pass**: 1,193 rows recovered with alternative Yahoo
  symbol forms (`JPM/PC`->`JPM-PC`, `NOVO_B.CO`->`NOVO-B.CO`,
  `GRF.P.MC`->`GRF-P.MC`, Frankfurt `.F` for German alternative venues,
  class-preserving Canadian forms).
- **Identity fix**: 204 Canadian preferred rows had resolved to the
  COMMON share in the first pass (TSX:RY.PR.S -> RY.TO). They were
  re-resolved with class-preserving symbols only; where Yahoo carries
  no preferred, the price is blank rather than the common share's.
- **899 rows** whose last Yahoo daily bar predates the 2026-10-21
  session (no trade yet today — mostly preferreds and micro-caps) are
  written with empty price fields instead of yesterday's price.
- **Mover spot-check**: the six largest US gainers and four losers were
  re-queried from Yahoo after the run — names match the universe rows
  and % changes agree within ~1pp (live drift between capture and
  re-check).

## Caveats

- Ticker `EXCHANGE:SYMBOL` prefixes stay in TradingView form so the
  archive is comparable with prior days; the price data behind them is
  Yahoo's. `exchange` is the TV venue code, `currency` is Yahoo's.
- `market_cap` / `pe_ratio` / `sector` are one day older than the prices
  (Yahoo quote/quoteSummary is blocked, so they are carried forward).
- No new listings can appear in this file: the universe is yesterday's
  cached pull (7-day TTL), because the screener is unreachable.
- Movers-mode forward-fill was not applied — this run is a full price
  refresh for every symbol Yahoo could resolve.
