# EOD US market snapshot — 2026-10-29

Full US-listed universe snapshot (OHLCV + change% + market cap + PE) for the 2026-10-29 cash session, assembled in the Cloud runtime at 16:0x ET.

## Headline

**4881 of 4899 US-listed symbols carry a settled 2026-10-29 close, and for every one of them the close is the vendor's own settled value** — each row's close is read from the quote service's "Closed at 2026-10-29 4:00 PM ET" block, not from a last-trade approximation. The session's 18 symbols with no print are dormant SPACs, preferred-share classes and small funds that carry a name but no live quote; their numeric cells are empty by design.

The caveat that matters is in the `open` column, and it is the one column the source that carries the close does not have. At 16:0x ET **neither of Nasdaq's own end-of-day aggregations had rolled over to this session** — the bulk screener and the per-symbol *historical* endpoint still served the prior session, and the date-pinned daily chart was still empty when the run started. So `open`, the official session volume and the session high/low were pulled from **Yahoo's chart API**, which serves the complete settled daily bar minutes after the close; the close itself stays on the vendor's settlement-stamped feed, and every `open` is gated on the two feeds agreeing on that close and on the open falling inside the session range.

## Method & caveats

- **Universe** — `shibui.general_info WHERE country_iso='US'` = 4899 symbols, the filter the skill specifies. The 12-name `research/watchlist.yaml` is not used as a universe anywhere in this workflow.
- **Close / change** — `GET https://api.nasdaq.com/api/quote/{SYM}/info?assetclass=stocks`. `secondaryData.lastSalePrice` is the closed session price, `secondaryData.percentageChange` the close-to-close move. Every one of the 4881 rows carried a 2026-10-29 timestamp — there were no stale rows in the set.
- **Class-share ticker repair** — shibui writes class shares as `BAC-P-L` / `BRK-A`; the Nasdaq quote API expects `BAC^L` / `BRK.A`. Left untranslated, 359 of the universe's symbols came back with no quote even though they are live and trading. They were re-requested under the vendor's own form and **336 were recovered**, which is the difference between a 4499-row snapshot and a 4881-row one. A strict class check rejects a fallback that would have read a Class B quote as the Class A common.
- **Open / session volume / range** — `https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/{SYM}?interval=1d&range=1d`, which serves the complete settled daily bar for the session. Verified against Yahoo's own history page (AAPL 348.00 / 351.43 / 347.30 / 348.91 / 40,889,228) and against shibui's ground truth for prior sessions (AAPL 10-28 346.92 / 347.18 / 343.52 / 343.83 / 35,714,300 — an exact match). `open` is written for 4258 rows and is **gated**: a Yahoo open is accepted only when the two feeds agree on the settled close and the open lies inside the session range, so an illiquid or split-affected bar cannot inject a wrong opening print.
- **Cross-source agreement** — the two feeds agree on the close for 4492 rows and disagree for 269; the session range is taken from the source whose range contains the settled close ({'nasdaq': 4825, 'yahoo': 56}).
- **Market cap / PE rescaling** — `shibui.valuation` and `shibui.fundamentals_derived_daily` are populated only through the prior session, and both are price-dependent, so `market_cap` is scaled by close(2026-10-29)/close(2026-10-28) and `trailing_pe` by the same factor, holding share count and trailing EPS constant. The prior close is derived from the vendor's own `netChange` rather than copied from shibui, which keeps reverse splits correct — ATYR's shibui close of 0.1284 is pre-split while the vendor's split-adjusted reference is 5.136 (= 0.1284 x 40), and rescaling against the unadjusted number would have inflated its market cap 40x. 69 rows still differ from shibui's own last session; those are the corporate actions and illiquid-name reference differences the split-adjustment corrects.
- **Rows with no print** — 18 symbols returned no settled quote from any reachable source. They are overwhelmingly dormant SPACs, preferred-share classes and small funds. A blank numeric cell means "no quote for this session", not "no data source".
- **Numeric columns are written to 4 significant figures**, per the skill. Missing values are empty cells, never "N/A", and no value is invented.
