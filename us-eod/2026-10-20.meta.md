## Known caveats

- `eu-de` has 1 row with inconsistent OHLC (`LSX:A25429`, SQD.AI Strategies
  AG) — upstream screener quirk on an illiquid instrument; left as-is
  rather than back-filled (no invented values).
- Extreme movers are dominated by sub-$100M micro-caps with near-zero
  volume (single-print ±50-300% swings). Use a market-cap floor before
  reading the movers list.
- Cross-listed issuers appear in more than one region file with different
  change_pct (known skill limitation; no cross-file dedupe).
- Blank `pe_ratio` = negative/absent earnings; blank `market_cap` = no
  market cap reported (105 US, 125 CA, ~5 elsewhere). 6 blank `change_pct`
  cells were filled from Yahoo Finance.
- No prior-day archive exists in this sandbox, so movers-mode forward-fill
  was not applied — every region is a full refresh.
