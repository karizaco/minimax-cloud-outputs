## Method & caveats

- **The session close was taken from the LATER of two price-history passes** (16:05 and 16:42 ET): the daily bar's close keeps moving after the close — the volume settles long before it — and **1531 of 4794 sampled session closes (31.9%) changed between the two passes**, the largest by 8.45%. The published close was then cross-checked against an independent reading, the 1-minute final bar (`O=H=L=C`, volume 0), which is the 16:00 ET auction print: **119/119 sampled names agreed exactly**. The quote service's `regularMarketPrice` is a LAST-TRADE quote, not the auction print, so it is not used as the close.
- **107 rows carry NO close, and that is deliberate.** The daily-bar feed publishes no session bar for them, so their price cells are empty (never zero) and they are flagged `NO_HISTORY` / `HIGH_LOW_UNAVAILABLE`; the quote service does show a live print on 107 of them, so the two sources disagree on whether a print occurred and no close is published for any of them.

- **`change_pct` is the PRICE change** against the prior session's published close (`2026-11-09`) — the archive's convention, verifiable from the two published closes. **81 rows went ex a distribution between the sessions** and are flagged `EX_DISTRIBUTION`: on those rows the price change is a mechanical artefact, not a market move, and a screen's dividend-adjusted basis shows a different number. Worst by size: `WINA` (275.4 → 270.36), `IBM` (246.1 → 244.42), `MAYS` (43.5 → 42.15), `POOL` (221.1 → 219.75), `TGT` (165.9 → 164.74), `AEP` (128.3 → 127.36), `MSM` (119.3 → 118.37), `PPG` (125.3 → 124.57), `SON` (50.24 → 49.7), `EZRA` (2.61 → 2.1).
- **CSV**: `2026-11-10.csv` — **30 columns** (the base 14 session columns plus 16 derived/provenance columns). A consumer reading by position still gets the same first 14 columns.
- **Market-cap currency**: `market_cap` is **USD**. `market_cap_usd` is published explicitly and equals it. **Conversion direction: none — no FX conversion is applied to this file**, because every row's listing currency is USD. The identity to assert downstream is `cap_usd * 1.0 == cap_local`; on the international file the same identity is `usd_cap * FX_per_USD == market_cap_local` (a MULTIPLY against a local-per-USD rate).
- **Market-cap carrier rule**: the cap belongs to the ISSUER and is resolved from the vendor's own carrier row — never a volume heuristic. 4443 rows carry their own issuer's cap; 293 rows carry it from another line of the same issuer and say which one in `cap_source_symbol`. **One issuer now appears on more than one row, so a cap-weighted aggregate MUST deduplicate on `cap_source_symbol` first.**
- **`data_quality`**: every row carries a flag from a closed vocabulary; a cell can hold several flags joined with `|`, in the fixed precedence NO_CAP > CAP_CARRIED > HIGH_LOW_UNAVAILABLE > RANGE_ADJUSTED > NO_HISTORY > NO_PRIOR_CLOSE > EX_DISTRIBUTION > SPLIT_ADJUSTED > NO_PE > NO_SECTOR > OK. `OK` appears only when nothing else applies. The cell is never empty, so a consumer can always tell 'not applicable' from 'the fetch failed'.
- **Derived columns are computed once, here**, from a single price-history pull, so two routines reading this file cannot disagree about what relative volume means. `rel_volume` = session volume / mean(volume of the 30 sessions BEFORE the session); `avg_daily_dollar_volume` = mean(close x volume) over the same 30 prior sessions; `sma_20/50/200` = mean(close) over the last 20/50/200 bars ending AT the session bar; `pct_vs_sma_50/200` = (close / sma - 1) * 100; `atr_14` = mean(true range) over the last 14 bars ending at the session bar; true range = max(h-l, |h-prev_close|, |l-prev_close|); `atr_pct` = atr_14 / close * 100; `pct_from_52w_high/low` = (close - max(high) / min(low) over the last 252 bars) / that extreme * 100; `gap_pct` = (published open - prior close) / prior close * 100.
- **A close is never published outside its own range.** 296 rows take their high/low from the vendor's day range (the daily bar did not bracket the close) and 107 rows carry empty high/low because no range bracketed the close at all.
- **A missing value is empty, never zero.** A cap that cannot be resolved is empty and flagged `NO_CAP`.
- **The index and sector-ETF moves are computed from the same daily-bar series** (split-adjusted closes - the series is NOT dividend-adjusted), so they can differ from an official cash close by a few basis points. The ETF moves are a read of the tape, not the official settlement.
- **The price history is split-adjusted**, so the moving averages, the ATR and the 52-week extremes are on the same adjusted basis as the session close.
- **81 rows are flagged `EX_DISTRIBUTION` and are EXCLUDED from the movers table and from the headline**: their price change is the mechanical effect of a distribution, not a market move. Their change % is still published in the row, and the count is here so the exclusion is visible.
- **Base columns are unchanged from the published session file**: the derive stage adds columns and the carrier rule; it does not restate a single price, volume, P/E, sector or name. The only base value that changes is `market_cap` on a row that now carries its issuer's cap (the issuer-level number) instead of no cap at all.
