The caveat that matters is provenance. The close of record is the exchange's own settlement stamp — every close in this file is stamped *"Closed at Nov 4, 2026 4:00 PM ET"* — and it was cross-checked against an independent quote service. The daily-bar feed was used for the session range, the opening print and the settled volume, and it lags per symbol; where its range could not bracket the settled close, the vendor's own day range was used instead, and the range was left empty only when neither source could bracket it.

Two gaps are real and are left empty rather than filled: 56 symbols did not trade at all today (mostly post-IPO SPACs that have gone dormant since the 11-03 session, plus a few preferred classes the quote services do not carry), and 116 rows have no settled session volume from the bar feed.

## Notes

- Every numeric field is published to 4 significant figures with trailing zeros stripped; empty cells mean the datum was not available and are never written as `N/A`.
- `close` is the exchange's settlement-stamped close. 4837 of 4895 symbols carry that stamp; the remaining rows either did not trade today or fell back to the quote service's settled print.
- `volume` is the settled session volume from the daily-bar feed, where the bar's own timestamp is at or after the 16:00 ET close. 116 rows fall back to the exchange's live counter, which for these thin preferred and dormant lines carries no after-hours prints; rows that neither source can price are left empty.
- The vendor's valuation table still carries its 2026-11-03 session rows (its EOD ingest lags), so each company cap is rescaled by today's close-to-prior-close ratio; a cap is written on the vendor's **carrier row only**, and sibling class rows carry none, so each company contributes exactly once to a cap-weighted aggregate.
- The vendor's cap was reconciled against two independent share counts on every row. 353 rows where the independent sources agreed with each other but not with the vendor were re-stated from the independent figure, 243 from the quote service and 25 from the exchange screener — the vendor's figure for a group of micro-caps is off by orders of magnitude against its own share count, so those rows are corrected against the independent sources rather than published as received.
- Preferred lines carry the issuer's common-share count in every quote service, so their cap would be the company's rather than the line's; no cap is written on preferred rows.
- The session range always brackets the settled close. Where the daily bar could not bracket it, the vendor's own day range was used; rows with no bracketing range from either source are written with empty `high`/`low`.
- Market cap is the vendor's company-level figure; do not build cap-weighted sector aggregates from this file without re-checking them against the sector ETFs.
