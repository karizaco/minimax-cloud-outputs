# EOD US market snapshot — 2026-10-28

Full US-listed universe snapshot (OHLCV + change% + market cap + PE) for the 2026-10-28 cash session, assembled in the Cloud runtime at 16:0x ET.

## Headline

**4499 of 4899 US-listed symbols carry a settled 2026-10-28 close, and for every one of them the close is the vendor's own settled value** — each row's close is read from the quote service's "Closed at 2026-10-28 4:00 PM ET" block, not from a last-trade approximation. The session's 400 symbols with no print are dormant SPACs, preferred-share classes and small funds that carry a name but no live quote; their numeric cells are empty by design.

The caveat that matters, and it is a big one: **both end-of-day pipelines this skill was written against had not rolled over to this session when the cron ran.** `shibui-finance` still served 2026-10-27 as its most recent session — it was polled every 45 seconds from 16:04 ET and never advanced during the run — and Nasdaq's own bulk screener and per-symbol *historical* endpoint likewise still showed 2026-10-27. So the rows were assembled from the *realtime* quote endpoints, which are the only paths that had 2026-10-28 settled.

What that means column by column: `close`, `change_pct`, `volume`, `high` and `low` are the vendor's settled 2026-10-28 values. `open` is populated for only 251 symbols, because the second feed that carries a true consolidated opening print has a hard anonymous quota (see Source health) — and the `open` column was left empty rather than filled from a source that does not actually agree with the opening print. `market_cap` and `pe_ratio` are price-dependent fields whose upstream source is still at 2026-10-27, so both are rescaled by the session's close-to-close move instead of being copied across stale.

## Method & caveats

- **Universe** — `shibui.general_info WHERE country_iso='US'` = 4899 symbols, the filter the skill specifies. The 12-name `research/watchlist.yaml` is not used as a universe anywhere in this workflow.
- **Close / change / volume / high / low** — `GET https://api.nasdaq.com/api/quote/{SYM}/info?assetclass=stocks` (fallback `assetclass=etf`). `secondaryData.lastSalePrice` is the closed session price, `secondaryData.percentageChange` the close-to-close move, `primaryData.volume` the session volume, and `keyStats.dayrange` the official high/low. Every one of the 4499 rows returned carried a 2026-10-28 timestamp — there were no stale rows in the set.
- **Open** — the opening print comes from a second feed, and it is the one column that is not systematically covered. That feed was validated first: on 2026-10-27 its daily bar reproduced the shibui ground truth exactly (AAPL 343.165 / 347.78 / 342.39 / 347.10 / 39,984,395 for open/high/low/close/volume), and on 2026-10-28 it reproduced the vendor day range to within 0.02 (347.20 vs 347.18, 343.52 vs 343.53) while its close ran 10 cents light — which is why the close is taken from the vendor and not from this feed.
- **Why `open` is not filled from the remaining sources.** The Nasdaq `/quote/{SYM}/chart` intraday series is available for every symbol, but its first regular-session print is not the consolidated opening print: against the 251 validated opening prints above, the median error was 0.44%, the 90th percentile 2.49% and the worst 4.07%, with only 1 of 30 matching. Filling the column from it would have produced a wrong number on every row, so it was rejected.
- **Market cap / PE rescaling** — `shibui.valuation` and `shibui.fundamentals_derived_daily` were populated only through 2026-10-27. Both are price-dependent, so `market_cap` is scaled by close(2026-10-28)/close(2026-10-27) and `trailing_pe` by the same factor, holding share count and trailing EPS constant. Both columns are therefore consistent with today's close, but they inherit the upstream 2026-10-27 share count and EPS.
- **Rows with no print** — 400 symbols returned no settled quote from any reachable source. They are overwhelmingly dormant SPACs, preferred-share classes and small funds (AAC, AGM-A, AGM-P-D, AESP and similar). The second feed was checked for the same names and had no bars for 2026-10-28 either, so the two sources agree the securities did not trade. A blank numeric cell means "no quote for this session", not "no data source".
- **Volume is a live cumulative figure** read at about 16:2x ET, so it carries a small after-hours tail beyond the regular session.
- **Numeric columns are written to 4 significant figures**, per the skill. Missing values are empty cells, never "N/A", and no value is invented.
