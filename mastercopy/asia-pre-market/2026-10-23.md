# Asia pre-market reactions — 2026-10-23 (Fri)

## TL;DR
- **All three open markets finished up, but only after violent intraday reversals.** Nikkei **-0.34%** (62,693.16, -214.68 pts, closed near the day's high after being down 1,082 pts at the morning close), KOSPI **+0.30%** (5,550.30, +16.53 pts, closed at the session high after opening -2.36% and falling to -4.12%), HSI **+0.69%** (23,093.37, +158.69 pts, reclaimed 23,000 in the afternoon). Hang Seng Tech **+2.05%** to 3,871.66.
- **Taiwan was closed all day** — Typhoon Champi (薔琵) forced TWSE + TPEx to shut the concentrated market and OTC board for Fri 23 Oct, with settlement deferred. TWSE is also shut Mon 26 Oct (substitute holiday); Taiwan resumes **Tue 27 Oct**. TW figures below are the **prior session (2026-10-22)**, labelled as such.
- **The one genuine, company-specific catalyst was SK hynix** (000660.KS **+4.42%**, ₩1,277,000): it **scrapped the US IPO review for its Solidigm NAND unit**, easing dilution/governance worries after the FSC said a dual listing fell under the split-listing guidelines. Samsung E&A (028050.KS **+7.94%**) extended its post-earnings run on the day the ₩780bn buyback window opened. Everything else tracked the macro: AI-capex financing doubts, an oil shock, and a US tech rout.
- **Macro driver: oil.** Brent settled **+3.1% at $116.72**, a four-year high (highest since June 2022), on Houthi attacks on Saudi targets and reports of possible renewed US strikes on Iran. US chip selling set the tone — **SOX -6.72%** with all 30 constituents lower, lowest since 29 Jul.
- **Widest reaction: MINIMAX-W 0100.HK +16.74%** (HK, AI apps). Worst: **Convano 6574.T -5.83%** (JP, after a second consecutive Axcel Mark share-lockup unwind) and **S-Oil 010950.KS -5.29%** (KR).
