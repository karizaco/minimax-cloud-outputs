## TL;DR

US equities suffered their worst session since the April 2025 tariff crash: the **S&P 500 fell
4.36% to 7,353.73**, the **Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.52% to 24,315.00** (confirming a technical
correction, 10%+ below its 6 Oct record close), and the Dow lost 1,833 points (-3.44%) to 51,523.06.
The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell **-12.2%** — its worst day since March 2020 — and roughly
**$3 trillion** of S&P 500 market value was erased (Nasdaq-100 alone: -6.4%, a record $2.3tn).

The catalyst is a **24-hour reversal in how the market prices one variable** — the Democratic sweep
of both chambers in the 3 November midterms. On Wednesday, "Democratic limits on data-centre
construction" was read as *good* for the hyperscalers that own scarce compute (MSFT +6.7%,
AMZN +6.1%). On Thursday the same fact was repriced as *bad for the entire AI build-out*, and the
suppliers were liquidated: NVDA -9.26%, AMD -17.26%, MU -17.46%, LRCX -16.36%, AVGO -10.43%,
TSM -10.78%.

Two caveats matter for how you read this. First, **the selloff is a political reprice, not an
earnings event** — almost none of these names reported. Second, **the tape is not capitulated**:
the equity put/call ratio closed at **0.44** (not elevated), and the after-hours session was bid,
with SPY +0.17% and the semiconductor complex recovering 2–6% off the lows. The washout may be
incomplete rather than finished.

Overnight is worse, not better: the **Nikkei is -5.77%** and the **Kospi -5.13%** after a circuit
breaker, the **Taiex -6.89%**. Hang Seng is the outlier at **+2.49%** as money rotated into
discounted Chinese internet names.

**The single most important item on the calendar is Friday 08:30 ET: the October Employment
Situation (payrolls consensus +65K vs +29K prior) — landing into a 28.18 VIX and a market that
closed below its 200-day moving average.**
