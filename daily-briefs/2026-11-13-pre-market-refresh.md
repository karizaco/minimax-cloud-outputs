Both screens were pulled with `range:[0,1000]` and `len(rows) == totalCount` held on each (312/312 gainers, 207/207 decliners), so `screen_complete: true` and the band counts are exact.
- **The regular session had not opened at the pull.** The scanner's `close` column is the last completed regular session's close, and the companion CSV's `live_close`, `live_change_pct` and `live_volume` columns are **blank, never 0**.
- **`prior_close` is DERIVED**, never taken from the screener's `close` column: `round(premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change_pct / 100), 4)`, computed from the values this CSV publishes (4 dp price, 2 dp change) so every row reproduces its own prior close.
- **Market cap**: the morning brief's reconciled cap (378 names); scanner (123 names); stockanalysis_shares x close (16 names). The cap published for a name is the RECONCILED one where the pull could corroborate it, and the scanner's own `market_cap_basic` otherwise.
- **The setup slot is empty - no name carries an anchored catalyst on the long side this morning.** The ladder takes the first rung that is satisfiable - anchored plus one of the five classes, then anchored plus any class whose gap is still holding at Hold 80 or better - and neither rung is reachable today. The screen's one anchored catalyst is **VIR** (`CORPORATE-EVENT+ANCHORED` - a $150M public offering priced at $10.00), and it is a **-15.43% gap-down at Hold 28%**, so a slot that is a LONG on a gap-up cannot take it. Every other headline name carries a catalyst with no hard number behind it - **MOD** is a reported takeover with **no deal price published**, and the earnings names publish a beat or a guidance change without a figure - so promoting one of them would be promoting a narrative gap, which is what the contract forbids. Line 1 therefore names the closest candidate as a **level, not a position**, and the watch table carries the three names the bottom line refers to.
- **The Driver is read from the morning brief's OWN sourced catalysts**, per name - `has_news = no` is `NONE`, and so is a name whose cluster carried the news but whose own catalyst was not sourced. `NONE` is a finding: 14 of the 25 headline names have no sourced catalyst.
- **The morning brief's CSV is a published-name subset, not the whole band.** It holds 505 rows (459 of them in the +/-3% band) against a band the brief counted at 459.0 names, so band-level fade and entrant counts cannot be derived from it: a name absent from the CSV was never published, which is not the same as having faded out of the band. **The comparison base used here is the 459 published band names**, and the fade count (85) and the entrant count (6) are stated against that base. The 139 live-band names the morning CSV never carried are reported as unclassified rather than counted as entrants.
- **No catalyst was searched for in this run.** The Driver column carries the catalyst the 08:30 brief already sourced and labels it with the fixed vocabulary. A name with no sourced catalyst is `NONE`.
- **4 rows carry a null value from the scanner** (OPP/PA, ECCC, GUNN, CDLR) and their cells are published **empty, never 0**: a missing average volume is not a zero volume, and a zero would make `pct_of_adv` a fabricated number.
