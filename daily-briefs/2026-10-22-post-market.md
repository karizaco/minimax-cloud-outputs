### Meta (META) — Q3 2026, reported after the close → **-6.98% to $648.85** (regular session)
**This is the single most important item on the tape.** Yesterday META closed +0.47% and rose **+6.08% after hours** on the print; today it closed **-6.98%**. A ~13-point round trip in 24 hours on the same report.

Per Reuters: Meta's results **beat** expectations, but the stock slid **7.5%** because it **posted its first cash outflow in more than a decade** due to AI-related expenditures, and **announced plans to issue non-voting shares in a two-for-one split to raise capital to spend on AI**. The headline numbers themselves (revenue $64,601M +26%, ex-charge EPS $7.40 vs a $6.54–6.85 consensus, Q4 guide $73–76B) were good; the capital structure and cash-flow message was not, and **no 2027 capex number was given** against a Street pencil of ~$186B.

- *Schwab:* **"Another worry is continued heavy spending on AI and the third straight quarter of negative free cash flow."**
- The read-through is the market's: XLK -3.29%, XLC -1.42%, and the entire AI-capex complex down double digits in places.
- **Action item:** the Class C share issuance and the 2027 capex path are now the two open variables for the entire AI trade, not just for Meta.

### Moderna (MRNA) — **-17.57% to $202.63**, -2.21% after hours
- **Partial Phase 3 INTerpath-001 data for intismeran autogene (Moderna/Merck's personalised mRNA melanoma vaccine) leaked online.** It appeared on Oncology Central and was **taken down shortly afterward**. The leaked partial data reported a **42% reduction in the risk of melanoma recurrence or death versus Keytruda alone**.
- Shares fell **as much as 25%** intraday; trading was **halted at 12:34 PM ET under a volatility-related LULD pause**; roughly $12 billion of market value erased (24/7 Wall St, measured at a -14.31% snapshot and an $84.9B cap).
- **The full data, released later on Thursday, appeared positive by Wall Street expectations**, and the formal company release described the recurrence-free survival and distant-metastasis-free survival results as positive. Full presentation is at the **ESMO 2026 Presidential Symposium this weekend in Madrid**.
- Merck -1.76%. A law firm (Frank R. Cruz) has opened a securities-fraud investigation into Moderna's disclosures.
- Also today: Moderna began a late-stage mRNA bird-flu vaccine trial, and the stock's Nasdaq-100 inclusion. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/moderna-stock-cancer-vaccine-data-leak.html), [MarketWatch via Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/20261022156/modernas-stock-tumbles-after-reported-leak-of-data-for-cancer-vaccine))
- **Watch:** Saturday's ESMO presentation is the event that resolves this. A leak-driven 17% drawdown on data that reads positive on the full release is a gap with a catalyst behind it.
