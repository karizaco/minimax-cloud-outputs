## Method & caveats

- **One pull.** The TradingView `america` scanner was pulled once at 09:23 ET (gainers + losers, 650 + 278 rows). Every table, number and the verdict above come from that one snapshot.
- **Gates** (from `cloud-prompts/skills/agentic-pre-market-brief/SKILL.md` §Step 2/2b): Gate A = pre-market volume ≥ 100,000 shares, or market cap ≥ $200M with ≥ 10,000 pre-market shares. Gate B = market cap ≥ $100M, applied to the headline tables only. Both gates reproduce the morning brief's own `liquid_screen`/`quality_screen` flags exactly (0 mismatches across 693 rows).
- **Columns.** `premarket_*` = the live pre-market session; `close` = the last completed regular session (2026-11-05). The identity `close − close/(1 + change/100) == change_abs` holds on 927 of the 928 rows (the 928th carries no prior-session move to test), so the prior-close column and the prior-session move are consistent.
- **The gap is not the price.** `PM range` is the pre-market high–low so far: a name sitting at the top of its range is holding its gap, one at the bottom has given it back. IREN prints 21.10 against a 20.42–21.66 range (mid-range), FROG prints 95.30 against 89.97–95.89 (top of range).
- **Band counts are not comparable across the two pulls** — the morning brief's gainers screen was capped at 400 rows, this pull read the band in full. Deltas are comparable; levels are not.
- **Four sub-$1 names** (ABTS, VIDA, HDRN, POLA) show a $0.005 prior-close difference between the CSV's 2-dp rounded value and the screen's raw value. It is a rounding artifact, not a data change, and it does not affect any published gap.
- **No catalysts.** This file reports moves, not reasons. A name that needs explaining belongs in the next brief.
