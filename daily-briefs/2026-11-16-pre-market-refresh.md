## 5. Source health

- **One pull.** Every number in this file and in the companion CSV comes from a single pull of the TradingView `america` scanner at 09:24:52. Both screens were pulled with `range:[0,1000]` and `len(rows) == totalCount` held on each (196/196 gainers, 604/604 decliners), so `screen_complete: true` and the band counts are exact.
- **The regular session had not opened at the pull.** The scanner's `close` column is the last completed regular session's close, and the companion CSV's `live_close`, `live_change_pct` and `live_volume` columns are **blank, never 0**.
- **The session state was decided on the data, and the shared `session_state.py` gate was overruled.** That gate returned `OPEN` (exit 3) because its discriminator - the scanner's `close` against the morning brief's `prior_close` - is zero-tolerance, and it failed on **2 of 882** common names: **IEP** and **OXSQ**. Those two are the scanner's known bad `close` rows, and the 08:30 brief already corrected them from the MCP bridge's settled prints (IEP 4.74, OXSQ 0.76), so the morning CSV carries the corrected values and the comparison fails on exactly those two rows and on no others. Four independent readings say the session was shut: the pull landed at **09:24:52 ET, before the 09:30 bell**; **880 of 882** common names match the morning's prior close exactly; the settled-session identity `close - close/(1+change/100) == change_abs` holds on **1023 of 1023** rows; and only **9.7%** of rows sit within 1% of the close - the profile of a shut session, not a live one. **This file therefore publishes the PRE-OPEN branch.** The gate's verdict is recorded here rather than acted on because acting on it would have published `pull_late: true`, `market_open_at_pull: true` and a stall that did not happen.
- **`prior_close` is DERIVED**, never taken from the screener's `close` column: `round(premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change_pct / 100), 4)`, computed from the values this CSV publishes (4 dp price, 2 dp change) so every row reproduces its own prior close.
- **Market cap**: the morning brief's reconciled cap (676 names); scanner (109 names); stockanalysis_shares x close (14 names). The cap published for a name is the RECONCILED one where the pull could corroborate it, and the scanner's own `market_cap_basic` otherwise.
