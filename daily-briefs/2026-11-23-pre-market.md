### C3 — Managed care is still paying for the CMS audit rule

**CMS revived the Medicare Advantage audit proposal on 16 Nov** as part of CRUSH (CMS-6098-P, a 545-page rule scheduled for Federal Register publication on 19 Nov). It would extrapolate risk-adjustment audit errors from sampled enrollees across entire contracts from payment year 2018, drop the fee-for-service adjuster, and reach roughly 590 audited contracts a year; CMS estimates $90.2bn of recoveries over 2027–2036 (~$9.2bn a year). Comments close 19 Jan 2027, and the government's appeal of the 2023 rule it was based on is pending at the Fifth Circuit in Humana v. Kennedy — Humana successfully vacated that rule in 2025. Sources: [actuary.info](https://actuary.info/insights/cms-crush-proposed-rule-radv-extrapolation-payment-year-2018-no-ffs-adjuster-medicare-advantage), [Modern Healthcare](https://www.modernhealthcare.com/insurance/mh-cms-medicare-advantage-audit-unitedhealth-humana/), [Morningstar/PR Newswire](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20261119ln03190/cms-proposes-medicare-advantage-audit-recoveries-reaching-back-to-2018).
