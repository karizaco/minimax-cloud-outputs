## TL;DR

Democrats took both chambers — a "blue tsunami" midterm sweep — and equities ripped: the **Dow +3.03% (+1,569 pts)**, S&P 500 **+1.58%** to 7,688.62, Nasdaq +1.44%, Russell 2000 +1.58%. **VIX collapsed 20.3% to 16.61**. But the tape was a *rotation*, not a risk-on melt-up: **XLK finished flat, SMH fell 2.75%, and energy -4.74%**, while discretionary +4.26%, financials +3.51% and staples/REITs/communications all added ~3.3–3.5%. The pressure point was policy, not earnings — CNBC reported the market repriced *limits on AI data centers* after Texas Governor Gina Hinojosa pledged an immediate pause, which crushed the data-center complex (HUT -23.2%, STRL -21.5%, CIFR -20.0%, MOD -21.4%) and dragged semis with it. Gold +3.68% and the 10-year yield down to 4.894% say the same thing: lower growth/policy risk, weaker dollar, some flight-to-quality inside an otherwise strong tape.

**Caveat that changes how to read it:** the rally is one event-driven, one-day re-rating with no volume confirmation in the index ETFs (SPY +1.65% on 61.6M shares vs a 76.9M 30-day average — i.e. a *below-average* participation day), and **VIX at 16.61 already prices the shock as resolved** before Thursday's jobless claims and Friday's payrolls. Treat the levels as an election gap that still needs a macro test, not a new trend.
