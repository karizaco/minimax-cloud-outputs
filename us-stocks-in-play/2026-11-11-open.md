## The headline

**One print owned the whole tape: CoreWeave's Q3, released after Monday's close, took the AI-infrastructure complex off the floor.** CRWV is +17.22% at 09:50 on a $3.98bn session-to-09:50 turnover and a pace of 9.41x — and the read-across is the board: Micron +7.94% on $5.87bn, SanDisk +10.03% on $3.95bn, Broadcom +5.69%, Lumentum +9.03%, Marvell +5.97%, and Nebius +13.00% alongside it. The reason CRWV worked is not the top line, it is the **funding**: revenue of $3.661bn (+168% y/y) beat its own guide, FY26 guidance went to $13.0-13.3bn, and management said it will fund the build with contract-backed financing and about $9bn of customer prepayments instead of unsecured debt. That took the AI-buildout financing fear out of the group, and SOXX is +3.94% while the Dow is -0.07%.

**The caveat that changes how this board should be read: the tape is one engine, not many.** Every chip, optical, storage, neocloud and AI-server name on this board moved for the same reason, so the 516 names that cleared the trigger are **516 expressions of one event, not 516 independent stories** — the correlation is the risk, not the breadth. The names that are NOT part of that engine are the interesting ones, and there are few: Fossil +25.71%, Gevo +42.76%, Riskified +28.12% and Satellogic +7.39% on their own earnings, against Petrobras -2.57% on a missed profit consensus and GoPro -15.75% on a delayed release.

**And the gap annex is the second headline: this tape gapped and kept going.** The screen's own numbers say the open was the trade — most gaps that opened more than 2% were still intact at 09:50, and the reversals are concentrated in the miners, where Riot gapped +6.60% and was -2.93% by 09:50. ## Method and caveats

- **Every price on this board is the 09:50 ET state, rebuilt from 5-minute bars.** The universe was pulled between 09:50 and 09:53 ET and the bars between 09:54 and 10:02 ET. A bar's timestamp is the START of its 5-minute interval, so **the price at 09:50 is the close of the bar stamped 09:45** and the session volume to 09:50 is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30, 09:35, 09:40 and 09:45. The market-context quotes are the bridge's own 09:55-10:12 ET prints, so they are **later than the 09:50 board** and read as the tape *after* the cut.
- **The prior close is reconstructed from the same 5-minute series** — the last non-null close of the 2026-11-10 session — because the `chartPreviousClose` of a multi-day range is the close *before* the range starts. The reconstruction is what the gap and the change % are measured against, and it agrees with the scanner's own gap column to a **median 0.12pp (p90 1.02pp)** across all 516 reconstructed names.
- **The pace measure is a screening measure, not a measurement.** The 0.125 elapsed-session constant is regime-dependent — the ten measured daily medians span **10.77% and 16.49%** — so a published pace of 2.0 is roughly 1.8-2.3 on a different session's regime. The 5-minute series is reconciled to the daily-bar basis by a **per-symbol factor measured on the 2026-11-10 session**; the missing volume is not perfectly proportional through the day, so a single name's pace can carry **±20%** of error. **The pace is not comparable with the pre-close slot's RVol** — the column is labelled `pace` everywhere, including in the CSV.
- **The tiering rule is stated so it can be checked:** Tier A = a material same-session company catalyst and pace ≥ 1.5x; Soft-A = a material same-session catalyst with pace below 1.5x; TECH_WATCH = no same-session catalyst and pace ≥ 1.0x, i.e. the name traded on size; Tier B = the same move on below-normal volume (pace < 1.0x); Tier C = a deal, a capital-structure event or a move that could not be sourced. **One difference from the 2026-11-10 board is worth naming: that board used a 2.0x Tier-A pace bar and this one uses the 1.5x bar the current spec sets, so Petrobras and its ADR sit in Tier A here and would have sat in Soft-A there.**
- **Screen completeness:** the universe was pulled in two unpaginated calls, each verified against its own `totalCount`. The 09:50 trigger was evaluated on the **pre-filtered subset of 9,202 symbols** — the documented union of turnover ≥ $100k, |move| ≥ 2pp and no-price legs — and **4,551 of the 9,095 symbols that returned a series printed no bar at all before 09:50**; those names did not trade in the first twenty minutes and are absent from the reconstruction. **516 names cleared the trigger and the floors, and every one of them is priced and tiered — no top-N cap, no unpriced tail.** The trigger screen is on the **09:50 reconstruction**, not on the scanner's live quote, so a name that was more than 3% at 09:50 and flat by 10:00 ET is not a hole in this board.
- **The 09:50 floors are the open slot's:** price ≥ $1, session dollar volume to 09:50 ≥ $500k. The market-cap floor is $50m soft and only 14 of the 516 published names carry a scanner market cap below it, so it is not the binding constraint this morning. **The low-float rule (under 10-20m shares outstanding) could not be evaluated at all**, because the scanner's shares-outstanding field is null on every row, so no low-float flag is published anywhere on this board.
- **Not verified:** several large moves could not be confirmed with a dated source and are published with the price, the pace and **no catalyst claim** — including **Ohmyhome +63.71%** (no news item located) and **STAK -53.00%** (no news item located; the stock halved after a +20% close on 11-10, so the cause is unsourced and it is published as a Tier C no-chase watch). Mirion Technologies -8.26% is published with no located catalyst either.
- **This is a live board, not a settled one.** Nothing here is a closing price, and the pre-close fire at 15:50 ET publishes the settled version of the same screen with `RVol` and the full-session volume floor.
