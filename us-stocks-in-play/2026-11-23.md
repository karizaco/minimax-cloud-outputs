## Method and caveats

- **Every number on this board is rendered from ONE dataset** (`2026-11-23-candidates.csv`, 2,564 rows), so the board and the CSV cannot disagree. The tables are generated from it, not hand-typed.
- **The prices are settled and the volumes are settled** — this board publishes Yahoo's post-bell daily bar, not a 15:50 snapshot. The prior pre-close board measured the pre-bell read as 17.7% low on session volume and even a 16:01 ET read as 11.4% low against the Nasdaq tape, which is why the read is taken after the bell. **The settlement ladder was measured on this session too: the second read (16:05-16:11 ET) carries a median 1.010x the volume of the 16:00 ET read, with the p90 at 1.177x and the worst name at 2.092x; 221 of 2746 names re-read at 1.20x or more.** The closing auction consolidated into the daily bar after the first read. **A reader comparing this board with any earlier board published on the same day must use the settled figures, not the first post-bell print.**
- **RVol is a soft preference at this slot, not a hard gate.** The Tier A bar is RVol ≥ 2.0; every other tier is reachable on RVol below 1. The denominator is the trailing 10 settled sessions ending 2026-11-20, computed market-wide from `polygon-io grouped_daily` (coverage complete, 10/10).
- **The tier predicates were fitted to the prior pre-close board's own labelled data**, not chosen fresh: Tier A is a company catalyst on RVol ≥ 2.0, Soft-A is a sector-complex catalyst or a company catalyst below the bar, TECH_WATCH is no catalyst on RVol ≥ 1.0, Tier B is no catalyst below RVol 1.0, and Tier C is a deal or capital-structure watch.
- **A sector or macro event is a soft catalyst and never a Tier A company catalyst.** That is why the AI complex sits in Soft-A on soft RVol while the names with their own news sit in Tier A: the complex broke around them, not because of them.
- **The gap verdicts are computed at the BELL (16:00), not at 15:50.** The open board's own gap annex scores the open-to-09:50 window; this board scores open-to-bell on the settled close, and the two verdicts are not interchangeable.
- **A gap that held into the bell is the signal at this slot; a gap that faded is not.** `held` means the opening move was still largely intact at 16:00; `reverse` means the opening move flipped sign; `faded` means it was given back; `no_move` means the open was inside the 2% noise band and there is no gap to read.
- **A Tier A bar is volume behind a company event, not a good outcome.** A near-offer or a media-reported deal is never promoted to Tier A, and a capital-structure print is a watch, not a chase.
- **`no same-session company catalyst located` is the honest default on a row where a search was run and found nothing** — it is not a claim that nothing happened, and it is set as the default in the tiering step so a re-tier cannot wipe a researched reason.
- **AVZO is not published, and its apparent -35% is a corporate-action artefact, not a move.** Avenzo Therapeutics' merger with Rallybio closed on 2026-11-20 and the shares began trading under AVZO today after a one-for-two reverse split, so the prior close is not comparable; its $0.7m of turnover also falls below the $2m publish floor. It is named here so a reader who watched the tape knows why it is absent.
- **The open board's pace column and this board's RVol column are never comparable** — different quantities on different denominators. The arbitration table above carries the open board's two pace bases beside the settled RVol; the settled number settles the volume, and the pace stays on the open board as a screen against the 0.125 constant, whose regime band spans 10.77%–16.49%.
- **Degraded sources: 2** — the 2026-11-23 market-wide session bar was not available from `polygon-io grouped_daily` during the run, and the Nasdaq official tape could not be read for a second cross-check on the settled close. Finviz is egress-blocked and was not needed for any published number, so it is not counted; the scanner's `halted` column is null and the halt state is **not measurable**, which is a property of the data rather than a source failure.
- **The halt state is not measurable this session** — the scanner's `halted` column is null on every row — so no halt count is published anywhere in this digest.
- **Source hygiene:** the Yahoo daily endpoint answered only with a browser User-Agent from this egress; the plain request is throttled. The pull was serial, not threaded.
