## Method & caveats

- **The clock incident, disclosed.** This run's window check ran against a container clock reading **2026-10-26 15:51 ET** and passed; the live feeds, the sandbox clock at execution and the marketmovers payload timestamp all place the real execution on **Oct 29 pre-open**. Rather than publish a digest of a three-day-old session as "today", this run reports **the last completed session (Oct 28) plus the overnight**, dated Oct 29. The **Oct 26 session digest is written separately as `2026-10-26.md`** — Monday was a known gap in the archive and it is now filled from the same live data pull.
- **Discovery was not re-run for Oct 29** because the Oct 29 regular session had not opened. The carry-forward book is the Oct 28 screen (461 names through the floors) plus the prior digest's Tier A list.
- **Two discovery endpoints were stale in this run and were not used for pricing.** The Yahoo **predefined screener** (`query2/v1/finance/screener/predefined/saved`) returned **Oct 26 data** when called at 05:33 ET on Oct 29, and the **Nasdaq bulk screener** (`/api/screener/stocks?download=true`) returned **Oct 27 data** at the same moment, while `api.nasdaq.com/api/marketmovers` correctly stamped "Oct 28, 4:15 PM ET" and `api.nasdaq.com/api/quote/<SYM>/info` correctly stamped "Closed at Oct 28, 4:00 PM ET". **Consequence: filter membership in any screen built from those two endpoints can be wrong even when every price in it is right.** Every quote in this digest comes from the **Yahoo chart API daily bar** (Oct 28 close, settled volume) or from **Nasdaq `/info` pre-market prints**, and every filter was applied *after* pricing from live bars. This is the single most important methodological note for the next run of this cron.
- **Nasdaq `/info` `secondaryData` is not always the close.** For GOOGL it returned **$291.69 labelled "Closed at Oct 28, 4:00 PM ET"** — that is the **after-hours** print; the official close was $324.89 (+1.34%), confirmed by the Yahoo daily bar and by the Nasdaq marketmovers payload. Where the two disagreed, the Yahoo regular-session bar was treated as authoritative and Nasdaq's `secondaryData` rejected.
- **Yahoo's `fullday*` metadata fields are the after-hours print**, not a second session — for GOOGL `fulldayPrice` 291.89 vs `regularMarketPrice` 324.89 is the -10.16% earnings gap. Pre-market prints are therefore taken from **Nasdaq `/info` `primaryData`** (timestamped, `isRealTime: true`), not from Yahoo.
- **VIX is quoted from two providers and they differ:** Yahoo's Oct 28 daily bar prints **16.91 (-3.48%)**, Cboe's delayed-quote CDN printed **16.86 (-3.77%)** per the Oct 28 digest. Both are shown; the ~5-cent difference is a provider close convention, not a data error, and I did not reconcile it beyond that.
- **Pre-market prints are thin.** The 06:45–07:00 ET prints are directional only; no pre-market dollar volume was available from these endpoints, so **no pre-market name is tiered on turnover**. Overnight tiers rest on the catalyst and on the Oct 28 session's liquidity.
- **Halt status is unverified.** The Nasdaq trade-halt RSS returns an Incapsula challenge shell from this egress (HTTP 200, ~1.4KB, no feed) and the halt API returns `data: null`. **No LULD or limit confirmation is available for any name** — including GOOGL, NOW and FTNT, where a -10% to -26% move is within a normal daily band but the absence of confirmation should be read as *unverified*, not as *no halts*.
- **MCP availability:** `tool_search` returns **zero** registered `mcp__tradingview_advanced__*` or `mcp__yahoo_finance__*` tools in this sandbox, so the skill's TradingView call budget was not applicable and no screener/news MCP corroboration was possible. **All catalysts are filing-grade (EDGAR 8-K item codes), issuer-grade, or wire-grade** (Reuters, CNBC, MarketWatch, Bloomberg via Reuters/Dow Jones, Morningstar, Investing.com, Briefing.com).
- **Unverified items are labelled.** The LITE/MRVL report is a **Bloomberg report citing unnamed sources**, relayed by Reuters and Dow Jones; neither company confirmed it and no terms exist — it is treated as a headline, not a transaction. Amazon's Q3 print has not happened yet. The "40–56%" crypto figure is a trailing statistic in a syndicated headline and is contradicted by the measured prints.
- **EDGAR verification for the overnight:** 8-K item codes confirmed for Oct 28 — GOOGL (2.02), MSFT (2.02, 8.01), NOW (2.02), FTNT (2.02), TER (2.02), ODFL (2.02), VRT (2.02), AXGN (2.02), BSX (2.02), EAT (2.02), WING (2.02, 8.01), SKHY (6-K), STLA (6-K), GMAB (6-K), ULBI (1.01 + SC TO-T/SC 14D9), RRC/EQT (1.01, 7.01, 425). **AMZN filed no 8-K**, which is why its pre-market -1.07% is treated as sympathy rather than as a post-print reaction.
