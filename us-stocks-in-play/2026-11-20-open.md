## The pace basis — what this board's pace is measured against

**`pace` and the pre-close slot's `RVol` are different quantities on different denominators, and they are never comparable.** The pace column here is a screening measure against the **0.125** opening-share constant; the ten daily medians behind that constant span **10.77%–16.49%**, so a published pace of 1.9 is really 1.8–2.3 on another session's regime. The pre-close slot of this session publishes the settled `RVol` for the same names and is the arbiter of the basis.

**The volume-basis factor is measured on THIS session, on a common window.** Putting the 09:50 numerator on the daily-bar scale needs the ratio of the two series, and that ratio has to be measured over the SAME window, so the engine fetches the 5-minute series and the daily bar for a name back to back and divides one by the other. **406 of the 465 published names are on the session's own same-window ratio** and **59 keep the archived prior-session factor** because their own same-window measurement was unusable — a glitching 5-minute bar, or a daily bar lagging the tape — which is flagged in the candidates CSV rather than smoothed over. **96 names had the archived factor repaired onto the session's own basis** (a disagreement of more than 20%, the archive's corruption signature). The archived prior-session pace is published beside the session's own in the candidates CSV so the pre-close slot can settle the basis against a settled number.

**Two run-level defects were found on this run, and both are named rather than smoothed over.**

1. **The engine's first run reused the PREVIOUS day's record cache, and was discarded.** The 09:50 pull engine resumes from the records file in its own output directory, and it keys that cache by symbol only — so a directory left over from the previous day's run was replayed as if it were today's session. The publish gate caught it immediately (the reconstructed SPY read +1.19% against a -0.44% index, and QQQ +2.29% against -1.36% — the previous day's numbers), **the gate refused to publish, the cache was wiped and the pull was re-run from scratch.** The published set comes from the clean run: the gate passed with the reconstructed index within **0.134pp on both SPY and QQQ**, and **0 of 465 published names** off the official prior close.
2. **One name was re-instated that the engine's floors had dropped: Anthropic (ANT).** It listed on the Nasdaq on 2026-11-19, so it has **no trailing-10 average volume** and the pace measure is not computable on one session of history — which dropped the loudest name of the session out of the published set. It is re-instated with the pace column left **empty (`n/m`)**, its reason stated, and its dollar turnover carried on the session's own volume factor. **The pace is not filled in with a number it does not have.**

## Method and caveats

- **The board is the 09:50 state.** The price is the close of the bar stamped 09:45; the volume to 09:50 is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30 to 09:45. The live index quote in Market context is 11:23 ET and is labelled as such; everything else is the 09:50 print.
- **The 09:50 trigger was evaluated on a pre-filtered subset.** The universe was cut before the pull as a union of three tests — turnover at or above $100k, a move or gap of 2pp or more, and no price — leaving 8,137 symbols of a 10,109-name tradeable universe. The names the filter dropped are a disclosed hole, and the trigger was never evaluated on them.
- **`pace` is a screen, not a measurement.** It is measured against the **0.125** opening-share constant, and the ten daily medians behind that constant span **10.77%–16.49%**; a pace between 1.5 and 2.0 on a high-share session is really about 1.2. **`pace` is never comparable with the pre-close slot's `RVol`** — different quantities on different denominators.
- **The pace basis is this session's own same-window ratio** (the 5-minute series and the daily bar fetched back to back), not the archived prior-session factor; 59 names whose own measurement was unusable keep the archived factor and are flagged, and the archived pace is published beside the session's own in the candidates CSV for the pre-close slot to settle.
- **One name has no pace at all and says so.** Anthropic listed yesterday, so it has no trailing-10 average volume; its pace reads `n/m` rather than a number the data cannot support, and it is published on its price, gap and turnover.
- **No reverse split and no 5-minute bar needed repairing on this session.** The split-shaped prints in the screen (LICN, NIXX, SOS, BKHA, NXAT) are all sub-$1m-turnover microcaps that the floors drop before the board can publish a corporate action as a trade, and no name's opening print exceeded 8x its own median bar volume. Both checks were run, so this is a measurement rather than an assertion.
- **The gap verdict covers twenty minutes, not the session.** `held` means the gap held for twenty minutes. The bell verdicts are the pre-close slot's job.
- **The catalyst research is scoped.** 14 names carry a researched, dated reason; the rest carry the contract's default (`no same-session company catalyst located`) plus the sector driver the name actually traded with. A row with no timestamp still carries its reason — the missing tape costs the timestamp, not the reason.
- **The ntrt gap brief was not published for today** — cron #25's 08:45 ET fire was held by the platform's Token Plan window and left no `ntrt/2026-11-20.md` in the outputs repo, so every reason on this board is this run's own research. The board does not depend on it.
- **The halt state is not measurable** — the scanner's `halted` column is null on every row, so no halt count is published anywhere on this board.
