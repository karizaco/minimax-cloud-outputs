## The pace basis — what this board's pace is measured against

**`pace` and the pre-close slot's `RVol` are different quantities on different denominators, and they are never comparable.** The pace column here is a screening measure against the **0.125** opening-share constant; the ten daily medians behind that constant span **10.77%–16.49%**, so a published pace of 1.9 is really 1.8–2.3 on another session's regime. The pre-close slot of this session publishes the settled `RVol` for the same names and is the arbiter of the basis.

**The volume-basis factor was MEASURED on this session, not carried over from an archive — and that measurement moved the board.** Putting the 09:50 numerator on the daily-bar scale needs the ratio of the two series, and that ratio has to be measured over the SAME window, so the engine fetches the 5-minute series and the daily bar for a name back to back and divides one by the other. **The archived prior-session factor — yesterday's ratio — read a 0.85 median against this session's own 0.99 (26 names measured simultaneously on 2026-11-18), a 16% error on every pace.** On 72 of the 316 published names the session's own ratio disagreed with the archived factor by more than 20% — the archive's corruption signature — and the board changed with it: the published set went from 333 names to 316, and the tiers moved from 23 Tier A / 8 Soft-A / 169 TECH_WATCH / 117 Tier B / 16 Tier C to 22 / 9 / 130 / 140 / 15. **286 published names are on the session's own same-window ratio** (median 0.9889) and **30 keep the archived factor** because their own same-window measurement was unusable — a glitching 5-minute bar, or a daily bar lagging the tape — which is flagged in the candidates CSV rather than smoothed over.

**The caveat the measurement carries:** the factor is measured on a live session, so it moves a little with the minute (this board's factors were measured at 11:45 ET, forty-five minutes after the window), and it is a session-to-date ratio rather than a settled one. The pre-close slot of this session publishes the settled `RVol` for the same names, which is how the basis is settled with a settled number instead of a live one. The archived prior-session pace is kept beside the published one in the candidates CSV so that comparison is a two-column read.

## Method and caveats

- **The board is the 09:50 state.** The price is the close of the bar stamped 09:45; the volume to 09:50 is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30 to 09:45. The live index quote in Market context is 10:12 ET and is labelled as such; everything else is the 09:50 print.
- **The 09:50 trigger was evaluated on a pre-filtered subset.** The universe was cut before the pull as a union of three tests — turnover at or above $100k, a move or gap of 2pp or more, and no price — leaving 6,543 symbols of a 10,033-name tradeable universe. The names the filter dropped are a disclosed hole, and the trigger was never evaluated on them.
- **`pace` is a screen, not a measurement.** It is measured against the **0.125** opening-share constant, and the ten daily medians behind that constant span **10.77%–16.49%**; a pace between 1.5 and 2.0 on a high-share session is really about 1.2. **`pace` is never comparable with the pre-close slot's `RVol`** — different quantities on different denominators.
- **The pace basis is this session's own same-window ratio** (the 5-minute series and the daily bar fetched back to back), not the archived prior-session factor, which read 16% lower and moved 23 names between tiers; 30 names whose own measurement was unusable keep the archived factor and are flagged, and the archived pace is published beside the live one in the candidates CSV for the pre-close slot to settle.
- **The gap verdict covers twenty minutes, not the session.** `held` means the gap held for twenty minutes. The bell verdicts are the pre-close slot's job.
- **The catalyst research is scoped.** 94 names carry a researched, dated reason; the rest carry the contract's default (`no same-session company catalyst located`) plus the sector driver the name actually traded with. A row with no timestamp still carries its reason — the missing tape costs the timestamp, not the reason.
- **The halt state is not measurable** — the scanner's `halted` column is null on every row, so no halt count is published anywhere on this board.
