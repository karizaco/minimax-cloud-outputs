**Two engine and state defects were found and corrected inside this run, and both are disclosed because they move published numbers:**

1. **The run-time engine's 09:50 cut included the bar stamped 09:50, whose close is the 09:55 price** — a five-minute look-ahead on every published move and a five-minute over-count of the volume-to-09:50 numerator. The skill spec is explicit that the price at 09:50 is the close of the bar stamped 09:45 and the session volume to 09:50 is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30, 09:35, 09:40 and 09:45. This board is built on the spec's rule. On the index itself the difference was **0.18pp on SPY and 0.25pp on QQQ** — it would have biased every mover toward the next five minutes. **The fix is landed in the repo** (`tools/open_0950_pull.py`, capability marker `OPEN_0950_PULL_V2`, with two regression tests that fail on the old cut), and the skill's fetch ladder now demands that marker, so a cached copy of the old engine can never win the fetch.

## Method and caveats

**The pace measure is a screen, not a measurement.** `pace` = (session volume to 09:50 ÷ trailing-10 full-session average volume) ÷ **0.125**, the measured opening share of a normal session. The ten daily medians behind that constant span **10.77%–16.49%**, so a pace of 1.9 is really 1.8–2.3 on another session's regime; treat it as a screen. **The pace column and the pre-close slot's RVol are different quantities on different denominators and are never comparable** — this board publishes `pace` and no RVol column. A pace between 1.5 and 2.0 on a high-share session is a name whose pace is really ~1.2, and it is Soft-A rather than Tier A.

**The published numbers are the 09:50 state, rebuilt from 5-minute bars.** The prior close is the prior session's last non-null bar close, re-based onto Yahoo's own official daily close (496 names re-based, 5 of them dropped from the published set as a result). The volume-to-09:50 numerator is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30–09:45, divided by a per-symbol volume-basis factor measured on the prior session (Yahoo's 5-minute total ÷ polygon's daily volume) so the numerator and the denominator sit on one basis; the factor's source is published per row in the CSV.

**The floors and the trigger.** Price ≥ $1, market cap ≥ $50m (soft), session $ volume to 09:50 ≥ $500,000, and at least one trigger: |move| ≥ 3% or |gap| ≥ 3%. Every survivor of the floors was priced — the board is not capped at the top N by turnover, and the priced count equals the tiered count.

**What this board is not.** The halt state is not measurable this session, so no halt count is published. Nothing here is a settled close, and the 15:50 ET fire is a different screen with a different volume floor. The prior board's figures were not re-verified — where Friday's print is still the driver it is cited from `us-stocks-in-play/2026-11-13-open.md` rather than re-researched.

**One honest gap.** The 09:50 window closed before this run finished, and the board was published roughly an hour late (the pull landed at 10:40 ET). The published numbers are the 09:50 state, but a reader should know the tape moved further after it: the index was down 1.36% (SPY) and 2.30% (QQQ) at 10:29 ET against -0.78% and -1.41% at 09:50. **A gap that held at 09:50 may not have held by the time this was read.**
