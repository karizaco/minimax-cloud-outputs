## Method and caveats

- **The trigger was evaluated on the SETTLED session, not on a live read.** The universe came from one TradingView scan (20,216 names), the trigger set was the names at |change| or |gap| of 3% or more, and every one of them was then re-priced from its own settled daily bar. There is no top-N cap on the screen and no unpriced tail inside the board.
- **Every number here is the SETTLED 2026-11-27 regular session — a 3.5-hour session.** The numerator is NOT the scanner's live volume column, which is stale against the consolidated tape, and the denominator is the trailing 10 settled sessions from the same polygon series, so the RVol on one field comes from one source.
- **The RVol denominator is a full session and today was not, so both RVol columns are published.** The raw `RVol` is the settled volume over the trailing-10 average; `RVol adj` divides it by the measured half-session share of 0.4781, so the adjusted column is the one on the same footing as the open board's pace. **The tiering uses the adjusted column.** A reader who wants the full-session bar should read the raw column and expect roughly half of it from the tier counts.
- **The gap verdict is computed AT THE BELL, not at 15:50.** The opening move is the session's own open against the official prior close, and the final point is the settled 13:00 ET close. A 15:50 read would have published the wrong verdict on every name whose last two and a half hours moved — and on a day when the semis added four points after the open, that is most of the leadership.
- **One edge in the shared verdict bands is closed, and it is disclosed here.** The helper's sign-flip test fires on a close that is a rounding error away from flat: a name that gapped -4.28% and closed at +0.00004% is not a reversal, it is a gap that was given back. A close inside **±0.10pp of flat** is therefore treated as flat for the sign test, which routes those names to `faded` where they belong.
- **Tier A = the floors + an adjusted RVol of 2.0 + a material same-session catalyst; Soft-A = a material catalyst with an adjusted RVol below 2.0; TECH_WATCH = no catalyst of its own but an adjusted RVol of 1.0 or more; Tier B = moved with no catalyst and an adjusted RVol below 1.0.** A name with no located catalyst still carries a reason — the house default is the factor that moved it, not a blank cell.
- **Tier B is selected by MOVE, never by turnover** (contract v2): the names with a move of 5% or more, biggest move first, and the CSV keeps every Tier B name so nothing is hidden. **The buyout rule won over the tape**: a name trading against a standing offer is a deal watch rather than momentum.
- **The scanner and the settled bar disagree on five OTC ADRs** (ATEYY, DIDIY, DSCSY, KXIAY, SFTBY; the largest disagreement is 2.28%). The settled bar is published, because it is the source of record for the session, and the disagreement is named here rather than silently resolved.
- **The halt state is not measurable this session** — the TradingView `halted` column is null on every row — so no halt count is published anywhere in this digest.
- **A gap that held into the bell is the signal, and a gap that faded is not.** **`pace` and `RVol` are never comparable** — the open slot's pace is measured against the 0.125 opening-share constant whose daily median spans 10.77%–16.49%, and the arbitration table above is the comparison, not a conversion.
