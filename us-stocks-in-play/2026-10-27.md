## Method & caveats

- **Universe:** NYSE/NASDAQ/AMEX common stock plus major ETFs. Excluded: warrants, rights, units, preferreds, and single-stock/leveraged ETFs (name and suffix heuristics, e.g. `PCTTW`, `GTERR`, `XBPEW`, `GPATW`, `AMPGZ` all stripped; `BA-PA`, `BFH-PA` preferreds excluded).
- **Liquidity floors applied (midday-scale, to a near-close print):** price ≥ $1; market cap ≥ $50M; session $ volume ≥ ~$2M; move ≥ ~3% (≥5% for large caps, ≥8% for smaller) to enter the screen. 770 discovery candidates → 711 passed → 535 cross-verified live.
- **RVol** = session volume ÷ 30-session average volume (Yahoo 1-year daily bars). A second figure using Yahoo's 3-month average is in the CSV.
- **Tier rules applied:** Tier A = floors + elevated RVol + material same-session catalyst (direction-agnostic — a beat-and-raise that sold off, like HUBB, still qualifies, and so does a miss that was bought, like INOD). Soft-A = high-material catalyst with *known-soft* RVol (the US slot does not treat RVol as a hard gate, but a known-soft number routes the name to Soft-A rather than A). TECH_WATCH = floors + soft-trigger move + real turnover, no same-session catalyst, RVol ~1.0–2.0×. Tier B = floors + move, weak or no catalyst. Tier C = deal-risk/arb only.
- **Buyout rule enforced:** RRC, UNF and CTAS were deliberately kept out of the momentum tiers. RRC trades *through* its deal-implied value; UNF is a deal-break print on one unconfirmed report; CTAS is the acquirer in the same story. None is a chase.
- **Cross-source conflicts, and how they were resolved:** the Nasdaq quote API returns **flat placeholder prints for a subset of NYSE-listed symbols** — XYL +0.08% (true -14.28%), XOM -0.10% (true -2.51%), WMB -0.01% (true +3.26%), VST +0.00% (true +3.44%) — and 16 symbols returned no usable row at all. In every case Yahoo chart bars were used instead, and each resolved value was confirmed against independent reporting. Across 535 rows, 77 differed from Yahoo by >0.6pp (nearly all explained by the 15-minute Yahoo delay); only 4 conflicted by >1pp, and all four were resolved in Yahoo's favour. **Never use the Nasdaq `/info` endpoint alone for NYSE names.**
- **Stale-data trap avoided:** the Nasdaq `screener/stocks?download=true` endpoint serves the **previous session's settled data** (it returned Oct 26 closes and Oct 26 percentage changes while labelled as current), and some rows in the Yahoo mover lists carried Oct 26 quotes. All session-dependent fields in this digest come from a timestamped live print, cross-checked against a second source.
- **MCP availability:** `tool_search` returns **zero** registered `mcp__tradingview_advanced__*` or `mcp__yahoo_finance__*` tools in this sandbox. The skill's TradingView budget (≤12–15 MCP calls/slot) was therefore not applicable, and no screener/news MCP corroboration was possible. Every catalyst in this digest is filing-grade (EDGAR 8-K item codes) or wire-grade (PR Newswire RSS, company releases, major wires).
- **Unverified items are labelled, not smoothed over:** JCI's Q3 numbers, FLEX's driver, GM's +5.6%, and RRC's 8-K beyond the merger agreement are explicitly marked unverified. MRNA's William Blair downgrade is cited as a pre-market note reference, not a filing.
- **Pre-market annex:** not produced — a 15:51 ET fire makes pre-market gap data stale. The gap column in the CSVs is the session open vs prior close, which is the actionable equivalent at this hour.
