**The regime break:** the market stopped selling AI *demand* and started selling AI *funding*. Two prints settled that today, and they are opposites of each other:

- **META −6.59%** on a record beat — because it answered the capex question with **equity**. A new non-voting Class C share (two-for-one split effective Jan 2027), FY capex raised to $135–145bn, FY expenses $175–179bn, **free cash flow of −$2.12bn**, operating margin 20% (from 40%), a $9.89bn legal charge and a **$25bn New Mexico privacy penalty**.
- **TTMI −20.25%** on a record Q3 and a raised FY guide — because its Q4 revenue guide missed by ~$10m while carrying a $650m Ophir Optics deal, a $1.1bn Epiq Solutions acquisition, a $400m Asia expansion and $500m of new notes.

Read TTMI's numbers, because they are the whole thesis in one print: revenue **+60% Y/Y to $1.20bn**, EPS $1.46 vs $1.25, Data Center & Networking **+115%**, book-to-bill **2.13**, record adj EBITDA $242.3m, FY26 raised, preliminary FY27 of $6.5–6.75bn revenue and $8.10–8.90 EPS — **well above consensus** — and the stock is down 20%. Demand was never the issue. Balance sheets are.

**The rotation bid went to non-AI earnings**, and the proof is the cleanest pair on the board: **PG +5.49% and TTMI −20.25% both guided the year down**. Same shape, opposite verdict. PG's guide-down is commodity and interest cost on a business with **+2% volume**; TTMI's is a quarter of demand timing on a levered build-out. The tape is not punishing guidance, it is punishing *how* the capex gets funded.

Also worth flagging: **WDC reports after the close today** (EPS est. $4.08, revenue est. $4.14bn) — the next direct test of the memory/AI-demand leg, and the first read on whether this de-rating is about funding or about demand after all.

Macro overlay: an **escalating Middle East conflict** spiked oil overnight and revived inflation/borrowing-cost fears into the open (Nasdaq futures were −1.6% pre-market). But the oil spike faded — **XLE closed −0.72%** — and the 10Y actually *fell* 9bp to 5.05%, so the rate channel never bit. What survived is the risk-reduction itself: **VIX +6.7% and BTC −3.25%**.
