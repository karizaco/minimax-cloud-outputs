## The pace basis - what this board's pace is measured against

**The `0.125` constant is measured, not exact.** The share of a normal session's volume that has printed by 09:50 was measured at a **median of 12.56%%** across 480 symbol-sessions, with the ten daily medians spanning **10.77%-16.49%%**. So a published pace of 1.9x is really 1.8x-2.3x depending on the session's regime. **The pace is a screening measure, not a measurement, and a pace number is never comparable with the pre-close slot's `RVol` - they are different quantities on different denominators.**

The pace published on every row of this board is `(session volume to 09:50 / trailing-10 full-session average) / 0.125`, where the session volume to 09:50 is the sum of the 09:30, 09:35, 09:40 and 09:45 bars and the trailing-10 average is the mean of the ten settled sessions from **2026-11-10 to 2026-11-23** (the denominator fetch returned all ten sessions, `coverage.complete = true`, with the prior session on 2026-11-23). The numerator is put on the daily bar's volume basis with a factor measured on **this** session's own same-window ratio - **545 of the 558 published names are on that `current_session_ratio` basis and 13 are on the archived prior-session fallback** - and every published row reconciles with the stated formula. The `pace_live_basis` column in the CSV carries the alternative basis for the pre-close slot to arbitrate later today.

## Method and caveats

- **The screen is the session's own 09:50 state.** The price is the close of the bar stamped 09:45 and the volume to 09:50 is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30 to 09:45; the price is five minutes old when it is published. The live tape in Market context carries its own 10:06 ET timestamp and is labelled as live.
- **The 09:50 trigger was evaluated on a pre-filtered universe.** 7,271 of 10,077 tradeable symbols were pulled, cut as a union of three tests: turnover at or above $100k (5,307), a move or gap of 2pp or more (3,697), or no price at all (1). A name outside that cut was not screened. The name-based exclusions removed 509 preferred shares and 550 leveraged or inverse products.
- **The pace is a screen, not a measurement.** The opening share of a normal session is not stable - its daily median moved between 10.77%% and 16.49%% over the ten measured sessions - so treat any pace number as a screen. `pace` and the pre-close slot's `RVol` are never comparable.
- **The gap verdict covers twenty minutes.** `held` at 09:50 means "the gap held for twenty minutes", not "held into the bell"; the bell verdicts are published by tonight's pre-close board.
- **The published prices are the 09:50 state and the reasons are as researched this morning.** Where a reason came from the ntrt gap brief it is attributed as such and carries no verified timestamp; where the board's own research found nothing, the row says so in those words rather than staying blank.
- **The halt state is not measurable this session.** The scanner's `halted` column is null on every row, so no halt count is published.
