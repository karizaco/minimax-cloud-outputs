## Method and caveats

**The pace measure.** `pace = (session volume to 09:50 / trailing-10 full-session average volume) / 0.125`. The 0.125 opening-share constant is **measured, not exact** - the ten daily medians span **10.77% - 16.49%**, so a published pace of 2.0 is really 1.8-2.3 on a different session's regime. **Treat every pace number here as a screen, not a measurement.** `pace` is NOT comparable with the pre-close slot's `RVol`: they are different quantities on different denominators, and the open board never labels a column `RVol`. The gap verdict at 09:50 covers only the first twenty minutes - `held` means the gap held for twenty minutes, not into the bell.

**The universe and the disclosed hole.** 11,133 US cash/funds/OTC names were screened; 509 preferreds and 550 leveraged or single-stock levered ETFs were dropped, leaving a 10,074-name tradeable universe. The 5-minute pull does not need every one of them: it was cut to 7,161 symbols as the **union** of three legs - turnover at or above $100k (5,227), a move or gap of 2pp or more (4,069), and symbols the scanner cannot price (1) - and **the 09:50 trigger was evaluated on that pre-filtered subset**. A name that skipped all three legs is not on this board; the reader can weigh that.

**What the floors cost.** Price at or above $1 (a soft $50m market-cap floor), session $ volume to 09:50 of at least $500,000 and the 09:50 trigger (a move or gap of 3% or more) left 1038 published names out of 7,161 pulled symbols. 29 pulled symbols were missing from the tape entirely and 25 had no print before the 09:50 cut; none of them is published and none of them is counted as a degraded source.

**The catalyst convention.** Every row of the candidates CSV carries two fields: `has_catalyst_timestamp` (yes/no) and `reason_without_timestamp`. A name with no located same-session company catalyst carries the literal default **"no same-session company catalyst located"** - and where the name traded with the AI and memory complex, that default is extended with the block-level reason and attributed to the ntrt gap brief (09:00 ET). **A blank reason is never published.**

**What could not be verified.** The halt state is not measurable this session - the scanner's `halted` column is null on every row, so no halt count appears on this board. Pre-market prints are not published as prices, only as gaps. The SpaceX-Tesla merger has no announced terms, and both the SpaceX dilution arithmetic and the Amber-Intelligence-style claims made about it are commentary, not deal terms. 55 names carry a pace computed on the archived prior-session volume factor rather than this session's own ratio, and their `vol_basis_source` column says so.
