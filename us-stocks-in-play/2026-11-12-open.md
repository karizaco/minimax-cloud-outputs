## The headline

**Yesterday's CoreWeave-led AI relief rally unwound inside twenty minutes of the open, and one event did most of the work: Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO.** Nvidia's board announced the resignation this morning, Huang left the board, CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO, Harvey Jones was named the company's first board chair, and a permanent CEO search began. NVDA is **-8.16%** on a **-14.07% gap** with **$13.4bn** of turnover to 09:50 and a **pace of 2.89** — more turnover than any other name on the board by a factor of four. The read-across is the tape: Micron -3.41% on $4.29bn, SanDisk -5.52% on $2.72bn, Super Micro -8.74%, Marvell -3.72%, Lumentum -4.81%, SK Hynix -3.67%, TSMC -2.02%, Bloom Energy -4.27%. The one exception is the tell: **Broadcom is +3.75%**, because the trade bought the names that do *not* depend on Nvidia's roadmap.

**The carry's own number one item resolved against the market, and it matters more than Nvidia.** Nebius (NBIS) beat on Q3 — revenue of $980.8m, up 571% y/y, against a ~$907m consensus, and a $0.13 loss a share against a $0.63 loss expected — and then guided FY26 revenue to the **low end** of its $3.0-3.4bn range against a ~$3.35bn FactSet consensus, pushed the $7-9bn ARR target from December to February, and raised FY26 capex to about **$27bn** from $20-25bn with FY27 capex guided to $40-45bn. NBIS is **-16.10%** on a **-22.50% gap**, $1.08bn of turnover, pace 2.63. A neocloud that beats on revenue and still falls 16% is the market repricing the *cost* of the AI build-out, not the demand for it.

**The second channel is rates, and it is running under everything.** The bond market reopened after Veterans Day into a hot-CPI-driven hawkish repricing: the 10-year is at **5.042%** (+4.4bp), the 2-year **4.888%** (+6.1bp), the 30-year 5.376%, and the Fed funds market prices an **87.3% chance of a 25bp hike** at the 9 December meeting. Gold is **-2.73% to $3,891** — below $4,000 for the second session, trading as a rates expression rather than a haven — bitcoin is **-3.54%**, and the dollar sits at a 19-month high. That is why the tape's non-AI leadership is defensive and rate-sensitive rather than cyclical: consumer discretionary +1.02%, staples flat, and the real-estate and utility complexes flat.

**The caveat that changes how this board should be read: the 09:50 screen is one engine plus a long tail of single-name events.** Of the 527 names that cleared the trigger and the floors, the AI-infrastructure block is a large share of the turnover, and yesterday's board had the same names moving the other way — correlation, not breadth, is the risk. The names that are *not* the engine are the interesting ones, and they are mostly idiosyncratic prints: **Twist Bioscience -14.50%** (Q4 revenue beat, FY26 revenue of $459.4m missed the $547.1m estimate), **Corteva -20.22%** (Q3 beat, Q4 guidance short, Brazil's crop-treatment market weak), **Enovix -22.99%** (record margin and a doubled FY27 guide, sold on the warrant distribution), **Cardinal Infrastructure -57.54%** (Q3 loss, FY26 guidance withdrawn), **YETI -6.86%** (revenue miss and a guidance cut).

**And the gap annex is the third headline: today's gaps did not hold.** The reversals are concentrated in exactly the names the previous day's board carried — the AI-infrastructure complex gapped down and stayed down, and the individual names that gapped hardest were sold into. ## Method and caveats

- **Every price on this board is the 09:50 ET state, rebuilt from 5-minute bars.** The universe was pulled at 09:52 ET and the bars between 09:54 and 10:05 ET. A bar's timestamp is the START of its 5-minute interval, so **the price at 09:50 is the close of the bar stamped 09:45** and the session volume to 09:50 is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30, 09:35, 09:40 and 09:45. The market-context quotes are the bridge's own 09:54-09:56 ET prints, so they are **later than the 09:50 board** and read as the tape *after* the cut.
- **The prior close is the official 2026-11-11 daily close** where Yahoo's daily bar was available (all 527 survivors) and the 5-minute reconstruction otherwise; the 21 names whose official close differed from the reconstruction by more than 0.5% were recomputed on the official close. The gap column agrees with the independent reconstruction in `_shared/preopen_feed.py` to a median of 0.02pp across the 12 cross-checked names.
- **The pace measure is a screening measure, not a measurement.** The 0.125 elapsed-session constant is regime-dependent — the ten measured daily medians span **10.77% and 16.49%** — so a published pace of 2.0 is roughly 1.8-2.3 on a different session's regime. The 5-minute series is reconciled to the daily-bar basis by a **per-symbol factor measured on the 2026-11-11 session** (median 0.78 across 3,898 measured symbols, with the global median used where the prior session fell outside the window), and the missing volume is not perfectly proportional through the day, so a single name's pace can carry **±20%** of error. **The pace is not comparable with the pre-close slot's RVol** — the column is labelled `pace` everywhere, including in the CSV.
- **The 09:50 floors are the open slot's:** price ≥ $1, session dollar volume to 09:50 ≥ $500k. The market-cap floor is $50m soft and is not the binding constraint this morning. **The low-float rule (under 10-20m shares outstanding) could not be evaluated at all**, because the scanner's shares-outstanding field is null on every row, so no low-float flag is published anywhere on this board.
- **Screen completeness:** the universe was pulled in one paged scan with the page-disjointness and union-equals-`totalCount` assertions passed. The 09:50 trigger was evaluated on the **pre-filtered subset of 9,086 symbols** — the documented union of turnover ≥ $100k, |move| ≥ 2pp and no-price legs — and **4,691 of the pulled symbols printed no usable bar in the first twenty minutes**; those names did not trade between 09:30 and 09:50 and are absent from the reconstruction. **1,466 names cleared the trigger and 527 cleared the floors, and every one of them is priced and tiered — no top-N cap, no unpriced tail.** The trigger screen is on the **09:50 reconstruction**, not on the scanner's live quote, so a name that was more than 3% at 09:50 and flat by 10:00 ET is not a hole in this board.
- **The tiering rule is stated so it can be checked:** Tier A = a material same-session company catalyst and pace ≥ 1.5x; Soft-A = a material same-session catalyst with pace below 1.5x; TECH_WATCH = no same-session catalyst and pace ≥ 1.0x, i.e. the name traded on size; Tier B = the same move on below-normal volume (pace < 1.0x); Tier C = a deal, a capital-structure event or a move that could not be sourced.
