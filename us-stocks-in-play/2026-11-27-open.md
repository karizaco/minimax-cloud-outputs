## Method and caveats

- **The 09:50 trigger was evaluated on a pre-filtered subset.** The universe is cut before the pull by a union of three tests - turnover at or above $100k, a move or gap of 2pp or more, and no price at all - and the trigger itself is `(|change| >= 3%) OR (|gap| >= 3%)`. All 643 published names passed the trigger. The pre-filter is a disclosed hole, never a silent one.
- **`pace` is a screen, not a measurement.** `pace = (session volume to 09:50 / the trailing-10 full-session average) / 0.125`. The 0.125 opening-share constant is measured: the ten daily medians span **10.77%-16.49%**, so a pace of 2.0 is roughly 1.8-2.3 on a different session's regime. `pace` and the pre-close slot's `RVol` are different quantities on different denominators and are never comparable.
- **The prior close is the official daily close.** Every published name is re-based on Yahoo's own daily close for the prior session and the trigger and the floors are re-applied afterwards: 573 names were re-based and 14 left the set as a result.
- **The gap verdict covers twenty minutes.** At 09:50 the later path points do not exist yet; `held` means the gap held for twenty minutes, not into the bell.
- **The denominator pull had to be re-anchored.** The first attempt asked for the trailing ten sessions before 2026-11-27, which includes Thanksgiving 2026-11-26; that session returns no bar, and because the average needs all ten sessions the whole map comes out empty. The pull was re-run anchored on 2026-11-26, which takes the ten completed sessions ending 2026-11-25, and the artifact's own `prior session` is 2026-11-25.
- **Thursday 2026-11-26 was a market holiday**, so the delta is measured against the 2026-11-25 open board across the holiday, and the overnight carry is two sessions stale.
- **This session closes at 13:00 ET.** The 15:50 pre-close fire for this date will read a settled four-hour session.
