## Method and caveats

- **The trigger was evaluated on the SETTLED session, not on a live read.** The universe came from one TradingView scan (20,425 names), the trigger set was the names at |change| or |gap| of 2% or more on the 15:52 ET pull, and every one of them was then re-priced from its own settled daily bar; the published trigger is |change| or |gap| of **3% or more** against the settled close, with the **$2m** settled session-$volume floor, the **$1** price floor and the **$50m** market-cap floor (soft, stocks and DRs only) applied on top. There is no top-N cap on the screen and no unpriced tail inside the board.
- **The gap verdict is computed AT THE BELL, not at 15:50.** The opening move is the session's own open against the official prior close from the polygon series, and the final point is the settled 16:00 regular-session close. A 15:50 read would have published the wrong verdict on every name whose last half-hour moved — and on a day that reversed 3.6 points of Nasdaq, that is most of them.
- **One edge in the shared verdict bands is closed, and it is disclosed here.** The helper's sign-flip test fires on a close that is a rounding error away from flat: a name that gapped -4.28% and closed at +0.00004% is not a reversal, it is a gap that was **given back**. A close inside **±0.10pp of flat** is therefore treated as flat for the sign test, which routes those names to `faded` where they belong. It moved 5 of the 1296 published names and no other verdict.
- **Tier A = the floors + RVol ≥ 2.0 + a material same-session company catalyst; Soft-A = a material catalyst with RVol soft or missing; TECH_WATCH = no catalyst of its own but traded on size with RVol at or above 1.0; Tier B = moved with no catalyst and below-normal volume.** A name with no catalyst but RVol ≥ 2 is published in TECH_WATCH, because Tier A requires a company catalyst. **A name with no located catalyst still carries a reason** — the house default is the factor that moved it (the AI complex, the defensive bid, the energy bid), not a blank cell.
- **Tier B is selected by MOVE, never by turnover** (contract v2): the names with |move| of 5% or more, biggest move first, and the CSV keeps every Tier B name so nothing is hidden. **The buyout rule won over the tape**: INSM trades at or near a stated deal price, so it is a Tier C deal watch rather than momentum, and the same holds for SVRE, GDRX, BNAI, PHAR and PLUG.
- **A gap that held into the bell is the signal, and a gap that faded is not.** The gap annex is computed from the settled session, so the `held` count is a settled-session count and not a 15:50 count. **`pace` and `RVol` are never comparable** — the open slot's pace is measured against the 0.125 opening-share constant whose daily median spans 10.77%–16.49%, and the arbitration table above is the comparison, not a conversion.
- **The halt state is not measurable this session** — the TradingView `halted` column is null on every row — so no halt count is published anywhere in this digest.
- **Every number here is the settled 2026-11-25 regular-session close and the settled regular-session volume.** The numerator is NOT the scanner's live volume column, which is stale against the consolidated tape, and the denominator is the trailing 10 settled sessions from the same polygon series, so the RVol on one field comes from one source. The prior close is polygon's official series; where Yahoo's own daily series disagrees by more than 0.5% the polygon value is published and the disagreement is named above.
