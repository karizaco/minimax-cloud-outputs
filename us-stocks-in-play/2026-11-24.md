## Method and caveats

- **The trigger was evaluated on the SETTLED session, not on a live read.** The universe came from one TradingView scan (20,398 names), the trigger set was the names at |change| or |gap| of 2% or more on the 15:52 ET pull, and every one of them was then re-priced from its own settled daily bar; the published trigger is |change| or |gap| of **3% or more** against the settled close, with the **$2m** settled session-$volume floor, the **$1** price floor and the **$50m** market-cap floor (soft, stocks and DRs only) applied on top. There is no top-N cap on the screen and no unpriced tail inside the board.
- **The gap verdict is computed AT THE BELL, not at 15:50.** The opening move is the session's own open against the official prior close from the polygon series, and the final point is the settled 16:00 regular-session close — the shared helper's `now` column prefers the 15:50 bar, so its verdict is recomputed against the bell. A 15:50 read would have published the wrong verdict on every name whose last half-hour moved.
- **Tier A = the floors + RVol ≥ 2.0 + a material same-session company catalyst; Soft-A = a material catalyst with RVol soft or missing; TECH_WATCH = no catalyst of its own but traded on size with RVol at or above 1.0; Tier B = moved with no catalyst and below-normal volume.** A name with no catalyst but RVol ≥ 2 is published in TECH_WATCH, because Tier A requires a company catalyst. **A name with no located catalyst still carries a reason** — the house default is the factor that moved it (the AI complex, the healthcare bid, the retail block), not a blank cell.
- **Tier B is selected by MOVE, never by turnover** (contract v2): the names with |move| of 5% or more, biggest move first, and the CSV keeps every Tier B name so nothing is hidden. **The buyout rule won over the tape**: INSM and ZS trade at or near a stated deal price, so they are Tier C deal watches rather than momentum.
- **Reverse-split artifacts are down-ranked and flagged**: Jaguar Health's 1-for-25 reverse split took effect today, so its printed move is a split artifact and it is not published as a momentum name; Jianzhi's 1-for-15 split does not take effect until 2026-11-30.
- **The halt state is not measurable this session** — the TradingView `halted` column is null on every row — so no halt count is published anywhere in this digest.
- **Every number here is the settled 2026-11-24 regular-session close and the settled regular-session volume.** The numerator is NOT the scanner's live volume column, which is stale against the consolidated tape, and the denominator is the trailing 10 settled sessions from the same polygon series, so the RVol on one field comes from one source.
