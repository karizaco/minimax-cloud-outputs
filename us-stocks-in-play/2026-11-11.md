## The headline

**CoreWeave's beat-and-raise is what moved this market, and the chip complex took it from there.** CRWV reported Q3 after Tuesday's close — revenue **$3.661bn against $3.541bn expected, +168% y/y and more than $1bn above Q2** — and raised FY26 revenue guidance to **$13.0-13.3bn from $12.4-13.2bn**, lifted adjusted operating income guidance **26% at the midpoint**, pushed FY26 capex to **$37-39bn** and said capacity is sold out through 2027 against a **$132.5bn backlog**. The stock closed **+24.71% at $60.77 on $9.39bn of turnover (RVol 2.61)** and the read-through did the rest: **SOXX +6.01% and SMH +4.82%** — the best semiconductor tape in months — **MU +11.89% on $28.54bn**, SNDK +13.69%, AMD +7.32%, ORCL +8.83%, INTC +7.46%, NVDA +2.92%. The **S&P 500 settled +1.31% at 7,619.38**, the Nasdaq Composite **+1.60% at 25,466.19**, and the **VIX collapsed 9.56% to 17.60**.

**The caveat that changes how this should be read: on this board's own arithmetic, this is a RELIEF rally and not a new leg up.** 865 names cleared the floors, but only **13 are Tier A** and **5 Soft-A**, and the volume ratio on most of the leaders is **below 1.0** — MU **0.99**, COHR **0.93**, AAOI **0.94**, AMD **1.03**, NVDA **1.28**, INTC **0.65**, ORCL **0.59**. A name that ran 12% on 0.99× its ten-day average has not been hunted; it has been bid back to roughly where it was. The Tier A bar of RVol ≥ 2.0 is met by almost nothing in the mega-cap complex, so **the tradeable end of today's board is not the chips — it is the single-stock earnings names**: **BGS +37.22%, GEVO +30.94%, CARL +26.44%, FOSL +26.68%, CAPR +26.49%, RSKD +22.29%, TONX +20.81%, AZTA +20.80%**, with the two Tier A losers **VELO -18.87%** and **SUJA -12.94%** on their own prints.

**The third thing worth saying is that the macro backdrop did not help, and it is still unrepaired.** Tuesday's hot October CPI left the rates complex repriced (the 10-year at 4.983% into Tuesday's close and roughly a 15% probability of a 50bp December hike), and the **US bond market is closed today for Veterans Day**, so there was no Treasury market in which to reprice anything — the 10-year level on this board is an indicative tick rather than a traded print, and TLT is Tuesday's settle. The rally is a sector event that landed on top of an unhealed macro overhang, and the names that bounced hardest are the names that were sold hardest (MU -30% in six sessions, NVDA -10% in two). One name shows the tension explicitly: **GEV gapped +6.02% and faded to +1.40% at the bell** — the AI-power bid did not hold, and at this slot a gap that faded is not the signal.

## Method and caveats

- **Every published price and volume is the SETTLED 2026-11-11 regular-session close and the settled regular-session volume.** The close, the previous close and the session volume are the bridge's `market-recap` post-bell regular-session print, cross-checked against the Nasdaq official quote API's post-bell `secondaryData` field on all 869 screened names — **zero disagreements above 0.6%**. The change percentage is recomputed from the **same** source's close and previous close, so no row mixes a close from one source with a percentage from another.
- **One source per field.** The `polygon-io` bar for the session being published had not consolidated upstream at run time, so the settled session volume is the bridge's, not the scanner's — and the scanner's `volume` column is never published as the numerator. The **first concurrent bridge pass priced only 509 of 869 names**; it was discarded and re-run sequentially rather than blended.
- **RVol = settled session volume ÷ the trailing-10 settled average from the same series** (the 10 sessions ending 2026-11-10, market-wide from `polygon-io grouped_daily`, 11,995 symbols with a complete denominator). The screener's own RVol column uses a different denominator and is **not** mixed in. **RVol is a soft preference at this slot**, and the RVol ≥ 2.0 Tier A bar is what pushes the mega-cap leaders — MU at 0.99, NVDA at 1.28, INTC at 0.65 — out of Tier A on a day when they were the biggest movers on the board. **That is the finding, not an omission.**
- **The gap verdicts are computed at the bell, not at 15:50.** At the pre-close slot the question is whether a gap *held into the bell*, so the annex verdicts are computed against the **16:00 close** of the reconstructed series, not the 15:50 point. The bands are the shared helper's: `|gap| < 2%` = `no_move`, a sign flip = `reverse`, the move given back = `faded`, otherwise `held`. The `Open` column is the scanner's session-open gap and the path points are the minute series' prints, each relative to the same previous close.
- **The gap path was rebuilt from a different series than the skill specifies, and the substitution is disclosed.** Yahoo's 5-minute bars returned HTTP 429 on every request and on the cookie/crumb handshake from this egress — although the same egress served 9,202 symbols to this routine's own 09:50 fire earlier today — so the path was rebuilt from the **Nasdaq official minute chart**, whose 16:00 print is the consolidated close. **61 of 865 names carry no regular-session series** from that endpoint (OTC lines and other symbols it does not carry) and are published with a gap verdict of `unknown`; no path was invented for them.
- **The halt state is not measurable this session** — the TradingView scanner's `halted` column is null on every row. No halt count is published anywhere in this digest. The `shares_outstanding` column is null too, so **no low-float flags are published** — that is a missing field, not a statement that every name is liquid.
- **The board is wide by construction and the tradeable end is thin.** 865 names cleared the floors, of which **13 are Tier A, 5 Soft-A and 3 Tier C**. The 265 TECH_WATCH and 579 Tier B names are the *beta*, not stories — they are published so the imbalance is visible, not because they are tradeable. **The counts in the tier headings are the full tier counts; the tables are capped.**
- **Every row carries its catalyst provenance.** `has_catalyst_timestamp` is `yes` where the catalyst carried a verified timestamp and `no` where it did not, and `reason_without_timestamp` carries the **reason** on every one of the 865 rows — including the explicit "no same-session company catalyst located" default on names where a search was run and found nothing, and the read-through wording on the sympathy names, so a re-tier can never wipe the reasons that were researched.
- **The tier tables are rendered from the same dataset the CSV is written from**, so a number cannot be right in one file and wrong in the other. Names whose reason could not be sourced to a company release (AVTX, ETRA, BRUN and the other small tails) are published as "no same-session company catalyst located" rather than carrying an unverified claim that they reported.
- **This board is a reconstruction of a live session, not a live board.** Every number is settled; the post-market brief owns the after-close earnings reactions, and this digest's `overnight_carry` hands the watch list forward rather than re-telling it.
