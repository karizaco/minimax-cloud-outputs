## The headline

**The tradeable end is the prints, and two of them read backwards.** Cerebras is **+11.83%** on **$2,037.4m** — the largest single-name mover on the board — after a record Q3 GAAP revenue of $210.8m and a raised full-year outlook. Nu Holdings is +7.20% on a record $1.09bn Q3 net income (+39% y/y) and Liftoff Mobile is +28.27% on a quarter that beat the high end of guidance at a 61% adjusted EBITDA margin. Arrowhead and HeartFlow are the mirror image — both beat (SHASTA-3/4 hit every endpoint; HeartFlow's loss and revenue both beat) and both are down, Arrowhead -3.79% and HeartFlow -14.95% after fading a -3.30% gap.

**The carry's calendar item resolved half-way, and the rates channel is still the live one.** October PPI came in at **+0.3% m/m against a +0.4% consensus** with core in line, but the annual rate accelerated to **6.5% — the highest in nearly four years** — and the 10-year is at **5.166%, +6.3bp**. Markets still price a 25bp hike on 8-9 December with some odds of 50bp. That is the shape of the tape: technology -1.93% and semiconductors -3.91%, while staples are +0.84%, utilities +0.35%, financials +0.52% and real estate +0.19%. Bitcoin is -3.59% at $68,808 and Hang Seng is -2.79%. Nothing is being bought as a haven — gold is +0.43% and the dollar is flat; this is a rate-sensitive narrow sell inside the AI complex, not a broad risk-off tape.

## Method and caveats

- **Every price on this board is the 09:50 ET state, rebuilt from 5-minute bars.** The universe was pulled at 09:54 ET and the bars between 09:50 and 09:55 ET. The price at 09:50 is the last non-null 5-minute close at or before the 09:50 bar, and the session volume to that cut is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30, 09:35, 09:40, 09:45 and 09:50. **The market-context quotes are the bridge's own 09:56-09:57 ET prints, so they are later than the 09:50 board and read as the tape *after* the cut.**
- **The prior close is the official 2026-11-12 daily close.** The 5-minute series' last bar close is only an approximation of the session close, so 324 of the 374 published names were re-based on Yahoo's own daily close and 7 names left the set as a result; the gate compared **all 374 published names** against the daily bar and found **0** more than 0.5% off.
- **The pace measure is a screening measure, not a measurement.** The 0.125 elapsed-session constant is regime-dependent — the ten measured daily medians span **10.77% and 16.49%** — so a published pace of 2.0 is roughly 1.8-2.3 on a different session's regime. The 5-minute series is reconciled to the daily-bar basis by a **per-symbol factor measured on the 2026-11-12 session** (median 0.8899 across the 374 published names, p10 0.7610 and p90 0.9736), and the missing volume is not perfectly proportional through the day, so a single name's pace can carry **±20%** of error. **The pace is not comparable with the pre-close slot's RVol** — the column is labelled `pace` everywhere, including in the CSV.
- **The 09:50 floors are the open slot's:** price ≥ $1, session dollar volume to 09:50 ≥ $500k, market cap ≥ $50m soft. The screen funnel is published rather than asserted: **10,022 tradeable names → 6383 pulled in the pre-filter → 5661 printed a usable bar → 401 rejected by the $1 price floor → 38 rejected for having no trailing-10 volume denominator → 2273 rejected by the $500k turnover floor → 15 rejected by the $50m market-cap floor → 2560 rejected by the 3% trigger → 374 published, and every one of them is priced and tiered, with no top-N cap and no unpriced tail.**
- **The pre-filter is a disclosed hole.** The 09:50 trigger was evaluated on the pre-filtered subset of 6383 symbols — the documented union of turnover ≥ $100k, |move| ≥ 2pp and no-price — and the remaining 3639 tradeable names were never pulled, so a name inside that hole is not on this board. **374 names cleared the trigger and the floors together.**
- **The tiering rule is stated so it can be checked:** Tier A = a material same-session company catalyst and pace ≥ 1.5x; Soft-A = a material same-session catalyst with pace below 1.5x; TECH_WATCH = no same-session catalyst and pace ≥ 1.0x, i.e. the name traded on size; Tier B = the same move on below-normal volume (pace < 1.0x); Tier C = a deal, a capital-structure event, a media-reported takeover or a move that could not be sourced.
- **The buyout and capital-structure names are deliberately demoted.** Modine (+29.97% on $384.4m) is a Bloomberg report that Samsung is in talks, with no price, no agreement and no confirmation from either side — it is a takeover-premium print, not a trade, and it sits in Tier C. Keurig Dr Pepper priced a $2.0bn equity offering at $34.50 and Vir Biotechnology a $150m follow-on at $10.00; both are capital-structure events, not momentum. **Never promote a name solely because a buyout was announced.**
- **Not verified, and published without a catalyst claim:** **Stantec (+13.93%)**, **Newsmax (+28.53%)** and **GCT Semiconductor (-17.73%)** each reported after the close on 2026-11-12 and the detailed figures were not sourced in this run; the rows carry the release, not numbers. **XLAB (+16.74%)** is a ~$150m market-cap flow event on a float that printed a 1,706% move in the prior session and no same-session catalyst was located. **The 09:50 verdict window is 20 minutes wide**, so `held` here means held for twenty minutes, and the pre-close fire owns the full-session question.
- **This is a live board, not a settled one.** Nothing here is a closing price, and the pre-close fire at 15:50 ET publishes the settled version of the same screen with `RVol` and the full-session volume floor.
