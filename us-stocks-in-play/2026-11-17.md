## The headline

**The sell was not the chips this time — it was software and healthcare, and the market bought it all day anyway.** The S&P 500 settled at **7,340.53, -1.03%**, the Nasdaq Composite at **24,328.38, -0.74%**, the Dow at **50,759.42, -1.64%** and the Russell 2000 at **2,703.05, -1.45%** — and the semiconductor complex barely moved: **SOXX +0.17%**, **SMH -0.39%**, with **Nvidia itself -0.44%**, a name that was never in play today. Two other things did the work. **The seat-priced software complex was sold to fund Anthropic's IPO** (priced Wednesday, trading from Thursday, with the financing reported at up to ~$64bn): **Salesforce -9.41%** on $2.23bn, **ServiceNow -6.55%**, **Intuit -7.17%**, **HubSpot -6.71%**, **Workday -5.80%**, **MongoDB -5.77%**, **Adobe -4.25%**. **The tradeable end is the idiosyncratic print, and it is a print on both sides.** **SELLAS +156.36%** on $2.18bn — the Phase 3 REGAL trial of galinpepimut-S met its primary overall-survival endpoint, a **41% cut in the risk of death** and median survival of **24.0 months against 14.7** — is the day's best story and the largest single-name mover on the board, on a **17.5x RVol** with the gap held into the bell. **AECOM +11.12%** on $279.8m on record margins and a fiscal 2027 guide above consensus, **Energizer +12.49%**, **Amer Sports +3.88%** on a 24% revenue quarter, **Klarna +4.77%** on a revenue beat. **The caveat that changes how this board should be read: the volume ratio says the tape was sold, not hunted, and today's screen is a narrow one.** 762 names cleared the pre-close floors — 10 Tier A and 31 Soft-A — and the largest names on the board are trading **below** their own ten-session average (TSM 1.27, CRM 1.80, UNH 1.24, MSTR 1.27, COHR 0.86, LITE 0.69). ## Method and caveats

- **Universe and floors.** One unpaginated TradingView scan of the US cash universe (20,306 rows, `totalCount` asserted on both rows and unique symbols). Leveraged/inverse funds, warrants/units and preferreds removed by rule. The trigger is `|chg| ≥ 3%` **or** `|gap| ≥ 3%` (3,236 names after the price and exclusion rules); the $50m market-cap floor removed 626; 1,069 names were priced and 296 were removed by the **$2m session-$volume floor**, leaving **762 priced and tiered** with no top-N cap. 36 names could not be priced from either source and are simply absent.
- **Prices are settled.** The published close, previous close and session volume are the 16:00 regular-session prints: Yahoo's own post-bell daily bar, cross-checked against the bridge's post-bell quotes. On the overlapping names the two agree inside 0.3% on the close. The scanner's own post-bell volume is not used for the published volume.
- **RVol is a soft preference at this slot.** The denominator is the trailing 10 settled sessions ending 2026-11-16 from the same `grouped_daily` series. The Tier A bar is RVol ≥ 2.0; RVol is not a gate anywhere else, and the CSV carries `rvol` and its basis for every row.
- **Gap verdicts are computed at the bell, not at 15:50.** The open is rebuilt from Yahoo's 5-minute series against the official prior close and the final point is the settled 16:00 close. A 15:50 read could not answer whether a gap held into the bell, which is this slot's whole claim.
- **Catalyst convention.** A **company-specific, same-session** event (earnings, a trial readout, an FDA action, a corporate action) makes a name Tier A when RVol ≥ 2.0 and Soft-A otherwise. A **sector-level** same-session event (the Anthropic IPO financing rotation, the Break Up Big Medicine Act and the CMS CRUSH proposal, the export-control and China data-centre-equipment headlines) is treated as a **soft** catalyst only — Soft-A, never Tier A. A **media-reported, unconfirmed deal is never a chase**: BILL's TPG take-private report is Tier C, not a momentum name. Every row of the CSV carries a reason; where a search found nothing the reason is the literal `no same-session company catalyst located`.
- **What could not be verified.** The halt state is not measurable (the scanner's `halted` column is null on every row). The VIX level is a 15:49 ET print, not the 16:15 settle. 36 names could not be priced from either source and are absent from the board. No 8-K sweep was run for every name, so a single-stock filing that did not also reach a wire may be missing for a name outside the tiers above.
- **This board has no open board to arbitrate.** The open slot's pace basis (the elapsed-session denominator, `pace`) is a different quantity on a different denominator from this slot's `RVol`, and the two are never compared here. The pace-arbitration comparison this slot normally publishes was **not** possible today because the 2026-11-17 open board does not exist.
- **The screens disagree where they should.** The settled close comes from the venue's own daily bar and the bridge; the opening gap comes from the 5-minute series; the scanner's `gap` column is used only where a name has no 5-minute open. Where the scanner and the settled print disagree on the change, the settled print is published.
