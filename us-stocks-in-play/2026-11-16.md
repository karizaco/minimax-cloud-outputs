## The headline

**The AI complex broke for a third week running, and this time the trigger was policy rather than a print.** The S&P 500 settled at **7,416.59, -1.92%**, the Nasdaq-linked QQQ at **661.75, -2.67%**, and the chips took the real damage: **SOXX -5.41%**, **SMH -5.11%**, **XLK -3.27%**, while Nvidia closed **-4.12%** on $48.34bn and Micron **-7.62%** on $18.65bn. Three same-session headlines did the work: the Commerce Department now requires licences for advanced chip exports to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil; Treasury Secretary Bessent called on Sunday for tighter enforcement of the China export controls and gave **Nvidia's board ten days to answer** on compliance; and a US federal judge let the **DRAM price-fixing suit against Micron, SK hynix and Samsung proceed**. The fear bid came with it (VIX 23.60, +22.3%), but this was **not** a rates shock — the 10-year yield *fell* 3.3bp to 5.121% and the dollar was flat-to-higher at 103.598. Money moved out of the AI complex, not out of the market.

**The tradeable end is the idiosyncratic print, and it is where the board's own work is.** **CG Oncology +13.95%** on $792.9m — the Phase 3 PIVOT-006 trial of cretostimogene met its primary endpoint with a 68% cut in recurrence risk (hazard ratio 0.32) — is the day's best story and the largest single-name mover on the board by turnover. **Powell Industries +5.94%** on $333.6m is bid into tonight's fiscal-Q4 print with a record $934m order book and a $2bn-plus backlog. **Telus +12.31%** on $435.9m pulled out of two Westbank Vancouver AI data-centre projects, **H World +8.91%** beat and raised, **Montauk Renewables +22.64%** got a lender term sheet. On the other side **Summit Therapeutics -16.47%** on $288.4m took an FDA complete response letter on ivonescimab and **Aclaris -8.04%** failed its Phase 2 ADMIRATION trial. The extremes were prints too: **Lexeo +12.54%** on a 32x RVol (gap faded), **Alpha Tau -10.54%** (its positive interim glioblastoma data reversed), **Ballard -10.71%** on a revenue miss, **POET -9.16%** sold on a record revenue quarter, and **Bitdeer -19.73%** with the crypto complex.

**The caveat that changes how this board should be read: the volume ratio says the tape was sold, not hunted, and the screen is enormous because the tape was broad.** 1,483 names cleared the pre-close floors — 9 Tier A and 32 Soft-A — and the largest names on the board are trading **below** their own ten-session average (NVDA 0.97, MU 0.72, AMD 0.73, SNDK 0.81, TSM 1.05, INTC 0.62). Every name with a company-specific story on this board did it on soft volume; the names that cleared the Tier A bar are the ones where a print put real volume behind the move. **The board's honest finding is that the tradeable end is the idiosyncratic print and the mega-cap complex is the beta.**

## Method and caveats

- **Universe and floors.** One unpaginated TradingView scan of the US cash universe (20,305 rows, `totalCount` asserted on both rows and unique symbols). Leveraged/inverse funds (751), warrants/units/rights (43) and preferreds (444) removed by rule. The trigger is `|chg| ≥ 3%` **or** `|gap| ≥ 3%` (7,434 names); the price ≥ $1 floor removed 3,026, the $50m market-cap floor 659; 1,938 names were priced and 433 were removed by the **$2m session-$volume floor**, leaving **1,483 priced and tiered** with no top-N cap. 32 names could not be priced by either the daily bar or the bridge (class-share and OTC lines) and are simply absent.
- **Prices are settled.** The published close, previous close and session volume are the 16:00 regular-session prints: Yahoo's own post-bell daily bar, cross-checked against the bridge's post-bell quotes. On the 260 overlapping names the two agree to the cent on the close (max 0.05% deviation) and to a median volume ratio of 1.0000. The scanner's own post-bell volume is 3-12% below the consolidated tape and is **not** used for the published volume.
- **RVol is a soft preference at this slot.** The denominator is the trailing 10 settled sessions ending 2026-11-13 from the same `grouped_daily` series. The Tier A bar is RVol ≥ 2.0; RVol is not a gate anywhere else, and the CSV carries `rvol` and its basis for every row.
- **Gap verdicts are computed at the bell, not at 15:50.** The open is rebuilt from Yahoo's 5-minute series against the official prior close and the final point is the settled 16:00 close. A 15:50 read could not answer whether a gap held into the bell, which is this slot's whole claim.
- **Catalyst convention.** A **company-specific, same-session** event (earnings, a trial readout, an FDA action, a corporate action) makes a name Tier A when RVol ≥ 2.0 and Soft-A otherwise. A **sector-level** same-session event (the export-licence rule, the Bessent ultimatum, the DRAM suit, the crypto tape) is treated as a **soft** catalyst only — Soft-A, never Tier A — and a stale print from an earlier session is cited as context but never promotes a tier. Every row of the CSV carries a reason; where a search found nothing the reason is the literal `no same-session company catalyst located`.
- **What could not be verified.** The halt state is not measurable (the scanner's `halted` column is null on every row). The VIX level is a 15:54 ET print, not the 16:15 settle. 32 names could not be priced from either source and are absent from the board. No 8-K sweep was run for every name, so a single-stock filing that did not also reach a wire may be missing for a name outside the tiers above.
- **The open board's pace measure is not this board's measure.** `pace` (the open slot, elapsed-session denominator) and `RVol` (this slot, trailing-10 full-session denominator) are different quantities on different denominators and are never comparable; the pace-arbitration table above is the one place the two appear together, and it changes nothing in the open board's published column.
- **The screens disagree where they should.** The settled close comes from the venue's own daily bar and the bridge; the opening gap comes from the 5-minute series; the scanner's `gap` column agrees with that reconstruction to a median of 0.003pp (p90 0.43pp). Where the scanner and the settled print disagree on the change, the settled print is published.
