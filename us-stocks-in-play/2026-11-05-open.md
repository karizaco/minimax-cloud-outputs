## Method and caveats

- **Every number is a 09:50 ET reconstruction, not a live print.** The conversation was resumed at 10:38 ET. Prices, session volumes, gaps and pace are rebuilt from 5-minute bars at the 09:50 cut; catalysts were verified at run time. The `chg @ 10:25 ET` column is the last 5-minute bar available when the screen was pulled — the selloff deepened (S&P ETF -1.41% → -2.18%, SMH -6.03% → -7.76%) after the snapshot.
- **The gap verdicts cover 20 minutes, not the session.** `held` = "held for 20 minutes". The pre-close fire at 15:50 ET owns the held-into-the-bell question, and its gap annex is a different screen with a different floor.
- **The pace measure is a screening measure, not a measurement.** The 0.125 opening-share constant is regime-dependent (the skill's own measurement put the daily median between 10.77% and 16.49%), and **pace is not comparable with the pre-close slot's RVol** — the column is labelled `pace` everywhere, including in the CSV. The Yahoo 5-minute series is reconciled to each symbol's own daily bar by a **per-symbol factor measured on the prior session** (median 0.893); the residual risk is that the missing 10.7% of volume is not perfectly proportional through the day, so a single name's pace can carry **±20%** of error. The rank correlation with an independent daily-basis volume measure (TradingView's live RVol) is **0.951**, which is the strongest available evidence that the *ordering* is sound.
- **Screen completeness:** the trigger sets were pulled atomically and verified against their own `totalCount` after a paginated pull silently dropped nine large names. **The 09:50 move trigger itself carries one honest hole**: a name that was more than 3% *at 09:50* but was flat by the time the scanner was pulled at ~11:00 ET, and whose pre-market gap was under 2%, and whose turnover was under 750k shares, would not have entered the candidate set — it would have to have been found by name. Everything that gapped ≥ 2% or was moving ≥ 3% at run time, or traded 750k+ shares, **is** in the set.
- **The gap column disagrees with TradingView's pre-market change by more than 2pp on 72 of 845 names** (a median 0.083pp). Those names are flagged `unverified` rather than dropped, and the digest's gap numbers are the Yahoo bar series throughout — one source per field.
- **Not verified:** the proximate cause of **AEHL +74.00%**, **ARQ +33.24%**, **ZNB +79.05%**, **FLYE +38.24%** and **ELPW +56.77%** could not be confirmed with a dated source; those names are published with the price, the float and no catalyst claim. **TSLA -8.90%** and **COHR -8.44%** carry a positive same-session print against a negative tape and are labelled that way. **BE -14.60%** has no same-session catalyst located before the 09:50 cut (the NTT DATA 900 MW announcement is timestamped 10:00 ET, after the snapshot).
- **The fuel log's `errors` count is the number of degraded sources, not the number of failed symbols.** The 136 Yahoo 404s are dead symbols that were already excluded by the universe, and are not counted as source errors.

**degraded_sources: 3** — (1) the TradingView scanner's `halted` column is null on every row, so **the halt trigger could not be evaluated** this session; (2) the shared pre-open feed's **catalyst column was unusable for every name** ("no catalyst captured at this checkpoint"), so the verdicts here are **bar-derived only**; (3) the **gap field is `unverified` for 72 of 845 names** (>2pp disagreement between the two independent pre-market sources).
