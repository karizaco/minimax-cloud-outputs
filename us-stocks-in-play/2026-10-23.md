**The headline: Bloomberg reported overnight that SpaceX is in talks to secure a minimum of 80,000 US cell sites**, in talks with Crown Castle specifically, which would solidify its path to becoming a **fourth nationwide wireless carrier** by pairing Starlink direct-to-device with ground infrastructure.

The 80,000 sites would equal **half the 158,500 purpose-built US towers** (Wireless Infrastructure Association). CCI owns ~40,000, AMT ~42,000, Vertical Bridge ~18,000. TD Cowen puts the cost of a nationwide terrestrial network at **>$80bn over years**. This lands on top of SpaceX's ~$8bn Grain Management 800MHz spectrum purchase (10/08, closing 1H27) and the FCC's approval of 15,000 more next-gen Starlink satellites for Starlink Mobile.

So the trade is clean and it is two-sided: **towers win, carriers lose.** CCI +11.1%, AMT +6.0%, SBAC +6.7% versus AT&T -4.8%, Verizon -3.0%, T-Mobile -3.3%.
