## The headline

**The jobs report bought back exactly one thing: the AI trade. Thursday's $2.3trn rout was not a fundamental reset, and the tape proved it within hours.** The **08:30 ET** October payrolls print came in at **+165,000** against a **+65,000** consensus, the prior month was revised up to **+60,000** from +29,000, unemployment fell to **4.1%** from 4.2%, and wage growth ran **+0.4% m/m**. The **Philadelphia Semiconductor Index closed +4.21%**, **SMH +3.63%**, **SOXQ +3.91%**, **QQQ +1.40%**, the **Nasdaq-100 +1.35%** and the **Nasdaq Composite +1.09%**, while the **Dow closed -0.46%** and the **S&P 500 +0.32%**. The **VIX fell 9.01% to 25.64** from 28.18 — a vol collapse from a vol shock, but not back to the 16.61 of two sessions ago.

**The caveat that changes how this should be read: this is a rotation inside a hawkish tape, not a broad risk-on session, and the mechanism that caused the rout is still standing.** The **10-year rose to 4.859%** (from 4.837%), the **2-year is up about 6bp to 4.537%**, and December rate-hike odds moved to **~78% from ~70%** — Oxford Economics now expects a **hike in December and again in March**. That is why the defensives are the other side of the trade: **XLP -1.28%, XLF -0.59%, XLB -0.73%, XLRE -0.68%, XLU -0.39%, XLV -0.06%** while the Nasdaq-100 is **+1.35%**. Gold fell **-0.79%** and the dollar firmed — the classic hawkish-macro signature. The second caveat: **the rebound is a bounce, not a repair.** The mechanism that caused Thursday's rout is a **post, not a bill** — Ansari's day-1 AI-pause language was never legislated — and the names that moved hardest today are the ones with a **story of their own**: **FROG +35.33%** (Q3 blowout, AI coding the biggest growth driver), **KRMN +42.29%** (Q3 record revenue plus a Mars-mission parachute contract), **GOGO +71.52%** (the FCC renews its exclusive air-to-ground spectrum licences through 2036), **FLY +36.80%** (a $1.3bn AST SpaceMobile engine deal), **IREN +20.02%**, **NET +9.89%**, **ABNB +11.67%** — and against them **IRTC -30.70%**, **DKNG -21.30%**, **AXON -18.18%**, **NTRA -14.59%**, **MCHP -10.02%**, where the print *was* the story and the print was bad.

**The third thing worth saying: the gaps that held into the bell were overwhelmingly the earnings gaps, on both sides.** **NET +17.37% → +9.89%**, **IREN +17.30% → +20.02%**, **ABNB +12.35% → +11.67%**, **FROG +27.30% → +35.33%**, **COHR +11.66% → +13.61%**, **AAOI +4.53% → +6.91%** held and extended on the long side. On the short side **DKNG -26.28% → -21.30%** and **NTRA -12.03% → -14.59%** held the gap down and extended it. But the **complex itself faded**: **SNDK +11.39% → +3.55%** and **WDC +7.24% → -1.44%** and **STX +8.36% → -1.19%** and **AVGO +6.72% → +0.74%** all gave the opening move back, and **STX and WDC outright reversed**. **A name whose gap faded is not a momentum name at this slot** — the opening buyer is gone. That is why the biggest dollar-volume names on this board sit in TECH_WATCH and Tier B rather than Tier A: **NVDA +2.78%**, **MU +5.12%**, **AMD +11.04%** on **$33.2bn**, **$30.7bn** and **$20.3bn** of turnover, but all three are the beta, not a story.

## Method and caveats

- **Prices are the official 2026-11-06 regular-session close** from Nasdaq `/info` `secondaryData`, stamped *"Closed at Nov 6, 2026 4:00 PM ET"*. **Volumes are the settled session volume** from the Yahoo daily bar, re-pulled at 16:34 ET after the closing auction consolidated.
- **RVol is the settled session volume ÷ the trailing-10 settled average from the same series** (the 10 sessions ending 2026-11-05). **A screener's own RVol column uses a different denominator and is not comparable across sources**, and it is never mixed into a published row.
- **The TradingView scanner is ~15 minutes delayed and its post-bell values are after-hours contaminated.** The scanner is therefore used here for **discovery, the gap columns and the universe only** — never for the settled print.
- **Screen completeness:** the trigger screen is `|chg| ≥ 3% OR |gap| ≥ 3%` on the **settled** move over 6,094 priced US cash equities. Every survivor of the floors was priced (**1,254 tiered**). There is no unpriced tail and no top-N cap on the screen; the **caps apply only to what is published in the tables** (Tier A and Soft-A up to 25, TECH_WATCH up to 30, Tier B and Tier C up to 25) — **the full tier count is stated in every heading**.
- **The gap annex covers 140 names, not all 1,254.** The path was rebuilt for every Tier A / Soft-A / Tier C name, every name with a gap ≥ 5% and every name with a move ≥ 8%, plus every TECH_WATCH name. A row outside that set carries `gap_verdict = n/a` rather than an invented verdict.
- **Catalyst sourcing:** every catalyst line was read from a dated item in **this session** (the Yahoo news API with `providerPublishTime`, a company release, an 8-K or `web_search`). **A catalyst with no verified timestamp still carries its reason** — the `has_catalyst_timestamp` field is `no` for the names that moved on the beta, and the reason says so. Where a name moved without a catalyst that could be sourced, it is published in Tier B or TECH_WATCH with *"no same-session company catalyst verified"* rather than being given a plausible story.
- **Not verified:** the proximate cause of **CIIT's +57.89%** (a $99m turnover name with a +60.00% pre-market gap), **VSA's +52.60%**, **CDT's +54.85%**, **SNT's +46.15%**, **STLN's -45.97%**, **PRAA's -36.73%** and **BDSX's -57.01%** could not be established with a dated source in this run. Those names are published with the price and no catalyst claim, and they sit in Tier A **only** on their RVol and liquidity — not on a story. **GOGO's +71.52%** is in Tier C rather than Tier A because the FCC licence renewal is a structural, multi-year grant rather than a same-session tradeable event.
- **degraded_sources: 1** — `polygon-io grouped_daily` had not published the **2026-11-06** bar at run time (upstream answered `403 / outside the plan`), so the **numerator** came from the Yahoo daily bar and the Nasdaq `/info` close instead. The **denominator** is unaffected because it excludes today by construction.
- **The halt state is not measurable this session** — the TradingView scanner's `halted` column is null on every row, so no halt count is published anywhere in this digest.
- **Split artifacts:** none today. **SOXX closed +3.51%** and is comparable with Thursday's -70.72% print only because the forward split has been in the price for two sessions; the cleaner semis gauges are **SMH +3.63%** and **SOXQ +3.91%**.
