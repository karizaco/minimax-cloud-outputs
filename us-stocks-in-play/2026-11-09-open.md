## The headline

**Nvidia's CEO is the trade.** Over the weekend Nvidia's board opened an **independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang** after he admitted to an affair with a woman US officials believe was linked to Chinese intelligence; Huang remains CEO and the board says it has no reason to believe company secrets were compromised. At **09:50 ET** that is the single largest dislocation on the board: **NVDA -4.93%** on **$25.0bn** of turnover in twenty minutes (**pace 8.29**) — and, crucially, **every rival chip name was bid**. **AMD +8.83%** on **$5.05bn**, **SNDK +5.92%**, **MU +4.20%**, **AVGO +6.54%**, **AMAT +8.25%**, **MRVL +7.27%**, **BE +11.34%**, **LITE +10.91%**, and the SOXX complex **+4.41%**. The market treated the probe as a **single-stock problem, not a demand problem**: Asia's chipmakers rallied anyway (Nikkei **+2.33%**, Taiwan +5.6% in Taipei) and the US tape is up across the board — **Nasdaq Composite +2.31%**, **S&P 500 +1.83%**, **Nasdaq-100 +2.67%**, **Dow +1.49%**, **Russell 2000 +1.95%**, with the **VIX down 16.2% to 21.48** off a 25.64 print.

The caveat that changes how this should be read: **this is a relief bid, not a repair.** Last week ended with the S&P down 2.7% — its worst week since June — after Thursday's post-midterm rout (Nasdaq Composite **-5.52%**, SOX **-12.25%**). Today's tape is the market buying that panic back, and it is buying it in a tape with **October CPI due tomorrow morning** and a stack of AI earnings this week (CSCO and AMAT Thursday, CRWV Tuesday). **Two other things are moving the board and they are not chip stories**: the **oil complex is broken** (WTI **-3.93% to $87.26**, USO -3.05%, and FTSE/Dax both up on the inflation relief) while **gold is bid** (**+1.35% to $4,129.90**) and the **miners gapped with it** (NEM +4.21% on a +4.49% gap, FCX +4.98% on a +4.02% gap, GDX +3.66%); and the **biotech tape is split** — **VIR +14.99%** on a Phase 3 hepatitis-delta win while **ZURA -73.47%** on a failed Phase 2, **IONS -27.60%** and **IBRX -24.03%** on Q3 prints.

The third thing a reader should know: **the tape is already past 09:50.** These are the 09:50 prices, and the market has kept going — the Nasdaq was **+2.31%** at 10:12 ET and **SOXX +5.70%** versus **+4.41%** at 09:50. ## Method and caveats

- **Every price is the 09:50 ET state, rebuilt from 5-minute bars.** The universe was pulled between 09:52 and 10:04 ET. Bar timestamps are the START of each 5-minute interval, so the **price at 09:50 is the close of the bar stamped 09:45** and the **session volume to 09:50 is the sum of the bars stamped 09:30, 09:35, 09:40 and 09:45**. The market-context quotes are the bridge's own 09:53–10:12 ET prints, so they are **later than the 09:50 board** and read as the tape *after* the cut.
- **The gap verdicts cover 20 minutes, not the session.** `held` = "held for 20 minutes". The pre-close fire at 15:50 ET owns the held-into-the-bell question, and its gap annex is a different screen with a different floor.
- **The pace measure is a screening measure, not a measurement.** The 0.125 opening-share constant is regime-dependent — the measured daily median spans **10.77% and 16.49%** — so a published pace of 2.0 is roughly 1.8–2.3 on a different session's regime. The 5-minute series is reconciled to each symbol's own daily bar by a **per-symbol factor measured on the prior session** (median **0.9168**); the residual risk is that the missing ~8% of volume is not perfectly proportional through the day, so a single name's pace can carry **±20%** of error. **The pace is not comparable with the pre-close slot's RVol** — the column is labelled `pace` everywhere, including in the CSV.
- **Screen completeness:** the universe was pulled in **one unpaginated call per instrument type** and each call was verified against its own `totalCount`; a paginated pull of the same universe silently returns duplicate rows and was not used. Every one of the **767** floor-cleared names is priced and tiered — **no top-N cap, no unpriced tail**. The trigger screen is on the **09:50 reconstruction**, not on the scanner's live quote, so a name that was more than 3% at 09:50 and flat by 10:04 ET is **not** a hole in this board.
- **One material mover is excluded by a floor, not by a source failure: EDSA** (Edesa Biotech, the Kyorin Japan licence, +52.8% at 09:50) fails the **$50m market-cap soft floor** at $42.4m, so it is not on the board. It is the single largest mover of the day and a reader should know it exists.
- **Not verified:** the proximate cause of several large moves could not be confirmed with a dated source and they are published with the price, the pace and **no catalyst claim** — among them **GFR +38.39%** (pace 28.13), **NWGL +36.64%** (pace 160.16), **MF +62.67%** and **ARWR -14.63%**. **No low-float flags are published anywhere on this board** because the scanner's shares-outstanding column is null for every row — the low-float rule could not be evaluated, not because every name is liquid.
- **The fuel log's `errors` count is the number of degraded sources, not the number of failed symbols.** The 62 symbols that returned HTTP 404 are dead or unlisted names.
- **This is a live board, not a settled one.** Nothing here is a closing price, and the pre-close fire at 15:50 ET publishes the settled version of the same screen with `RVol` and the full-session volume floor.
