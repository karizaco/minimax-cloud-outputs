## The headline

**The market bought the panic back and left Nvidia behind.** Monday was the relief bid after last week's AI-led rout, and it was a broad one: the **S&P 500 closed +2.39% at 7,553.33**, the **Nasdaq Composite +2.81% at 25,269.10**, the **Nasdaq-100 +3.47% at 27,927.31**, the **Dow +2.19% at 52,408.55** and the **Russell 2000 +3.42% at 2,848.32**, with the **VIX down 22.31% to 19.92** from 25.64. Wall Street added roughly **$1trn** in market value on the day. The engine was **oil**: WTI fell to **$86.88, -4.35%**, after President Trump said fresh Iran strikes are *"highly unlikely"*, and lower crude pulled the rate-sensitive complex with it — the airlines (**UAL, DAL, JBLU, AAL**) were up 3.6–3.9% on the crude move alone.

**The caveat that changes how this should be read: the single-stock story was Nvidia, and the market treated it as exactly that.** Over the weekend Nvidia's board opened an **independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang** after reports that he was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. **NVDA closed -4.27% on $79.7bn of turnover** (RVol 3.28) — and it is the only major chip name that fell. **Every rival was bid**: **AMD +13.26%** on $25.1bn (Oppenheimer upgraded it to Outperform with a $600 target, and Google Cloud named AMD processors for its processing-unit infrastructure), **AMAT +11.37%**, **COHR +16.75%**, **ALAB +13.72%**, **AVGO +8.78%**, **ASML +6.21%**, **SNDK +6.16%**, **MU +6.25%**, **STX +9.79%** — with **SOXX +7.53%** and **SMH +5.07%**. On the day the **Bank of England's Governor Andrew Bailey** warned in Zurich that an **AI asset-price bubble poses clear financial-stability risks** and that AI returns *"take longer to be realised than predicted"*; Morgan Stanley's Mike Wilson called the correction's *"final innings"* with a **7,100–7,300** S&P bottom. **A $1trn day that opens with a central-bank warning about the bubble it is pricing is the fact to carry into tomorrow's CPI.**

**The third thing worth saying is that this was not only a chip tape.** The **AI-power complex was repriced by Constellation's guidance raise** — CEG beat (adjusted operating earnings **$5.06** against roughly **$3.96** expected) and lifted FY26 guidance to **$12.50–12.80** from $11.50–12.50, with about **8,500MW** of long-term agreements signed this year — which took **CEG +10.46%** on $1.6bn, **BE +11.97%** on $3.0bn, **GEV +7.11%** on $3.1bn and **OKLO +13.97%** with it. **The biotech tape split down the middle**: **VIR +11.75%** on a Phase 3 ECLIPSE 1 win, **IONS -21.36%** on a Q3 beat buried by a **TRYNGOLZA guidance cut to about $50m from $100–110m**, **ZURA -69.68%** on a Phase 2 miss, **FTH -42.49%** on a Phase 2 response rate the market did not believe, **IBRX -17.66%** on a Q3 print, and **ABEO -39.09%** on a $30m private placement. And the **deal names are not momentum**: **GFR +38.30%** on Strathcona's cash-and-stock bid is a spread, and **MRVL +9.49%** and **LITE +10.69%** trade as one arb around the **$115bn** all-stock Lumentum merger. **The tradeable end of the board is therefore small and company-specific** — **TLS +48.76%**, **DCTH +35.77%**, **FRVO +26.09%**, **QXO +19.29%**, **CECO +19.06%**, **WRBY +18.52%**, **FERG +11.22%** — while the mega-cap moves are mostly **TECH_WATCH or Tier B**, because they are the beta rather than a story.

## Method and caveats

- **Prices are the official 2026-11-09 regular-session close** from the Nasdaq quote API (`secondaryData`, stamped *"Closed at Nov 9, 2026 4:00 PM ET"*), and **volumes are the settled session volume** from the bridge's `market-recap` quotes. **The two sources agree on the close** across the 702 names priced by both — a median absolute difference of **0.0000%**, p90 **0.047%** — so the published close is the official one. **RVol is the settled session volume ÷ the trailing-10 settled average from the same series** (the 10 sessions ending 2026-11-06). **A screener's own RVol column uses a different denominator and is not comparable across sources**, and it is never mixed into a published row.
- **Screen completeness:** the trigger screen is `|chg| ≥ 3% OR |gap| ≥ 3%` on the **settled** move over the US cash equity universe. **Every survivor of the floors was priced (1501 tiered).** There is no unpriced tail and no top-N cap on the screen; the **caps apply only to what is published in the tables** (Tier A and Soft-A up to 25, TECH_WATCH up to 30, Tier B and Tier C up to 25) — **the full tier count is stated in every heading**.
- **The gap annex covers 510 names, not all 1501.** The path was rebuilt for every name with a gap ≥ 5% or a move ≥ 8%, plus the 120 largest by turnover. A row outside that set carries `gap_verdict = n/a` rather than an invented verdict.
