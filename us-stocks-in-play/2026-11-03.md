## The headline

**This was a rotation with a vengeance, not a rebound — and the index levels hide almost all of it.** The S&P 500 closed **+0.31%**, the Nasdaq-100 **+0.62%**, the Dow **-0.45%** and the Russell 2000 **+0.24%**, and none of that is the story. The story is that **semiconductors and AI infrastructure ripped while software and Monday's defensive winners were sold back**: **SOXX +2.50%**, **XSD +3.40%**, **AMD +3.65%** into its own print, **MU +2.58% on $16.6bn**, **SNDK +4.78%**, **AVGO +3.07%**, **CRWV +7.16%**, **NBIS +7.25%**, **IREN +11.08%**, **CIFR +18.37%**, **RIOT +18.41%** — against **SHOP -8.74%** on its first revenue miss in four years, **PLTR -1.65%** on a bookings deceleration, **DT -14.58%**, **IT -13.29%**, **DASH -3.55%**, and a defensive unwind of **XLP -2.06%, XLB -2.05%, XLY -0.69%**.

The caveat that changes how this should be read: **the cross-section was one-directional and leadership is now very narrow.** Every name that mattered on the board carried *company* information, but the information pointed in two opposite directions — semis and AI power got bid, software and consumer staples got sold — and the macro tape was quiet, which is exactly what lets a rotation run this cleanly. **It was also Election Day**: results come tonight, AMD reports after the bell, and the volatility a contested result brings sits on top of a tape whose entire bid is sitting in one theme.

Two things are genuinely new information rather than a continuation of Monday:

1. **Shopify broke a four-year streak.** Q3 revenue **$3.742bn vs ~$3.75bn est** — an $8mn miss, but the *first* top-line miss since 2022 — with **GMV $119.15bn vs $120.03bn**, **subscription revenue $836M vs $848.1M** and **subscription gross profit $661M vs $680.3M**. Adjusted EPS beat at **$0.49 vs $0.46** and FCF rose 61% to **$816M**. **Gapped -12.8% and never recovered**, on $3.5bn. At ~99x earnings there was no cushion.
2. **The AI-hardware bid is a positioning event, not an earnings event.** **AMD +3.65% ahead of its own print** (consensus EPS $1.97 on $13.0bn revenue, Data Center seen at $8.19bn, +88% y/y), **MU +2.58%** with no company news after Monday's -5.78%, **SNDK +4.78%** ahead of its 11-20 8-for-1 split, and the whole neocloud/miner complex (**CRWV, NBIS, IREN, CIFR, RIOT, CLSK, CORZ, APLD, HUT, WULF**) bid together after KBW warned on Monday of a "drought in lease catalysts". **Relative volume on MU is 0.76x and on CRWV 0.54x — this is a short-covering bounce in a broken complex, not accumulation.**

## Method and caveats

- **Two-phase pre-close procedure, as the skill requires.** Phase 1 ran live 15:50–16:00 ET. Phase 2 ran after the bell: the scanner universe was re-pulled at **16:04 ET** and every tiered name was re-priced from the **settled** daily bar. **The numbers published here are settled.** The gap annex's `15:50 %` column is the helper's intraday print, not the close, and is labelled as such.
- **Screens and floors.** Price ≥ $1; market cap ≥ $50M (soft); session $ volume ≥ $2M; RVol a **soft preference, not a hard gate**. Trigger = **`|chg| ≥ 3%` OR `|gap| ≥ 3%`** — screened on the trigger, not the close-to-close move, so gap-and-fade names are not silently dropped. **788 of 809 floor survivors were priced; there is no unpriced tail and no top-N cap on the screen.**
- **RVol basis.** Session volume ÷ the **trailing 10 settled sessions from the same series** (sessions ending 2026-11-02), computed market-wide from `polygon-io grouped_daily` in 11 calls. The TradingView `relative_volume_10d_calc` column is used only where that denominator is missing and is flagged `tv10d`; **the two denominators differ and are never mixed within a row**. A screener's own RVol column is not comparable across sources.
- **Tier rules used.** Tier A = trigger + liquidity floors + **RVol ≥ 2.0** + material same-session catalyst. Soft-A = trigger + floors + high-material catalyst + **known-soft RVol** (< 2.0). TECH_WATCH = trigger + floors + strong absolute turnover + no same-session catalyst. Tier B = trigger + floors with no or weak catalyst. Tier C = buyout gap watch. **A gap that FADED is demoted out of Tier A** (BLZE and PLTR were demoted on exactly this rule) and tagged `GAP_FADED`; a gap that **REVERSED** is tagged `REVERSED` and is treated as a positive in-play signal.
- **Buyout rule applied strictly.** ACB is Tier C despite being one of the day's largest gainers, because it still trades ~7.5% below the implied value of its stated cash terms. **A name is never promoted solely because a buyout was announced.**
- **Could not be verified.** **LULD / limit status for any name** (both halt feeds are dead). **The catalyst tape is absent**, so no name carries a verified catalyst *timestamp*. **The specific catalysts for the 274 TECH_WATCH names and the bulk of the 484 Tier B names** — those rows are explicitly labelled "no catalyst verified" or "read-across" and are not dressed up as information. **`polygon-io`'s 2026-11-03 bar** (so the settled volume numerator is Yahoo's, not a second independent settled feed). **The figures for BETA, whose 10-Q and 8-K were filed today but which I did not read.**
- **Low float.** `shares_outstanding_float` was carried for the whole board and the `⚠ low-float` tag is applied only where it measures **< 20M shares outstanding**. **No published Tier A / Soft-A name carries the tag.**
