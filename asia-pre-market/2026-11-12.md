# Asia pre-market reactions — Thursday, 12 November 2026

## TL;DR

- **All four markets closed lower, and the damage is concentrated in one place: semiconductors.** Taiwan -2.63% (43,091.87), Hong Kong -1.26% (24,008.55), Japan -0.70% (60,819.36), Korea -0.61% (5,342.73). The driver was Nvidia, which fell **-6.88% in the US pre-market on 7.58m shares** after the Jensen Huang controversy broke overnight, and every large move in the tables below sits in that complex - or in gold, where spot broke below US$4,000/oz on a Fed 50bp-hike repricing.
- **Taiwan was the most consequential market** and the only real sector break: electronic components -5.97%, six PCB and board names locked at the TWSE -10% limit, TSMC -2.30% (roughly 440 points of the index) and ASE -5.02%. It is the second session of violent chop after a -12.19% week, with the NT$500bn National Financial Stabilization Fund already deployed.
- **The caveat that changes how to read it:** the index damage is much smaller than the table implies, because the two biggest *corporate* events of the session were earnings in opposite directions - Resonac +12.48% on a sharply raised FY26 forecast (core operating profit guidance lifted to ~¥250bn) against Rakuten Bank -14.87% and Dexerials -16.57% on guidance left unchanged - and none of the three has a US-listed proxy. Also treat the top numbers as artefacts where they are limit prints: Korea's +29.93% is the KRX daily limit on a thin-float preferred line, and Taiwan's -10.00% is the TWSE limit.

**US read-across.** The overnight move is already partly priced in the US pre-market, but only the semiconductor leg of it: Nvidia -6.88% on 7.58m shares, SK hynix -3.97% on 309,519, Alibaba -4.26% on 341,317, TSM -2.26% on 62,292 and the South Korea ETF -1.94% on 186,046. The gold leg is not priced (GLD -0.95% on 109,056 versus -10.35% in Hong Kong gold equities), and neither is the earnings dispersion.

- **Exposed US names.** TSM -2.26% pre-market (62,292 shares) carries the Taiwan PCB/AI-hardware rout; SKHY -3.97% (309,519) carries the Korean semiconductor complex; BABA -4.26% (341,317) carries the Hong Kong/China risk-off. For the day's biggest single mover, HKEX:2259 Zijin Gold International, the only US-listed line is the OTC ADR ZJGIY - **no material US pre-market print yet**.
- **Exposed US sectors.** Electronic Technology / Semiconductor is where the Asian damage concentrates (Nvidia, TSM, SK hynix, and the OTC ADRs for Ibiden and Square Enix, neither of which has a pre-market print). The largest US pre-market moves overall - ADXN +60.1%, YMT -15.4%, BZ -17.8% - are idiosyncratic name events, not this read-across.
- **The overnight implication.** The US pre-market has priced the Nvidia leg of the Asia move, and has not priced the Japan earnings dispersion or the gold-equity break - neither has a US proxy. The residual risk sits in the US session, not in the Asia open; a Taiwan stabilization-fund headline is the one overnight catalyst that could re-open the Asia gap.

## Method, source health & caveats

**Method.** TradingView's per-country scanner, eight calls (4 markets x up/down legs), with the up leg filtered `change > +2` and the down leg `change < -2`, a US$2bn market-cap floor converted to local currency (JPY 159.23, TWD 32.925, HKD 7.8456, KRW 1,336.9), and a local-price floor of 5. `change` is read in percent units. Each market keeps the top 15 up and 15 down. The primary-listing rules were applied before the cap: the Japanese regional-venue rule (all 49 NAG/FSE/SAPSE/SSEO rows resolved against their TSE line - 8 substituted, 28 dropped for having no TSE line, the rest filtered on the primary listing), Hong Kong rows with a non-HKD currency dropped (3 rows), TPEX kept only where no TWSE listing of the same company exists (2 kept), and Hong Kong dual-ticker dedupe on company name plus market cap (0 found today). Market status came from the index session date (Test A) corroborated by an archive comparison against the 2026-11-11 artifact (Test B): all four markets returned a session date of 2026-11-12 and an archive verdict of fresh, so all four published.
