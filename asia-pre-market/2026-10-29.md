## TL;DR

Asia split hard on Thursday: the **Nikkei closed up 1.10%** while Taiwan (-1.42%), Hong Kong (-0.59%) and Korea (-1.24%) all fell, and the damage was company-specific rather than macro - earnings, one collapsed contract and one vague shareholder-return plan. **Korea was the loudest session** (44 names moved more than 4%) - Daewoo E&C (-18.57%) and Doosan Enerbility (-7.65%) were hit by the collapse of the Czech Dukovany nuclear consortium talks, and Samsung Electronics (-4.13%) dragged the KOSPI below 6,000 on a vague shareholder-return plan despite a record print. **Taiwan was a semis rout** - 29 names moved more than 5%, UMC closed at its low (-9.77%) and TPEx saw both a limit-up (Win Semiconductors, +10.00%) and a limit-down (Chunghwa Precision Test, -10.00%). **Japan was the outlier**: the Nikkei rose 1.10% even as Hitachi fell 9.77% on a one-off cost overrun despite record revenue and a second guidance raise, and ONO Pharmaceutical fell 9.99% on the end of the Forxiga co-promotion.

Earnings-cycle note: Japan reported Q2 (Apr-Sep) on Oct 29 and Korea reported Q3 on Oct 28-29, so this is a peak earnings window for both markets; Taiwan's next earnings cluster sits in mid-November.

### Method and caveats

- **Companion file.** `2026-10-29.csv` carries all 303 rows (98 REACTION-tier, 205 notable) with close, change, volume, local and USD market cap, the screener's next earnings date and the resolved catalyst.

- **Universe.** Primary-listing exchange only: TSE, TWSE, TPEx, HKEX, KRX. Screened with `type = stock`, `country = <Japan|Taiwan|Hong Kong|South Korea>`, `|change| > 2%`, `close > 5` in local currency, and `market_cap_basic > $2bn` **converted to local currency with FX** (TradingView's `market_cap_basic` is local currency, so an unconverted 2,000,000,000 filter would have meant ~$13m in Japan).
- **Dedupe.** 14 Japan rows dropped as regional-venue duplicates (NAG/FSE carrying a stale prior close against the TSE line - e.g. NAG:4361 showed +37.27% on 100 shares where TSE:4361 was +1.20%); 2 Hong Kong CNY-denominated duplicate counters dropped (HKEX:80992 Lenovo, HKEX:80175 Geely - thin counters off the liquid HKD line); 2 Korea Doosan preference lines folded into the 000150 common line; Taiwan TPEx names kept because their TWSE ticker does not exist.
- **Earnings dates are the *next* scheduled print, not a "reported today" flag.** `earnings_release_next_date` pointed at 2027-02-01 for ONO Pharmaceutical, 2027-02-09 for SKC and 2027-01-26 for UMC, all of which had in fact already reported. Earnings-driven flags marked `Y(date)` are the screener's own date, and where a print was confirmed by a company source the row carries the real catalyst.
- **No daily price limits on the TSE** for large caps since Oct 2024, so Japan moves are genuine repricings; TPEx and KOSPI/KOSDAQ still apply limits, and the two TPEx +/-10.00% rows are genuine limit moves (both closed at the day's extreme).
- **KRX limit moves:** none of the Korean names below hit the +/-30% KOSPI/KOSDAQ limit - the biggest, Daewoo E&C at -18.57%, is a fundamental move, not a limit artefact.
- **Unverified:** 68 of the 98 REACTION-tier names carry "no fresh catalyst found" - the catalyst budget was spent on the largest and most index-relevant names, not on every row. Convano (+15.46%) traded only 261,400 shares, so its move is thin and may be a small-float artefact.
- **Catalyst-call budget:** the skill caps catalyst lookups at 4 (one per market). This run used **8** - the 4 per-market calls plus 4 targeted follow-ups on large-cap REACTION-tier names (ONO Pharmaceutical, SKC, and market-level context for Taiwan and Hong Kong). The overage is disclosed rather than silently taken, because publishing an unexplained -10% or -12% on a $3-7bn name would be worse than the cap.
