# Asia pre-market reactions — Friday, 20 November 2026

## TL;DR

- **Asia was sold hard, and it was one trade, not forty.** Anthropic's record Nasdaq debut faded in after-hours, the crowded AI complex unwound, and the chip-heavy markets took the full hit: TAIEX **-6.23%** to 36,033.29 (a **-17.50%** five-session slide, the worst week since 1991), Nikkei **-5.06%** to 56,436.65 (a seven-month low), KOSPI **-5.21%** to 4,869.96 (sell-sidecars triggered on both KOSPI and KOSDAQ). The Hang Seng held up best at **-1.89%**.
- **Taiwan is the market that matters.** 147 names sat at the -10% limit intraday (TVBS), TSMC -4.8% and MediaTek -10%, and all 15 published Taiwan rows print between -10.00% and -9.98%: these are limit prices, not clearing prices. The Taiwan long is the position most likely to gap at the US open.
- **Read it as macro, and discount the single-name narrative.** The driver was the AI trade repricing after Anthropic's debut fizzled, so only **3 company-specific catalysts surfaced across all 73 published rows** (SoftBank, Hanjin KAL, Netease). Japan's insurer pair (Tokio Marine +2.03%, Sompo +2.01%) is the only sector that moved the other way, and the numbers most likely to be mis-quoted are the Taiwan limit cluster, one dropped Taiwan row and a stale index percentage (all three in *Traps* and *Flags*).

**US read-across.** The US cash session on 19 Nov already broke the AI trade (Dow -2.19%, Nasdaq -2.25%, Philadelphia chip index -2.4%), so Asia is largely continuation — but the overnight legs are steeper than the US close: TAIEX -6.23%, KOSPI -5.21% and the Nikkei -5.06% all print a heavier session than the -2% US close, and S&P futures are only -0.61% at 06:30 ET. The US open, not the futures, carries the gap.

- `TSM` $352.09, pre-market **-4.00%** on 253,731 shares — the one material US pre-market print attached to Asia's heaviest weight, and it is already down.
- `BABA` $83.31, pre-market **+0.72%** on 164,500 shares — Hong Kong's large-cap is the one Asian gainer with a live US line; `NTES` closed -11.39% and its pre-market print (16,731 shares) is too thin to quote.
