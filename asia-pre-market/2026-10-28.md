## TL;DR

One macro story drove all four markets: **SK hynix's blockbuster Q3** (revenue ₩104.62T, operating profit ₩83.33T, +632% YoY, 9% above consensus; ₩100T shareholder-return plan for 2026) plus **Anthropic's IPO filing** and Broadcom's $63B Anthropic XPU financing deal, which re-litigated the AI trade after three weeks of doubt. Money went into semis/AI hardware, robotics and innovative drugs, and out of gaming, beverages, foods, utilities and casinos.

Korea was the epicentre (KOSPI +6.63% to 6,012.44, buy-side sidecar at 09:54 KST). Taiwan +2.04% to 47,035.58, Japan +1.1% to 63,954.19 (but TOPIX slightly negative — the Nikkei gain was semis-heavyweight, not broad), Hong Kong +0.23% to 23,094.83 with HSTECH +1.40%. Biggest single mover in the region: Ingenic Semiconductor (3223.HK) +18.94%. In Japan, MARUWA hit limit-up on a guidance raise; Capcom was the largest decliner at −12.44%.

Earnings-cycle note: Korea is at the peak of its Q3 window (SK hynix, Samsung Thu, Samsung Electro-Mechanics), whereas Japan's Q3 disclosure season is not until late Jan / early Feb — Japan's moves are guidance revisions and AI beta, not an earnings season.
