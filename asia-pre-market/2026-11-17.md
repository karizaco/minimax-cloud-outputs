# Asia pre-market reactions — Tuesday, 17 November 2026

## TL;DR
- **Asia sold a second session into Nvidia's print, and this time Hong Kong was the exception, not the rule**: Korea -2.59% (4,968.48, its first close below 5,000 in nine and a half months), Taiwan -2.31% (40,092.11), Japan -1.34% (58,860.43) and Hong Kong -1.03% (23,508.17) — with 188 of 225 Nikkei constituents lower and foreign investors selling KRW1.5tn of Korean stock.
- **Taiwan was the most consequential market again, but for a different reason**: the TAIEX swung 1,747 points intraday (39,786.45 → 41,533.84 → 40,092.11) and dealers said the National Financial Stabilization Fund intervened to limit losses — six published Taiwanese decliners sit pinned at the -10% daily limit, which is the floor, not a measured move.
- **The caveat that changes how to read it**: this is positioning ahead of Nvidia's Q3 print and the Anthropic IPO, not a fresh fundamental shock — the US pre-market is flat-to-slightly green in the same complex (NVDA +0.10% on 2.57m shares, MU +0.78% on 306k, MRVL +2.59% on 175k, TSM -0.66% on 198k), so Asia has priced a second leg down that the US tape has not confirmed.

**US read-across (what this means at 09:30 ET)**

The US session has already paid for part of this story: the AI-hardware complex was hit hard on Monday (SOXX -5.41%, NVDA -4.12%, MU -7.62%, MRVL -7.27%, INTC -7.02%, TER -7.07%, AMD -5.58%, ARM -8.12%, TSM ADR -6.34%), and the Hong Kong data-centre landlord GDS fell 14.69% there. What the US pre-market has NOT done is extend it — the same names are flat to slightly green this morning.

- **Exposed US names**: no Asian mover of note has a US-listed line with a material pre-market print — the Japanese names are OTC-only (RCRUY, SHWDY, IBIDY, IPXHY) with no quote at all; BEKE +1.82% on 43,993 shares and HSAI -2.87% on 11,891 are below the 50,000-share materiality floor, so read them as "no material US pre-market print yet". The exceptions with real volume are BILI -3.55% on 69,615 shares, BIDU -1.74% on 52,057, and XPEV -0.10% on 81,203.
- **Exposed US sectors**: Electronic Technology / Semiconductor is the transmission channel — the AI-hardware complex and the Taiwan/Korea foundry-and-substrate chain (TSM ADR, MU, MRVL, AMAT, LRCX, ASML) sold in Asia for a second session; the oil leg is separate, with Inpex -5.86% on a supply-surplus expectation, and the Japanese bank group lower on falling US Treasury yields.
- **The overnight implication**: the risk is not in the US open but in tonight's Nvidia print — Asia has de-risked twice into it, the US pre-market has priced almost none of that, so a positive print leaves the AI complex short of a squeeze while a negative one lands on a US tape that has not been marked down. The Hong Kong rally is the one soft patch in the Asian map (foundries such as SMIC +6.77% and Hua Hong +7.96% rose while the Taiwan supply chain was locked limit-down).

**Method.** Universe: Tokyo (TSE), Taiwan (TWSE), Hong Kong (HKEX), Korea (KRX), each capped at 15 rows per direction (30 rows per market) after a $2bn market-cap floor converted at the rates above, a >5 local-currency close floor and a ±2% change screen. Tiers are evaluated on the published 2-decimal percentage. Market status was decided on the index's own last daily bar (Test A) and cross-checked against the previous run's artifact (Test B: JP/TW/HK/KR all `fresh`, 0-16.7% of rows unchanged) — not on the row count.
