## Method, source health & caveats

**Method.** Eight TradingView scanner calls (four markets x up/down) with the cap floor applied server-side in local currency, pre-filtered to the primary listing venue, deduped, and capped at 15 names per direction per market. The down direction uses `change < -2`; re-sorting the gainer list ascending does **not** return losers. Every regional JP venue print (NAG / SAPSE / FSE) was resolved against the TSE line and discarded where a TSE line existed. Tiering is on |intraday %|: REACTION > 4% (JP, KR, HK) and > 5% (TW), NOTABLE 2-4% (2-5% for TW). All 102 published names appear exactly once. Market status came from the index session date (Test A), corroborated by the archive comparison against the previous artifact (Test B, fresh for all four markets) and the bridge's `market_state`.

- Korea's ±30% KOSPI limit (and ±15% for most KOSPI names) and Taiwan's ±10% limit mean several published rows are limit-locked rather than traded outcomes.

- Press quotes of index levels, individual closes and pre-market moves are point-in-time snapshots; where they conflicted with a settled close, the settled close is published and the conflict is flagged.
