# Asia pre-market reactions — 2026-10-22 (Thu)

## TL;DR
- **Wednesday's rebound fully erased; Hong Kong was the epicentre.** HSI **-3.03%** (22,934.68, ~3.5-month low, largest single-day fall in ~7 months), Nikkei **-2.65%** (62,907.84), KOSPI **-3.52%** (5,533.77), TAIEX **-1.81%** (46,041.43). Hang Seng Tech **-5.03%** to 3,793.93 — a two-year low.
- **Trigger: late US earnings revived AI-capex doubts.** SpaceX's first public-company profit and Meta's revenue beat initially reassured, but **Meta's plan to issue non-voting Class C stock rekindled capital-commitment concerns**. Then **Lam Research fell ~6% after hours** on a weaker outlook (Iwai Cosmo's Saito pointing to China demand) — the proximate spark for chip-equipment selling. Capital.com's Rodda: *"we're really only at the start of the meaty part of earnings season."*
- **Second driver: oil.** Brent **+2% to $111.03**, a five-month high, on further Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia and reports of planned US/Israeli strikes on Iran.
- **Widest reaction: W-Scope 6619.T -10.00%** (JP). Biggest blue chips: **Lenovo 0992.HK -10.92%** (HK worst, reportedly seeking a $2bn loan), **Meiko 6787.T -9.79%**, **Japan Display 6740.T -9.09%**, **SoftBank 9984.T -6.86%**, **Tokyo Electron 8035.T -6.99%**, **Advantest -6.01%**, **Alibaba 9988.HK -6.12%**, **Samsung 005930.KS -4.03%**, **SK hynix 000660.KS -5.49%**.
- **One genuine earnings winner against the tape: Samsung E&A 028050.KS +16.14%** — Q3 operating profit **KRW 500.6bn, +183.6% y/y**, revenue +45.8%, YTD orders +317.2%, FY2026 guidance raised to KRW 19tn orders / KRW 1.4tn OP; also retiring ₩690bn of treasury shares.
- **Structural warning:** **China's CSI 300 entered a technical bear market** — ~20% below its June peak after ten straight weekly losses. Taiwan foreign selling hit NT$816.57bn in one session; October cumulative is NT$821bn.
