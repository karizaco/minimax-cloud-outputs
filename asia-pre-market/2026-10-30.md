# Asia pre-market reactions — 2026-10-30

Scan of the just-closed Asian equity session (Fri 30 Oct 2026 local) across TSE, TWSE, HKEX and KRX, produced 06:30 ET ahead of the US pre-open brief. Read-only; no trade ideas.

## TL;DR

Hong Kong was the lone gainer (+1.46%) while Korea (-3.48%), Taiwan (-2.49%) and Japan (-2.12%) sold off — all three AI-heavy markets repriced the same overnight US trigger: Alphabet -12% on a revenue miss, Intel -23% after hours and Amazon -7% on cash-burn concerns, which re-opened the "will AI capex pay back?" debate. The dispersion is the story: the damage was concentrated in AI hardware and semiconductors (Japan's chip-equipment complex, Taiwan's electronics, Korea's chip and auto names), while the winners were an entirely different trade — shipping on Red Sea/Hormuz capacity contraction (Wan Hai, Evergreen, Yang Ming, HMM, COSCO Shipping Ports, NYK) and Chinese-domestic consumer, property and utilities in Hong Kong. Japan split cleanly by sector: chip equipment and AI infrastructure collapsed (Kurita Water -20.99%, Fujitsu -16.20%, IBIDEN -15.21%) while gaming, theme parks, airlines and retail rallied (KONAMI +15.02% on record results, Oriental Land +8.45%). Biggest single mover overall was Kurita Water Industries at -20.99% on a US-project loss provision; 77 REACTION-tier names cleared the 2% screen, of which the majority were downside.

Earnings cycle context: Japan and Korea were in peak Q3 reporting (KONAMI, Kurita, Kia, Hanwha Aerospace, Oriental Land, AUO all printed on or just before the session), while Taiwan's selloff was a valuation event rather than a reporting one. The notable earnings pattern is that records did not help: Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Samsung Electro-Mechanics all reported record or raised numbers and still fell.

## Cross-market themes

1. **The trigger was one story.** Alphabet fell more than 12% after Q3 revenue rose 25% y/y to US$127.58bn but missed expectations; Intel plunged 23% after hours on a revenue-guidance miss and PC demand weakness; Amazon fell about 7% on the scale of its AI spending. The Nasdaq Composite closed -1.38% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index -1.4%, and every Asian AI-linked market followed.
2. **Records stopped working.** Samsung Electronics and SK hynix fell on record earnings; Samsung Electro-Mechanics fell 7.50% after raising its Q4 operating-profit estimate 28.44% and signing a KRW879.3bn AI-server capacitor order; Delta Electronics fell 9.80% while guiding *up* on cloud capex. The market repriced the multiple, not the numbers.
3. **Shipping was the counter-trade, across all four markets.** Rerouting around the Red Sea and the Strait of Hormuz has added 10-14 days to Asia-Europe transits and structurally contracted effective capacity; the BDI rose 2.26% to 2,712 with Capesize +5.25%, and COSCO Shipping Holdings' Q3 net profit jumped 80.7% y/y. That lifted NYK (+5.55%), HMM (+4.71%), Wan Hai (+4.35%), Evergreen (+4.02%), COSCO Shipping Ports (+7.42%), Orient Overseas (+3.59%) and Yang Ming (+2.42%).
4. **Two consumers of the same theme split.** Hardware (IBIDEN, Lasertec, Sumco, Murata) and the consumer/leisure complex (KONAMI, Oriental Land, Bandai Namco, Capcom, Toei Animation) moved in opposite directions inside Japan, and the consumer names that rallied did so on their own earnings.
