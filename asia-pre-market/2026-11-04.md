# Asia pre-market reactions — 2026-11-04

Post-close scan of the just-finished Asian equity session (JP / TW / HK / KR), built 06:30 ET ahead of the US pre-open. All prices are the **settled close** of each market's 4 Nov session; market caps converted to USD at open.er-api rates (JPY 158.399, TWD 32.771, HKD 7.846, KRW 1,343.93).

## TL;DR

- A **V-shaped election day**: early US-midterm results pointing to a Democratic Congress triggered an AI/data-centre-capex scare that hit Japan hardest, then both Japan and Korea round-tripped into the close.
- **Japan was the outlier** — Nikkei -1.42% to 62,188.63 after being as low as 60,030 (-4.7% intraday), with the damage concentrated in AI hardware/materials (NTT -6.02%, Furukawa -6.19%, Sumitomo Electric -7.25%, Kyocera -7.40%). Korea closed **up** (KOSPI +0.62%) and Hong Kong was the session's winner (+1.67%) on gold and HK-local AI hardware.
- **Biggest movers**: Lingbao Gold (HK) +17.04%, Hanwha Solutions (KR) +18.64%, Krafton (KR) +16.55%, Kurita Water (JP) +15.06%, HLB (KR) -17.69% on an FDA classification setback. Kurita's +15% is the one headline move **without a sourced catalyst**.
- **Order of this brief:** grouped by country — **Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong** — and within each country every row is sorted by the absolute size of the move, biggest first.
- **Earnings-cycle context**: outside the Q3 reporting window for JP/TW; HK reports semi-annually; Korea's window is late Oct, so the KR prints that dominate the tape (Hanwha Solutions 9 Nov, SKC) are just behind us.
