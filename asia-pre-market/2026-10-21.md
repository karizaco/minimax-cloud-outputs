# Asia pre-market reactions — 2026-10-21 (Wed)

## TL;DR
- **Rebound day, except Hong Kong.** KOSPI **+3.29%** (5,735.66), TAIEX **+1.28%** (46,887.77), Nikkei **+0.85%** (64,621.24) — all recovering from Tuesday's rout. **Hang Seng -0.90%** (23,651.87) was the sole decliner, dragged by CATL.
- **Catalyst: Super Micro Computer's earnings beat + raised sales outlook**, which eased the AI-spending doubts that drove Tuesday's sell-off; SpaceX's robust AI/satellite results reinforced it. Kiwoom's Han Ji-young: *"bargain hunting after the previous session's sharp sell-off, buoyed by easing concerns over oil prices and interest rates and upbeat earnings from Super Micro."*
- **Worst fade of the day was Japan.** The Nikkei opened at 64,885.95, spiked to 65,404.63 (+1,300 pts) in the first half-hour, then gave it all back on profit-taking above 65,000 and **closed near the session low** (64,621.24, ~7% off the low end of an 846-pt range). It's a recovery day, not a bullish reversal.
- **Biggest single-name moves:** **SK Inc 034730.KS +12.62%** (₩482,000, reversing ~-8% Tuesday); **Samsung E&A 028050.KS +9.40%**; **Japan Display 6740.T +6.45%**; **SK hynix 000660.KS +5.81%** (₩1.29m); **Samsung Electronics 005930.KS +5.67%** (₩223,500); **Nan Ya Plastics 1303.TW +8.28%**; **CATL 3750.HK -11.41%** (HK's worst blue chip, post-earnings); **Tencent 0700.HK -4.04%** (broke below HK$400).
- **Secondary driver — export data.** Korea's first-20-day October exports **+105.4% y/y to US$61.6bn**, semiconductors **+222.5%**. Japan's September exports **+16.7% y/y**, a 13th straight month of growth.
