# Asia pre-market reactions — Thursday, 05 November 2026

## TL;DR

- **What Asia did:** an AI-hardware rout hit the three chip-heavy markets — Taiwan **-3.20%**, Korea **-5.28%**, Japan **-1.79%** on the Nikkei — while Hong Kong bucked it at **+3.67%**. The trigger was the Democratic sweep in the US midterms reviving fears of tighter AI/data-centre rules, stacked on a Philadelphia Semiconductor index that fell 3.1% overnight to a six-month low after Qualcomm and Arm results.
- **Most consequential market: Korea.** KOSPI closed at **5,383.80 (-5.28%)**, the Sell-Sidecar program-trading curb triggered intraday, ~$203bn of market value was erased, and foreigners sold a net ₩3.40tn. KOSDAQ fell 5.11% to 678.41 on the same day.
- **The caveat that changes how to read it:** this is a factor rotation inside markets, not broad risk-off. TOPIX rose **+1.16%** while the Nikkei fell -1.79%, and the buying was concentrated in autos (Toyota +16.68%), pharma, internet and beaten-down names. Fifteen Taiwan names printed at (or within 0.05% of) the **-10% daily limit** and several Korea moves exceed the ±15% KOSPI limit — those percentages are clipped, not closing sentiment.

**US read-across (what this means at 09:30 ET).** The US cash session on 4 Nov already took the semiconductor de-rating (DELL -8.8%, VRT -12.9%, FLEX -12.3%, SOX -3.1%), so the Asian close is mostly a confirmation of that trade. The new information in the Asian session is (a) the *policy* layer — data-centre rules — which US chips have not yet priced, and (b) the rotation into autos and non-tech, which US listings have barely begun to price.

- **Exposed US names / ADRs:** QCOM **-9.89%** pre-market on 105,816 shares (material print) after its results; ARM -4.68% (91,626), MU -2.72% (286,217), TSM -1.68% (61,006), NVDA -0.89% (418,926), MRVL -3.12%, GLW -3.02%. Toyota's ADR (TM) prints +8.74% but on only 2,425 shares — **no material US pre-market print yet**, so the +16.68% Tokyo move has not reached the US tape.
- **Exposed US sectors:** Electronic Technology / Semiconductor is the direct expression (semis, test and assembly equipment: MU, QCOM, ARM, TSM, AMAT -2.82% on a thin 23,960 shares, LRCX -2.61%, TER -2.39%, VRT -2.60%); Consumer Durables is the other side of the rotation (F +0.87% on 76,093 shares). No direct US-listed proxy exists for most JP/TW/KR names in today's tables.
- **The overnight implication:** the priced part is the chip selloff; the un-priced part is the data-centre policy risk and the Asian auto bid. The US open carries that gap — a Reuters-style policy headline re-opens the chip leg, while a benign US policy headline leaves the autos/non-tech rotation without a US bid.

## Method, source health & caveats

- **Method:** TradingView per-country scanner (japan / taiwan / hongkong / korea; up and down legs, 8 POST calls, `change` in percent units, `market_cap_basic` in local currency filtered at the USD $2bn equivalent), primary-listing whitelist applied (TSE / TWSE / HKEX / KRX; NAG, FSE, TPEX, SAPSE, OTC, LSE dropped unless the primary venue had no line), HK CNY counters dropped, HK dual-ticker duplicates merged, top 15 up + 15 down per market published. Index levels and session dates come from the Yahoo chart API's **last daily bar** for ^N225, ^TWII, ^HSI, ^KS11 and ^KQ11 (bar timestamps converted with the exchange timezone in `meta.exchangeTimezoneName`); index % changes come from two consecutive bars, never from the MCP bridge's `previous_close`.
- **Market status:** all four markets are **open** for 2026-11-05 — Test A (index session date) equals the fire date for every market, Test B (archive comparison against the 2026-11-04 artifact) returns `fresh` for all four (0% of prior rows unchanged, 0/5 top-5 matches). `^N225` shows no 2026-11-03 bar, which is the Culture Day holiday, not a data gap.
- **FX:** open.er-api.com (2026-11-05 00:02 UTC): JPY 158.378, TWD 32.824, HKD 7.845, KRW 1,340.31. Market caps are converted at those rates; a 1% FX move shifts the displayed market cap by ~1%.
- **Earnings-driven column:** `Y` only where an earnings print is the identified driver (Organo). `—` means not determined (preferred lines). The screener's next-print field is an estimate and is labelled as such.
- **Caveats:** the scanner's `update_mode` for Japan and Korea reads `delayed_streaming_1200` (up to a 20-minute delay) and Taiwan/Hong Kong `delayed_streaming_900`; prints are close-of-session but can still be revised. No trade ideas, no price targets — this is a read-only brief.
