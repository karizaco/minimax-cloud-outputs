# Asia pre-market reactions — 2026-10-20 (Tue)

## TL;DR
- **All four markets down, Korea hit hardest.** KOSPI **-3.73%** (5,552.74) fully erased the Oct 16 + Oct 19 two-day rebound (+213.79 pts). Nikkei **-1.30%** (64,075.42), one-month low. TAIEX **-1.13%** (46,292.93), 5-week low. Hang Seng **-1.32%** (23,867.74).
- **Dominant theme: unresolved AI-financing doubts.** Overnight the Nasdaq gained 1% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index posted its first gain in nine sessions — but Asia sold off anyway. Daishin's Lee Kyung-min: *"Concerns about credit and profitability surrounding AI were not completely resolved, limiting the sustainability of the rebound."* Secondary hits: rising Treasury yields, Brent ~$110, European political/fiscal instability, and reports that Trump sold Micron/AMD from an account in his own name.
- **Widest single-name reaction: Convano 6574.T -12.93%** (Japan). Biggest blue-chip drag: **SoftBank 9984.T -6.01%** — its CDS spreads hit the highest in nearly four years on AI-financing and possible major-acquisition concerns. **Lenovo 0992.HK -9.98%** was HK's worst blue chip (worst performer globally among mega-caps). **Hanwha Solutions 009830.KS -10.02%** led Korea.
- **Sector detail:** Korean **defense** was the worst-hit sector (Hyundai Rotem -13.70%, Hanwha Systems -13.90%, Hanwha Aerospace -11.71%, LIG D&A -8.21% — ~₩10.2trn of value shed). Korean **secondary battery** -5.50% erased 71% of Monday's gain (LG Chem -8.35%, EcoPro BM -9.15%, Samsung SDI -6.08%). HK **Hang Seng Tech** hit an intraday low of 4,026.90 — the lowest since 26 Sep 2024.
- **Countertrend:** HK pharma/physical-AI bucked the rout — Hansoh +8.3%, WuXi Biologics +3.84%, Sunny Optical +4.51%. Taiwan financials were the worst TW sector (-2.4%): Cathay Financial -3.93%, Fubon -3.15%.
