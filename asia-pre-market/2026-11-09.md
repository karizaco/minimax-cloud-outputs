## Flags

**Numbers a human should distrust, and why:**

1. **Japan Tobacco -15.95% is a real event, not a distribution.** The drop is a round -¥1,000 (-19.31% limit-down on 6 Nov, the largest daily fall since 2000 per Japanese press, then a further -15.95% on 9 Nov — roughly -32% over two sessions). JT's dividend ex-dates are 29 Jun and 29 Dec 2026, so this is **not** an ex-dividend adjustment. The trigger is a Russian decree placing JT's local subsidiaries under temporary management, with the scope widened on 9 Nov to the sales companies. As of the 9 Nov disclosure JT had **not** announced an impairment amount, a deconsolidation decision, or any change to its JPY272 annual dividend. The "~JPY1.5T of Russian assets, ~25% of profits" and "~JPY4.4T of value lost" figures are **press estimates carried from Japanese commentary and are not independently verified here**.
2. **Dropped row: a bogus Takeda print.** The scanner returned SAPSE:4502 Takeda at +23.26% on a **100-share** print at a non-primary venue (Sapporo). The real TSE line moved **+1.72%** (Yahoo, 6,560 → 6,673) — below the 2% floor, so Takeda is not published. The +23.26% figure should not be quoted.
3. **Korea's top two rows are limit artifacts, not common-stock moves.** HANJIN KAL Pfd +29.90% and Korean Air Pfd +29.87% are **preferred-share lines pinned at the 30% KOSPI limit** (tagged `KR_LIMIT_MOVE` in the table). ## Method, source health & caveats

**Method.** Eight TradingView scanner calls (four markets x up/down) with the cap floor applied server-side in local currency, pre-filtered to the primary listing venue, deduped, and capped at 15 names per direction per market. The down direction uses `change < -2`; re-sorting the gainer list ascending does **not** return losers. Tiering is on |intraday %|: REACTION > 4% (JP, KR, HK) and > 5% (TW), NOTABLE 2-4% (2-5% for TW). All 95 published names appear exactly once. Market status came from the index session date (Test A), corroborated by the archive comparison against the previous artifact (Test B, fresh for all four markets) and the bridge's `market_state`.

**Caveats.**

- Yahoo symbol resolution is suffix-sensitive: Korea needs `.KS`/`.KQ` and Taiwan `.TW`/`.TWO`; a `Not Found` is a symbol-format miss, not a delisting, and never a reason to omit a row.
