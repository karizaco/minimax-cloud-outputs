## Method — and two deviations that change what you are reading

**1. The $2B market-cap floor had to be re-derived.** The skill specifies `market_cap_basic > 2000000000` (a USD figure) on the TradingView screener. The TradingView MCP is absent from this runtime, so the screener was reproduced on Yahoo's `v1/finance/screener` REST endpoint — where `marketCap` and `intradaymarketcap` are denominated in **local currency**, not USD. Applying `2e9` unconverted is a no-op for JPY and KRW (¥2bn ≈ $13m; ₩2bn ≈ $1.5m) and would have returned a near-complete exchange listing, not a screened universe. The floor was therefore converted before use: JPY 157.746, KRW 1328.52, TWD 32.437, HKD 7.8442 per USD (Yahoo, 2026-10-27). **This is a material change to the universe, not a cosmetic one** — on the unconverted floor the screener returns 542 Japan gainers; on the converted floor it returns 110.

**2. Because the floor excludes most of the day's actual news, this brief is reported in two layers.** Section "Core" below is the skill-spec universe (≥$2B USD cap, |move| ≥2%). Section "Below the floor" lists names outside it that carry a *publisher-verified* catalyst. Without that second layer the brief would omit the region's biggest event of the day — a live tender offer — and four exchange-limit moves. **No row is reported without an exchange or publisher anchor.**

Venue, close and change for every Taiwan row are taken from **official** TWSE/TPEx daily-close files, not from Yahoo. One Yahoo Taiwan error was found and corrected (see Data quality).

**Catalyst cap:** 4 lookups, one per market, as the skill requires. Source is `web_search` — Yahoo's HTML news pages have returned empty for every symbol since 2026-10-20.
