# Asia pre-market reactions — Wednesday, 25 November 2026

## TL;DR

- **Asia gave back the entire Jensen Huang relief rally and then some.** The TAIEX fell **-4.80%** to **38,195.43**, the KOSPI **-6.16%** to **4,697.51**, the Nikkei **-2.78%** to **57,011.62**, and the Hang Seng **-1.14%** to **21,966.01** — after Tuesday's record-setting bounce (TAIEX +8.99%, its largest single-day point gain on record). The trigger was Wall Street's own AI unwind: Nvidia closed Tuesday **-5.86%** at $155.38 and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell **-7.88%**, with fresh US tariff threats on China, Japan and Korea layered on top.
- **Taiwan is the market that matters, and the index move understates it.** All **15** published Taiwan rows are limit-locked or near-limit prints — five closed at exactly **-10.00%** — and the TAIEX is **-17.14%** over November, its worst month since 2008. TSMC fell only **-3.59%** to NT$2,150 because bargain hunters bought it, so the **-4.80%** index number badly flatters the damage done to the chip complex underneath it.
- **The caveat that changes how to read it: this is a de-risking of a one-day relief rally, not a new information shock — and the US pre-market is still falling.** The 18 November Fed minutes flagged AI investment as an inflation and financial-stability risk, US PCE lands today, and at ~06:35 ET Nvidia was still **-4.65%** pre-market on 2.74m shares. The second leg of the de-risking is being priced in the US session, not yet in Asia's close.

**US read-across.** Wall Street set the tape and the US pre-market is extending it: Nvidia -5.86% at the close and a further -4.65% pre-market, Micron -11.01% then -5.44%, and the Asian ADR lines are still lower again in the pre-market (SK hynix -5.07%, UMC -10.00%).
