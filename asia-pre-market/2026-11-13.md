# Asia pre-market reactions — Friday, 13 November 2026

## TL;DR

- **Asia split hard along the AI chain: Taiwan up 1.35%, Hong Kong down 1.55%.** The TAIEX closed at **43,675.12 (+1.35%)**, its best week since 2002, on an ASIC rally — MediaTek +7.01%, Alchip +9.39%, Global Unichip +9.54%, Win Semiconductors +9.86% — while the Hang Seng fell **-1.55% to 23,637.60** with the Hang Seng Tech index **-3.02%** as AI-hardware and AI-application names sold off on the Jensen Huang resignation (Z.AI -10.04%, MiniMax -8.19%, Kanzhun -24.69%, Kinwong -13.73%, Victory Giant -13.46%). Japan was a wash at **61,142.35 (+0.53%)** and the KOSPI fell **-0.98% to 5,290.53**.
- **Korea was the most consequential market** because it reversed an early rally and gave back a 2.05% gap on the Huang news, and because the day's single largest corporate event landed there: **Korea Electric Power -9.79%** on a preliminary Q3 operating profit down 70.9% to ₩1.6441tn against ₩2.4143tn consensus. Foreigners turned from buyers in the morning to sellers into the close, and the index touched 5,504.44 before closing 214 points lower.
- **The caveat that changes how to read it: the two biggest headline numbers in this brief are artefacts.** The top two Korean decliners are **thin preferred lines** (Korean Air Pfd -15.64%, HANJIN KAL Pfd -15.41%) whose common stocks closed -0.32% and +₩500 respectively, and Japan's biggest move (Asics -14.34%) is real but its sector is unrepresented in the US pre-market. Read the tables as *dispersion*, not as an index-level risk.

**US read-across.** The overnight move is partly priced but only in the Korea/HK leg: SK hynix -1.34% on 347,055 shares, Alibaba -1.70% on 162,048, TSMC +1.01% on 54,604. The Taiwan ASIC rally is **not** priced — MediaTek, Alchip, Global Unichip and Win Semi have no US-listed line with a pre-market print — and neither is the Japan earnings dispersion or the KEPCO collapse.

- **Exposed US names.** Korea Electric Power's NYSE ADR **KEP** shows -7.19% but on only 9,954 shares — no material US pre-market print yet. The day's biggest Japanese mover, Asics, has only the OTC line ASCCY (closed -5.06%, no pre-market print). The clean exposures are SKHY -1.34% (347,055 shares), BABA -1.70% (162,048) and TSM +1.01% (54,604).
- **Exposed US sectors.** Electronic Technology / Semiconductor is the whole story, and it is split: the Korea leg is down (SK hynix -3.03%) and the Hong Kong AI-hardware leg is down hard (Kinwong -13.73%, Victory Giant -13.46%, Zhongji Innolight -9.39%, Hua Hong -6.10%), while the Taiwan ASIC leg is up 7-10% with no US proxy. Utilities carries the KEPCO -9.79%; Energy Minerals is the one strong US-mapped sector (PetroChina +2.79% on Middle East crude).
- **The overnight implication.** The US pre-market has priced the Korea/HK AI-hardware leg and the Huang headline, and has NOT priced the Taiwan ASIC rally, the KEPCO earnings break or the Japan earnings dispersion — the first two have no US-listed proxy with a print. The residual risk therefore sits in the US session, not in the Asia open, and the one overnight catalyst that can re-open the gap is a fresh Huang-succession headline.

**Method.** Universe per market: the TradingView per-country scanner, ±2% change screen, market-cap floor US$2bn converted at the day's FX into local currency (JPY 319.6bn, TWD 65.95bn, HKD 15.69bn, KRW 2,668.4bn), close floor 5 in local currency. Rows are keyed by the bare ticker with the venue prefix restored; `change` is read as percent units; every tier test runs on the **published** 2dp percentage. Venue rules applied: every JP regional print (NAG/FSE/SAPSE/SSEO) was resolved against its TSE line and the regional row dropped - all 13 diverged, including Takeda +23.26% (SAPSE) against TSE +1.48%; TPEX kept only where no TWSE twin exists; HK rows in non-HKD currencies dropped or replaced by their HKD twin; the HK 5-digit/4-digit duplicate pair resolved to the 4-digit line. Caps: 15 rows per direction per market. Catalysts: 5 lookups - the top REACTION name in each market plus one extra for the third sourced event, disclosed here as the skill permits; no catalyst is invented, and unsourced rows carry the explicit "no company-specific catalyst identified in the sources checked". Read-only, no trade ideas, no price targets.

**Caveats.** The Asia session is closed and settled for all four markets, so the mover prints are final; the US pre-market quotes are live at ~06:40 ET and thin by construction, so only prints on 50,000+ shares are treated as a market. The four Hong Kong names dropped for CNY-only pricing are a real coverage gap. Korean preferred-line prints are tagged, not verified against the primary line. This artifact is read-only context for the US open, not a forecast.
