The Japanese AI-hardware names with the largest Tokyo moves have **no material US pre-market print yet**, and the Hang Seng's weakest lines (gold miners, China internet) have no clean ADR expression at all, so the US session carries the risk on both flanks.

What is NOT priced is the Tokyo AI-hardware bid - Advantest, Murata, Lasertec and Panasonic have no material pre-market print - and what has no bid at all is the HK gold/China-internet block. So the US session carries the risk on both flanks, and the Hang Seng's -0.91% is the one Asia leg with no overnight expression.
