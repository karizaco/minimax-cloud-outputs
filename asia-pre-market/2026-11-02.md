Earnings-cycle note: this is a **post-earnings reaction week** (JP Q2 FY27 prints on 30 Oct), and the trade is a sharp split between memory/semis damage and shipping/power/brokerage strength — not a uniform risk-off.

**Method notes / degraded inputs**
- `mcp__tradingview__*` and `mcp__yahoo_finance__*` are **not registered** in this runtime (as documented in the runtime contract). The TradingView **public scanner HTTP API** was used instead — richer than the absent MCP tool, since it also returns `earnings_release_next_date`.
- **Unit check passed:** the scanner's `change` column is already in **percent**, not a fraction. Verified by the identity `close − close/(1 + change/100) == change_abs` across all 281 priced rows — 0 mismatches. A ×100 rebuild would have inflated every move 100×.
- **JP regional-venue stale prints dropped: 12.** NAG/FSE rows carry a wrong prior close on tiny volume (Mitsubishi Heavy FSE −21.00% on 200 shares vs the real TSE print −1.46%; Tokyo Gas NAG −16.19% vs TSE +2.01%). Every regional row was resolved against its primary venue before publication.
- **HK CNY-counter duplicates dropped: 2** (filtered on `currency != HKD`), since the CNY lines quote off the liquid HKD line at an implied ~0.854 rate.
- **TPEx listings kept: 12** — each was resolved against TWSE and has no TWSE line, so these are genuine TPEx-primary names, not mis-tagged main-board rows.
- **Market-cap floor converted with FX** (the scanner's `market_cap_basic` is local currency): JPY 315.1bn / TWD 65.3bn / HKD 15.7bn / KRW 2,665.5bn for a $2bn intent.
- **`earnings_release_next_date` is forward-looking only** — it is the *next* scheduled print, never a "reported today" flag. Every earnings-driven Y/N tag above comes from a company or press source, not from that column.
- **KOSDAQ limit-move noise:** the sub-$2B KRX screen returned a cluster of KOSDAQ ±30% prints (e.g. CJ Seafood preferred −50.00%, NUINTEK −29.99%, WOT +29.92%). These are KOSDAQ daily-limit closes, not fundamental repricings, and are **excluded** from the headline tables.
- **HK volume check:** HKEX:2299 Billion Industrial printed +111.32% on **1 share** — excluded as a non-trade print.
- **TPEx volume conflict:** the scanner's volume can differ from Yahoo on TPEx rows (e.g. TPEX:3105). Close and change agree; volume is not quoted from the scanner for TPEx names.
- **One intraday figure discarded:** Investing.com quoted SCREEN at ¥9,253 (-18.6%) at 12:32 during the session; the settled close of ¥9,218 (-18.96%) is used.
- **US context:** the catalyst chain begins with the 30 Oct US session — SOX −7%, Intel −30%, SanDisk −13% — which drove the Asian semi complex. One early query returned a stale Yahoo quote page for 7735.T (¥12,805) and a stale TradingEconomics HK50 table (D-1 index levels); both were discarded in favour of settled closes from primary market sources.
