## Method, source health & caveats

- **Procedure**: TradingView per-country scanner, 8 legs (4 markets x up/down), `change > +2` and `change < -2`, `type=stock`, `market_cap_basic` above a $2bn floor converted at the session FX (JPY 160.0, HKD 7.846, KRW 1,337.13 per USD, TWD set at a conservative 60bn), `close > 5` local. Every leg was asserted complete (rows == totalCount) before merging; `range` was set to the scanner page cap of 20,000 so no tail was cut.
- **Venue hygiene**: all 16 Japan regional rows (NAG/FSE/SAPSE) were resolved against their TSE line via a wide-range `in_range` venue pull and dropped when TSE diverged; 28 Taiwan TPEX rows were kept only where no TWSE twin exists; 8 Hong Kong CNY rows were dropped; HK dual-ticker pairs were deduped by issuer (no pairs were present this run).
- **Ordering**: inside each country the REACTION table and the NOTABLE one-liners are one continuous descending `|change %|` stream (largest absolute move first, gainers and decliners interleaved by magnitude), per the skill's Output-ordering rule.
- **Market status**: all four markets were verified OPEN for 2026-11-16 by the index session date (Test A) and corroborated by the previous artifact comparison (Test B: JP/TW/HK/KR all `fresh`, 0.0%/7.7%/0.0%/0.0% of prior rows unchanged). No market was excluded and no market was closed.
- **Catalysts**: taken from same-day press rather than an earnings feed (no structured Asian earnings calendar exists in this runtime) — `asia_earnings_detection_method=press_catalyst_lookup`. `catalyst_lookups=5`: one per market plus the one extra lookup permitted by the skill's disclosed exception (used to reach three *sourced* events in `## What matters today` without padding the list with unsourced rows).
