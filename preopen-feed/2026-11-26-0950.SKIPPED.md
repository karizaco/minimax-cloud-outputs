# Pre-open catalyst capture — SKIPPED (checkpoint 0950)

- **Reason:** US market holiday — 2026-11-26 is Thanksgiving Day, and the NYSE is closed (the canonical fleet calendar `cloud-prompts/_shared/nyse-holidays.txt` lists `2026-11-26`; `window_check.sh` carries the same date in its `HOLIDAYS_DEFAULT`). There is no pre-market session and no open to capture, so this checkpoint was DECLINED by the shared window check (`window_check.sh`, evaluated against the fire's own instant) and no tape was published.
- **Checkpoint:** 0950 (fire instant 2026-11-26T09:50:00.267-05:00 ET; slot derived from `session.created_at`, not the wall clock)
- **Tape:** none. `preopen-feed/2026-11-26-0950.jsonl` is intentionally absent — a tape on a closed market is not a missing tape, it is a false one.
- **Digest:** none. `preopen-feed/2026-11-26-0950.md` is intentionally absent.

## Why a tape was not published even though the capture itself succeeded

The capture ran and returned 376 records, but its PRICE layer does not measure a pre-market
move, so it was rejected rather than published. Measured on this fire:

- **The scanner's pre-market reference close is STALE by one session.** For **362 of 362**
  tickers common to the 2026-11-25 and 2026-11-26 tapes, the implied reference close
  (price ÷ (1 + pct)) is IDENTICAL on both days. The 2026-11-26 band was therefore measured
  against the **2026-11-24** close, while the last completed regular session (2026-11-25)
  closed elsewhere — the scanner never rolls the pre-market anchor when the market is shut.
- **The published moves are therefore wrong, sometimes badly.** NRSN was published at
  **+93.64%** (print 2.13 against an implied reference of 1.10); against its actual last close
  of 1.76 the same print is **+21.02%** — inflated 4.5×. RAYA was published at +85.93%
  (print 0.79, implied reference 0.4249) against a true +20.61%. (The last-close measurements
  are the sibling 0830 checkpoint's; the identical-reference-close result above is this
  checkpoint's own independent measurement.)
- **The band is an artifact of that stale base, not a market.** The priced half of the tape
  came back **35 up / 329 down** — 90% decliners. A market that closed lower on 2026-11-25
  leaves the stale reference sitting ABOVE most names, so nearly everything reads as a
  decliner. The procedure's documented reference band was 271 up / 187 down (2026-11-13).
- **The capture's own band pull shows the same skew**: 168 up + 951 down across 1,119 names
  (85% decliners), with 765 dropped by the 25,000-share quote floor.

The catalyst layer the capture produced was real and dated (SEC EDGAR filings, one article,
eight router answers), but a tape whose `pct` column — the one thing it exists to carry — is
anchored to the wrong close is the same defect the procedure records for 2026-11-09
("publishing Friday's move as if it was Monday's pre-market"), and it must not be published.

## The rejected run's own ledger, for the record

The capture reported `errors=1` on its 376 records (`article_errors=15`, `router_errors=0`,
`provider_errors=1`), with `mover_coverage=14/15 (93%)` and 11 dated catalysts
(article=1, edgar=10, router=8), `stale_dropped=1`, `sign_conflicts=0`, `empty_dropped=9`,
`pool=20`, `premarket_priced=364`, `premarket_unprinted=12`,
`as_of=2026-11-26T14:50:40.761Z`. Those numbers belong to a tape that was **never
published** — a deliberate skip is not a failed capture, so the capture's own ledger does not
raise this checkpoint's error count. They are recorded here so the rejected run leaves no
silent trace.

The board rendered from that rejected tape passed the checkpoint gate (179/179 checks,
`tools/check_preopen_checkpoint.py`, `CHECKER_VERSION: 2026-11-20.1`). That is expected and is
not a defence of the tape: **the gate checks the artifact's internal consistency, and it
cannot know the market was closed.** The holiday is a question about the calendar, and only
`window_check.sh` asks it.

## What was published by mistake, and the retraction

This checkpoint ran the capture and pushed its tape and digest **before** the window check
was consulted, so a false artifact was briefly live on the outputs repo
(`preopen-feed/2026-11-26-0950.jsonl` and `preopen-feed/2026-11-26-0950.md`, commit
`37a019f`, on `main` and on the `master` mirror). It was **retracted** in commit `90ae956`,
which removes both files from `main` and fast-forwards `master` to match. The retraction is
a deletion commit on the branch, so the false artifact remains in the repository's history —
anyone reading the tree at `37a019f` will find it. The window check must run **first** on
this routine; this fire ran it late.
