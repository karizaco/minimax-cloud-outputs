## 4. What this file cannot contain

Stated explicitly so a later reader does not mistake the absence for an omission:

- **The top-10-by-V/OI table.** V/OI needs per-strike volume divided by per-strike open interest
  for contracts that have expired. Yahoo serves an empty chain for every expired expiry; Cboe's
  delayed quotes serve only the live session. Neither will return the 2026-10-19 book.
- **Per-strike IV.** Not archival anywhere reachable.
- **Per-strike moneyness.** Derivable in principle from the closes in section 3 plus a strike
  price, which is exactly what is unavailable.
- **Call/put split within the 8-name universe.** Cboe's historical service publishes per-underlying
  volume and its market statistics publish call/put splits per *product class*, not per underlying.
  The split in section 2 is market-wide, not universe-wide.
