1. **Six of the eight underlyings have no session-date expiry** in either tape (TLT, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOGL, AMZN), so their windows start 2026-11-04 and their same-day flow is absent from the data rather than from the scan. 2. **Cboe's `open_interest` is the prior session's settled OI** while `volume` is today's session, so V/OI is inflated by construction on a gap day. Today was a gap day for MSFT (+2.74%), NVDA (+2.29%), AMZN (+2.06%) and AAPL (-1.90%). The OI ≥ 50 slice is published alongside for exactly this reason.
3. **Cboe's IV column is an artifact on 0DTE ETF contracts** priced at a penny with a wide spread — 15 of the 70 invertible rows diverge by more than 10 vol points, all of them IWM/GLD 0DTE. See the IV reliability section; the inverted vol is quoted there instead.
4. **ITM/OTM is side-aware** — a call is ITM below spot, a put above spot. Validated against Yahoo's own `inTheMoney` flag on all 73 kept rows with zero disagreements.
5. **The single biggest line is wide and thin.** IWM 280P exp 2026-11-03 traded 195,366 contracts against an open interest of 3,886 (V/OI 50.3) — but the quote was 0.33 / 0.72, so the mid is 0.53 and the whole line is worth roughly $104k of premium. 6. **No second tape on option volume/OI today** (Nasdaq option-chain 404, second day running). The underlying closes are cross-checked on 8/8; the option volume and open-interest numbers are not.
7. **The window is a same-week book.** 36.8% of the chain's real-print volume trades outside the four listed expiries and is invisible to this scan.
