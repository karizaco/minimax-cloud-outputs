## Caveats

1. **AAPL has no 2026-11-02 or 2026-11-03 expiry in the Cboe snapshot**, so its 4-expiry window starts 2026-11-04 and its 0DTE flow is not in today's tape. AAPL's rows are all DTE ≥ 2.
2. **Cboe's `open_interest` is the prior session's settled OI** while `volume` is today's session, so V/OI is inflated by construction on a gap day. Today's session was a gap day for MSFT (−2.83%), AAPL (−1.83%) and NVDA (−1.26%). The OI ≥ 50 slice is published alongside precisely for this reason.
3. **Cboe's `iv` is a decimal fraction** and is rendered ×100 here; `iv = 0` is treated as missing (`n/a`), not as 0%. Cboe's IV is unreliable on deep-ITM near-worthless contracts (MSFT 525C shows 166.6% on a $0.000 theoretical value).
4. **ITM/OTM is side-aware** — a call is ITM below spot, a put above spot. Verified on every row above.
5. **The V/OI leaders are mostly tiny-open-interest 0DTE contracts.** GLD 361P (V/OI 1,509×) traded 9,057 contracts against an open interest of 6 — a real trade, but a fraction of a day's flow and a one-tick premium.
6. **No second tape on volume/OI today** (Nasdaq option-chain 404). The underlying closes are cross-checked, the option volume/OI numbers are not.
