## 0. Read this first — data-quality caveat

**The skill's primary tool, `mcp__tradingview-advanced__stock_options_unusual_activity`, does not exist in this runtime.** The MCP-over-HTTP bridge answers `stocktwits` and `shibui` only, and the pipeworx catalogue entry point that fronts both is currently out of anonymous quota (HTTP -32000, "Today's 50 free anonymous calls are used up"). The scan therefore ran against Cboe's delayed-quote option-chain CDN, reproducing the skill's `top_n=10, min_volume=100, expiries=4` semantics locally over the real tape. An independent second tape was still available and passed: **open interest agrees on 138 of 138 contracts (100%)** against Nasdaq's option-chain endpoint, and volume agrees on every contract with material size.

**Three measured facts change how this file should be read. None of them is a data error.**

1. **Roughly half the headline volume sits in contracts that are worth less than a nickel.** Of the 75 kept rows, **41 carry 2,100,257 contracts — 49.5% of the kept volume — against a Cboe theoretical value below $0.05.** The contract counts are real; the premium dollars behind them are trivial. Section 2c strips this layer out and is the economically meaningful read.
2. **Cboe reports open interest of exactly zero on three contracts, so V/OI is undefined for them.** The GOOGL 282.5 call, 277.5 put and 277.5 call (all 2026-10-30 expiry, all within 1% of spot) traded 84,629 contracts against OI 0 — they are series opened during today's session. The skill's `v_oi_ratio >= 1.0` filter cannot evaluate a ratio with a zero denominator, so these rows sit at the top of a naive V/OI ranking with no meaning attached. They are reported as `n/a` throughout.
3. **This was an expiry-day tape, and the 4-expiry window is wider than it looks.** The skill caps each underlying at its first four listed expiries; for six of the eight names those now include *daily* expiries (11-02, 11-03, 11-04), so the window reaches a week out. ### Selection sensitivity — the tag depends on how the per-underlying top-10 is chosen

The skill calls each underlying with `top_n=10` but never states the ranking. That choice changes the answer, because the call/put volume sums behind the bias tag are computed over whatever survives the cut.

| Per-underlying top-10 selected by | n kept | Call vol | Put vol | P/C | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Volume** (used here) | 75 | 2,067,308 | 2,177,796 | 1.05 | **MIXED** |
| V/OI | 73 | 551,215 | 186,676 | 0.34 | CALL |

The V/OI cut keeps 737,891 contracts versus 4,245,104 for the volume cut — it drops 82.6% of kept volume. That is the self-referential trap: V/OI pre-selection preferentially keeps rows with small open interest, which is exactly what inflates V/OI, and the small-OI rows are also the small-volume rows. **On 2026-10-29 the two cuts disagreed outright (CALL vs PUT); today they agree on direction (both call-leaning) but differ on how strong the lean is — MIXED at 1.05 versus CALL at 0.34.** The tag is a property of the ranking convention.
