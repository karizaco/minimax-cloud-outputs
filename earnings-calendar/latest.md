## Method notes

- TradingView official MCP (`user-TradingView`), one call at a time, at most 50 symbols each. US refresh: 5 earnings-calendar calls (50+50+50+50+8). Non-US refresh: 1 symbol-data-batch call (48). Enrichment: 2 symbol-data-batch calls for 80 in-window US names (50+30). Watchlist: 1 list + 1 get. **10 TradingView calls total**, all successful, no 429 or auth error.
- Refresh rule: no cache row, stale, unconfirmed, next date on or before 2026-11-15, or past. 256 symbols refreshed, 25 kept from cache, 23 funds/ETFs skipped (no_earnings).
- StockAnalysis (WebFetch) checked for all 89 in-window names. Where SA confirms a different in-window date than TradingView, the SA date is used and noted (e.g. SII: TV 2026-11-02, SA Nov 4). Soft-filtered when SA only has an estimate or a confirmed date outside the window (TNK SA Nov 11; MSZ SA Nov 19; CCCC/CRSP/IBRX/etc. estimates mid-November).
- Timing: calendar `release_next_time_flag`, else symbol-data `earnings_release_next_time`, else (non-US only) `earnings_release_time`; -1 is BMO, 1 is AMC, anything else unknown.
- Growth: only when both inputs exist and the last-quarter value is not 0; `n/m (tiny base)` when last-quarter EPS is under 0.05 in absolute value or growth exceeds 300%. Rev growth beyond 40% in either direction is flagged in notes.
