## Market backdrop

- This is a global AI trade unwind, not a US-only session. Overnight the TAIEX fell 6.2% (its worst week since 1991), the KOSPI 5.2%, the Nikkei 5.1% and the Hang Seng 1.9%; SoftBank dropped 14.5% and TSMC 4.8% in Taiwan.
- The trigger was Anthropic (ANT), which debuted on Nasdaq Wednesday, closed +17.07% at $97.17 on Thursday, and gave the entire AI complex back this morning. Nasdaq 100 futures were 1.5-1.6% lower at 07:03 ET, the Dow was flat, and the VIX touched 30 for the first time since 30 March.
- The second driver is rates. Hawkish Fed commentary — Philadelphia Fed President Anna Paulson signalling another hike may be needed — has the market pricing a 98.4% chance of a December rate HIKE, and the AI complex is the most rate-sensitive part of the market.
- The AI capex-versus-cash-flow argument is the stated reason for the de-rating: Microsoft's capex is up 30% to $45.2B with free cash flow down 27%, Alphabet's FCF was -$9.1B and Amazon's -$42.4B, and Nvidia's $227.3B buyback authorisation has not stemmed it.
- The session's macro calendar is the University of Michigan November consumer sentiment index at 10:00 ET. Sector detail: semis led (AMD about -7% after Thursday's -7.7% on a short-seller report about its China ties), and Nvidia has now closed lower six sessions running.

## Method, source health & caveats

- **Primary source:** TradingView public scanner (`POST https://scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`), run twice — `premarket_change` in [3,1000] sorted desc and in [-1000,-3] sorted asc — with `range: [0,2000]`. Both pulls returned `totalCount` equal to the rows returned (598 down, 135 up, 733 names total), so neither was truncated. The scan carries `type: stock` and `exchange != OTC` only: there is NO market-cap filter in the request, and the $200M floor is applied after the scan.
- **ntrt backend:** probed first with `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` → HTTP 000 (connection refused), so the run is Mode B, the screener fallback. The brief does not use any backend field.
- **Pre-open catalyst tape:** `preopen-feed/2026-11-20-0830.jsonl` (273 rows) from the local 08:30 checkpoint, usable rows 9 (`conf != low` and a `why` present). Four of them land on the published below-the-floor watchlist (VWAV, FCHL, GRSD, MEDS) and supply its notes; the other five (PARA, JDZG, ONFO, INHD, INLF) are names the gate did not publish. Its `ref` field is prose evidence and is never printed as a link; `news_url` is the citable URL. The tape is counted as a degraded source for SVRA, where its 08:30 price and the screener's 08:52 price disagree by a session's worth of move.
- **Secondary sources:** Reuters, CNBC, FT/Private Equity Wire, PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire, Seeking Alpha, Investing.com, StockTitan and 24/7 Wall St for the catalyst lookups; the 2026-11-19 Tier-A board from `minimax-cloud-outputs` for the prior short list. No Yahoo pre-market price or range was published.
- **Importance measure:** `premarket_volume / average_volume_30d_calc * 100` (the raw ratio, published to 3 dp in the CSV). The GATE compares that raw ratio against the session-normalised bar of 4.8788% (5.0% x 322/330). A name with no 30-day ADV history has an empty `pct_of_adv`, never a zero.
- **Market cap at the print:** the screener's `market_cap_basic` is a last-close figure, so share count is recovered as `market_cap_basic / prev_close` and multiplied by the pre-market print. `prev_close` is derived as `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change/100)` and was cross-checked against the screener's own `close` column, which matched on every published row.
- **Could not be verified:** the SVRA -22.2% print has no corroborating news and no second quote source; its cause is unknown. AXTI, TRON, ENHA, DGICB, PSA/PF, OSUR, SLBT, BUUU and CMTV have no confirmed catalyst either, and their Why cells are deliberately empty — a blank cell means 'not found', not 'there wasn't one'.
- **Delivery:** the run was suspended after the published pull and resumed on Saturday 21 November, so the brief was written and pushed about 18.5 hours after the session it describes. The movement check is a per-ticker re-read of the session's final pre-market print; the screener's universe query no longer serves a filtered screen outside the pre-market session, so the movement check was taken ticker by ticker and is therefore flags-only.
