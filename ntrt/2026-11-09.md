## Method, source health & caveats

- **Primary source:** the TradingView public scanner (`POST https://scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`), pulled at 09:02 ET with no market-cap filter in the request. Both sides came back complete: 390 rows against totalCount 390 on the gainers side and 216 against 216 on the decliners side, so the screen is not truncated. A second pull at 09:24 ET was made for movement flags only; it published no numbers.
- **Mode A failed:** the ntrt backend probe `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` returned HTTP 000 (connection refused), so the brief is built on the screener fallback.
- **Pre-open catalyst tape (cron #92):** the 08:30 file `preopen-feed/2026-11-09-0830.jsonl` was fetched, but it carries only 50 rows and its `pct` column is the last completed regular session's move, not today's pre-market gap — verified against closes for IRTC (-30.7% = 130.19 on Nov 5 to 90.22 on Nov 6), IREN, DKNG and KRMN. It was therefore used for catalyst leads only; no price or gap in this brief comes from it. The names it carries are mostly absent from today's pre-market screen.
- **Catalyst sourcing:** every published name was looked up individually with a dated source; drivers are SEC 8-K exhibits, company releases, Reuters, AP and MarketWatch-derived summaries. Six names produced no confirmable driver and their CSV catalyst cells are blank.
- **Numeric conventions:** gaps are the screener's premarket change versus the prior regular close; the prior close is derived as premarket print / (1 + gap/100), not read from the screener's `close` column, which after the open is a live intraday price. Prices are 2dp, markdown gaps 1dp, CSV gaps 2dp, %ADV 3dp in the CSV and 1dp in the markdown.
- **What could not be verified:** the market cap for SNUS/PH and AIOK (absent from the screener); the 30-day ADV for AIOK; and any driver for the six names listed in Flags.
- **Universe floor:** the $200M market-cap floor was applied after the scan, not in the request. Names it excluded are published in the below-the-floor watchlist, which is a watchlist only — those rows are not part of the asymmetry table.
