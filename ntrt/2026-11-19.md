**Filters held.** The screen was scanned **without** the market-cap filter so the $200M floor could be applied afterwards and the names it excludes could be published (§10). The floor is tested on the market cap **at the pre-market print** — the share count is recovered as `market_cap_basic / prev_close` and multiplied by the print — because the screener's `market_cap_basic` is a last-close figure and would let a sub-$200M shell sit in the tradeable table on a stale number.

**The denominators.** `pct_of_adv` is the **raw** ratio `premarket_volume / average_volume_30d_calc * 100`; the **gate** applies the session normalisation, so a name must clear **4.8788%** (5.0% × 322/330, the pre-market session prorated to the canonical 09:22 pull) of the raw 30-day ADV. The published `pct_of_adv` column stays raw so it is comparable with every earlier brief. `prev_close` is derived as `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change/100)`, never taken from the screener's `close` column, which carries the live intraday price once the session is open.
