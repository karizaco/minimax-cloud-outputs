## Method, source health & caveats

- **Primary source**: TradingView public scanner (`POST scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`), pulled twice — one side each for gaps ≥3% and ≤-3%. Both pulls were COMPLETE: 183 rows against totalCount 183 on the up side, 576 against 576 on the down side, one page each, no truncation. The scan carries no market-cap filter, so the $200M floor is applied afterwards and the names it excluded are published below.
- **ntrt backend probe**: `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` → HTTP 000 (connection refused), which is why this is Mode B. **degraded_sources: 1**
- **MCP catalog probe**: `catalog_ok total=135 finance_tools=0`; the MCP-over-HTTP bridge answered (market-recap) and supplied the prior closes and the overnight backdrop. Yahoo JSON was not used for any published price.
- **Pre-open catalyst tape**: `preopen-feed/2026-11-16-0830.jsonl`, 256 rows (local copy, above the 120-row regression threshold). It carries one usable driver for a published name (MBOT) and otherwise confirmed the direction of the tape — its prints sit within ~1 point of the screener's for every name it quotes. Its numbers are a second opinion, never the price of record.
- **Catalyst lookups**: one lookup per published name (39 names) via web_search, plus company press releases and RTTNews/Zacks/FT wires. The `catalyst` column of the CSV is populated for those 39 names and for the MBOT watchlist row; for the remaining screen rows it is EMPTY, which means "not looked up", not "no catalyst".
- **Denominators**: `% of 30d ADV` is the RAW ratio (pre-market volume ÷ `average_volume_30d_calc`); the 4.8788% bar is the session-normalised gate threshold and is a constant, not a moving number. Market cap is measured AT the pre-market print (shares recovered as cap ÷ prior close, × the print), because the screener's own cap is a last-close figure and would let a sub-$200M shell sit in the tradeable table.
- **What could not be verified**: a confirmed driver for 13 published names (listed in Flags), the identity of the driver behind TELUS's +22.6%, and any causal link for IEP's -12.2%.
