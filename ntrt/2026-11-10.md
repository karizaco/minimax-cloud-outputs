**Filters held.** The scan carried NO market-cap filter (`type=stock`, `exchange != OTC`, `premarket_change` band only), so no name was deleted by a column that can be null. The $200M liquidity floor was applied in post-processing, and the names it excluded are published in the `### Below the $200M floor` watchlist rather than lost.

**The pull was complete.** `range` was a page size, not a filter, and `totalCount` matched the rows returned on BOTH sides: gainers 198 = 198, losers 452 = 452, 650 names at ≥3% gap in total.

**Derived fields.** `prev_close` is derived as `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change / 100)` — the screener's `close` column is the live intraday price, not the prior close, and was never used. The %ADV denominator is `average_volume_30d_calc`, a 30-session daily ADV proxy (not an intraday one). Pre-market volume is the numerator and is the importance measure: a ±20% gap on 100 shares is a quote, not a market.

**What could not be verified.** No catalyst could be confirmed for 12 of the 50 published names — their `Why` cell reads "no catalyst confirmed" and their CSV `catalyst` cell is empty, which means *not found*, not *there wasn't one*. Two names move against their own catalyst (TTD, APPS) and two move against a miss (UONEK, TTEC); both are flagged above.
