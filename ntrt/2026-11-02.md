   proposal from the holder of 56.8% of the voting power is close to a certainty of a raised
   price, but the spread was still ~9% pre-open and the special committee had formed only hours

2. **PERI's ~2.5% spread to its $11.70 deal price is the market pricing the 40-day go-shop.**

   closing.
3. **LITE's decline was not a fundamentals story and could easily be misread as one.** Its beat
   was large and guidance was raised; the -4.6% pre-open gap reflected a fixed 4.5x ratio
   against a falling acquirer. Anyone trading the "good quarter" thesis in LITE was trading the
   wrong variable.
4. **DRUG's decline continued well past the pre-open.** The pre-open print was $30.00 (-45.4%);
   the stock traded to $25.02 by 09:38 ET and closed at $26.20 on the primary listing. Anyone
   sizing off the pre-market number alone was undressed by the follow-through.
5. **PGEN is a two-sided story, not a broken one.** Inovio's approval is real, but Papzimeos
   holds a head start (approved Aug 2025, 500+ patient-hub registrations, 51% surgery-free
   response vs 27% for Voceryq) and Precigen reports Q3 on 4 Nov. Retail sentiment into the
   print was 94/100 "extremely bullish" - a crowded position against a real regulatory fact.
6. **OFRM's decline was under-read pre-open.** The pre-open print was -16.8%; the actual move
   was -37.2%, the largest single-day decline in the screen. A recall headline is what it was,
   but the tape repriced much harder than the pre-open quote suggested.
7. **The oil move was the quiet macro story.** USO -5.5% and XLE -1.7% pre-open, dragging the
   whole Energy Minerals cohort (KOS, EGY, PARR, WTI, OBE) down. It reads as a sector-wide

8. **The market-cap floor hides the day's largest movers.** Dropping the $200M floor surfaces
   INO **+230.4%** (the FDA approval that also sank PGEN), CLRO +95.9%, and KPTI **-75.9%**.

## Method

- Screener run **twice** so both sides are carried - `premarket_change` in `[3,1000]` sorted
  desc, and `[-1000,-3]` sorted asc - then ranked by **absolute** `premarket_change`. A
  descending-only sort would have published only gainers.
- `type: "stock"` **includes REITs**; the result was not re-filtered to common stock.
- `exchange != OTC` and `market_cap_basic >= $200M` floors applied as specified.
- **`premarket_volume` is the importance measure.** It is published as % of
  `average_volume_30d_calc` on every row. No pre-market price or range is published from a
  second source without a screener cross-check, and no Yahoo pre-market range is published at all.
- **Prev close is derived, not assumed.** TradingView's `premarket_change` is measured against
  the 30 Oct close; the reference close shown is back-solved from
  `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change/100)` and then verified against the MCP bridge quote
  and, for DRUG, the Nasdaq quote page.
- Two captures were taken: a 09:03 ET snapshot and a final pre-open snapshot. The final
  pre-open print is published; where the two disagreed materially (BRAI +9.8% -> +4.1%,
  DRUG -48.3% -> -45.4%, OFRM -11.3% -> -16.8%) the move is noted, because a gap that changes
  that much while the clock runs is itself information about the tape's quality.
- Deep dives capped at 5 per the skill; the 14 catalysts above were obtained from primary
  sources without per-ticker dossier calls.
