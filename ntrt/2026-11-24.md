## Method, source health & caveats

- **Primary source — TradingView public scanner** (`POST https://scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`): answered, both sides pulled, `range [0,2000]`, `totalCount` == rows returned on both sides (198 up, 447 down, 645 names at a 3% gap or wider). No market-cap filter in the request.
- **ntrt backend** (`GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health`): HTTP 000 — unreachable, so Mode B. `NTRT_BASE_URL` was unset and no auth token was configured.
- **MCP probe**: `catalog_ok total=135 finance_tools=0` — no financial MCP is registered in this runtime and the MCP-over-HTTP bridge answered with `packs=tradier,marketdata-app,market-recap`. The absent `mcp__*` tools are not an error.
- **Pre-open catalyst tape** (`preopen-feed/2026-11-24-0830.jsonl`, local copy): 209 rows, inside the normal band for this capture — not a short-tape regression. It supplied dated events for CRMT, GMEX, SAFX, EVRL and ABTS, shown in the watchlist notes; every published name in the two screened tables was looked up separately.
- **Identity**: every published name's company came from the screener's own `description` column before any lookup — BGIN is Bgin Blockchain (not BG Industries) and INN is Summit Hotel Properties (not InnPhase).
- **Second searches**: every move of 10% or more in absolute terms got a second, differently-worded search — INSM, FLNC, ANF, EMBC, HPE, ADI, BGIN, INN, MRAM, ALTO. Where the first driver could not explain the size of the move, the mismatch is flagged above rather than smoothed over.
- **Filters held**: `type=stock` (REITs included), `exchange != OTC`, `premarket_change` in [3,1000] and [-1000,-3], no market-cap filter in the request. The $200M floor, the 4.8788% session-normalised ADV bar and the 25,000-share print floor are applied in post-processing.
- **Importance measure**: `premarket_volume / average_volume_30d_calc`. `pct_of_adv` is the RAW ratio; the gate applies the constant 4.8788% bar (5.0% x 322/330). Market caps are derived at the pre-market print (share count recovered from the last-close cap, times the print), not taken from the screener's last-close column.
- **What could not be verified**: no second source carries pre-market volume, so no independent check on the %ADV column was possible; every catalyst was read from a dated filing, press release or wire story and none was confirmed against a second market-data feed.
