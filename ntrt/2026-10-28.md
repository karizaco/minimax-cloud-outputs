### Method & data caveats

- The universe came from Yahoo's predefined screener across seven lists (day_gainers, day_losers, most_actives, small_cap_gainers, most_shorted_stocks, undervalued_growth_stocks, growth_technology_stocks): **1,246 unique symbols, 908 with a pre-market print, 516 with a print ≤30 minutes old.** Ranked on `preMarketChangePercent` — *not* on the prior session's move, because the session-based lists would have hidden the day's best pre-market movers. Names that gapped on news without a prior-session move (ULBI, LOGI) are absent from the screener universe and were added from press coverage, then verified on the tape.
- The 10% floor on market cap used the TradingView `market_cap_basic` field, which for US listings is in USD. 86 names qualified at ≥$200M and a ≥3% gap (57 up, 29 down).
- **TradingView's `relative_volume_10d_calc` is a daily proxy, not intraday relative volume**, and it runs systematically ~5–20% below a true trailing 10-session average, so it flatters high-RVOL names. The "PM vol (% of 3M ADV)" column is the more honest importance measure and is computed from Yahoo's `averageDailyVolume3Month` against the Nasdaq pre-market volume counter.
- **The TradingView `america` scanner has no pre-market columns** — `premarket_price`, `premarket_change_percent` and `premarket_volume` all returned null across the universe. It was used for sector, industry, market cap and relative volume only.
- Pre-market percentages are taken from the Nasdaq `extended-trading` composite string; where the composite and the last trade disagree by more than ~1.5pp (VRT, BSX, TER, IP, LOGI), the last trade is shown separately in the table's "PM last" column so the reader can see the spread.
- Yahoo's `chart`, `spark` and custom screener POST endpoints were not used: this runtime is rate-limited on them (HTTP 429). The **predefined** screener endpoint answered 200 without a cookie or crumb and carries the live pre-market fields, which is what made a market-wide screen possible at all.
- Catalyst language is taken from morning press (Reuters, Barron's, Seeking Alpha, Investing.com, CNBC, Benzinga, company releases) and cross-checked against at least two independent outlets for each of the five deep dives. Where no catalyst could be confirmed, the row says so rather than guessing.
- No trading advice is given or implied. Nothing here is an instruction to transact.
