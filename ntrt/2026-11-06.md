## Method, source health & caveats

- **Primary source:** TradingView public screener (`scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`), two pulls — 09:04 ET (the published screen) and 09:26 ET (delta only). Both sides pulled with `range [0,2000]`; `totalCount` matched rows returned on both (594 up, 240 down), so the pull is complete.
- **Backend probe:** `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` → HTTP 000 (connection refused), no `NTRT_BASE_URL` set, so Mode B applies. The ntrt backend contributed nothing to this brief.
- **Pre-open catalyst tape:** fetched, 849 rows from `preopen-feed/2026-11-06-0830.jsonl`; 8 rows carried a `grok` catalyst with a source URL (NET, IREN, DKNG, FROG, ETON, LASR, ABNB, SNT). The other 841 rows are scanner rows with `conf: low` and no source, so they were not used as catalysts.
- **Catalyst lookups:** every published name was checked against the tape, the issuer's own press-release page and news search. 38 of the 48 checked names have a sourced driver; the 10 that do not are named in Flags with an empty `Why` cell — a blank there means *not found*, not *there wasn't one*.
- **Prev close was derived, never taken from the live `close` column** (which is intraday once the session opens): `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change/100)`. Verified against the screener's own pre-open close column on all 834 names — 834/834 agree within $0.02.
- **Importance measure:** `premarket_volume / average_volume_30d_calc`. Yahoo is not used for any published price or volume — Yahoo pre-market bars carry zero volume and its tape goes stale on thin names.
- **Not verified:** the 4,827 names between ±3% (never screened, so absent by construction), and the %ADV denominator for names with no 30-day history (3 names: DAVI, RZAI, CDLR — `pct_of_adv` left empty, never zero).
- **Floor:** the $200M market-cap floor was applied after the scan, not inside the request; the names it excluded are published in the watchlist, with no catalyst lookup and `watchlist (not checked)` in the CSV.
