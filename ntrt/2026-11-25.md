Catalyst lookups — `web_search`, one per published name, plus a second, differently-worded search for every move of ≥10% absolute. Yahoo was not used for prices: it publishes no pre-market volume and its pre-market tape goes stale on thin names.

**Filters and conventions.** The screen is `type = stock` (REITs included) with `exchange != OTC`; the $200M floor is applied after the scan, so no name is deleted by a filter on a null column. `prev_close` is derived as `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change/100)`, and the market cap is derived at the pre-market print (share count from the screener's last-close cap × the print), so the price, the cap and the gate in a row all describe the same instant. The %ADV bar is the constant 4.8788% of the raw 30-day ADV (5.0% × the 322/330 session factor); the published `pct_of_adv` column stays raw, so it is comparable with every earlier brief.

**What could not be verified.** The pre-market prints for SPRY and METAM conflict with independent quotes and with the screener's own identity and cap fields, and they are published as the screener shows them, flagged above. No catalyst could be confirmed for the names whose `Why` cell is empty; those rows are flagged, not filled in. Degraded sources: 1.
