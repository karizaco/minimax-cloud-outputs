## Method, source health & caveats
- **Primary source:** TradingView public scanner `POST https://scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`, pulled once for the published numbers (09:21 ET) and once at ~09:00 ET for research only. Both sides were pulled with `range [0,2000]`; `totalCount` matched the rows returned on both sides (289 up, 188 down), so the screen is complete and not truncated.
- **ntrt backend probe:** `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` → HTTP 000 (connection refused). Mode B was used, as the procedure specifies; this is the designed fallback, not a degraded source.
- **Pre-open tape:** `preopen-feed/2026-11-13-0830.jsonl`, 164 rows, 9 with a usable driver (`conf != low` and a `why`). It supplied the drivers for MOD, CADL, CBRS and VTSI — but those rows carry no `news_url`, so the drivers are publishable without a citable article behind them. This is one degraded source. Every other published name was looked up with a same-day news search.
- **Market backdrop:** MCP bridge `market-recap` (`get_quotes`, `market_snapshot`) for futures, rates, oil, gold, FX and the prior close. **Catalog probe:** no financial MCP is registered on this runtime, which is expected and is not a finding.
- **Denominators and derivations:** `avg_volume_30d` is the screener's 30-session average, published as an integer and used as the denominator of `pct_of_adv` (raw, 3 dp). The gate's bar is the constant 4.8788% of that raw ratio — 5.0% of the ADV prorated to the 322 of the 330 pre-market minutes that have elapsed at the 09:21 ET pull. `prev_close` is derived as `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change/100)`; `mkt_cap_usd` is the cap **at the pre-market print** (screener cap scaled by the move), and the $200M floor is tested on that number.
- **What could not be verified:** no citable URL behind the MOD/CADL/CBRS drivers; 4 published names have no confirmed catalyst at all (blank cells, listed in Flags); the screener's pre-market volume column is implausible for at least three sub-$200M names, so those %ADV figures should not be read as real participation.
- A **delta pull at ~09:27 ET** could not be completed before this artifact was written, so there are no movement flags between the published pull and the open; the published numbers are the 09:21 ET pull.
