- **The deal spread is the real number: ZS trades ~3.4% BELOW the deal's implied value.** At the exchange ratio (1.135 CRWD per ZS share) and CRWD's pre-market price of $211.26, the terms imply $239.78 per ZS share against a $232.00 print. A stock-for-stock deal usually trades at a premium to its implied value; a discount this wide is the market pricing deal risk, and it is why the acquirer is being sold as hard as the target is being bought.

## Method, source health & caveats

- **Primary screen:** TradingView public scanner (`POST https://scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`), pulled twice — the ≥3% gainers side and the ≥3% decliners side — with **no market-cap filter in the request** and `range [0,2000]`. Both pulls completed: `totalCount` matched the rows returned on each side (1271 names in total), so the screen is complete and not silently half a screen. Published pull clock: **08:55 ET**.
- **Backend:** `NTRT_BASE_URL` unset; `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` → **HTTP 000** (connection refused), so the run is Mode B. The MCP-over-HTTP bridge served the backdrop quotes and the macro calendar; the MCP availability probe reports `catalog_ok total=135 finance_tools=0`.
- **Pre-open catalyst tape:** present locally (`preopen-feed/2026-11-23-0830.jsonl`, 342 rows) and used for the watchlist rows it covers. Its `conf: low` rows were treated as **leads, not catalysts** — every published catalyst came from a named search, and the tape's `news_url` (not its `ref`, which is prose evidence) is the citable link.
- **Identity:** every published name's company came from the screener's own `description` column before any lookup (one targeted pull, 44 names, coverage asserted), so no ticker was ever expanded by guesswork. A second, differently-worded search was run on every move of ≥10% or where the first driver could not explain the size of the move.
- **Importance measure:** `pct_of_adv` is RAW — pre-market shares ÷ the 30-session average daily volume, published to 3 decimals in the CSV. The gate applies the session-normalised bar of **4.8788%** (5.0% × 322/330) to that raw ratio; the bar is a constant and does not move with the pull minute. Market caps are **derived at the pre-market print** (screener cap ÷ prior close × print), never taken from the screener's last-close column.
- **Catalyst coverage:** every actionable and wider-screen name was looked up (36 names). 16 of 16 actionable names and 16 of 20 wider-screen names have a sourced driver. The CSV's `catalyst` column carries a driver for those names, `watchlist (not checked)` for the below-the-floor rows, and `not checked (not published)` for the remaining screen rows — a blank would wrongly mean "no catalyst existed". On a published name a blank cell means the lookup found nothing.
- **Not verified:** the screen names that no table publishes were not looked up, so their CSV rows carry no driver; no second source was used for any price — the screener is the single source for every gap, print, share count and cap in this brief; and the `Why` cells for the sector-driven names (AEHR, MRAM, IREN, THM, TRX, EXK, AGL, CYPH, ASST) carry a market-wide driver rather than a company release.
