## Method, source health & caveats

- **Primary source:** TradingView public scanner, `POST https://scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`, two pulls (gainers `premarket_change` in [3,1000] desc, losers in [-1000,-3]
  asc), `type: stock`, `exchange != OTC`, **no market-cap filter in the request**, `range`
  [0,2000]. Both pulls were COMPLETE: 196/196 and 497/497 rows against `totalCount`. The row
  key is the bare ticker (`d[0]`), never the venue-prefixed `s`.
- **ntrt backend:** `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` → HTTP 000 (connection refused). The brief
  is Mode B, the screener fallback, and every number here comes from that pull.
- **Pre-open tape: short (47 rows).** Cron #92's 08:30 ET checkpoint is far smaller than a
  movers-sorted snapshot and covers only a fraction of the screen, so it was not treated as the
  day's screen; every catalyst was sourced by an independent news search instead. The tape's own
  numbers are also a second opinion, not the price of record — the screener's number is published.
- **Importance measure:** `premarket_volume / average_volume_30d_calc`, published as % of 30-day
  ADV. `average_volume_30d_calc` is a daily proxy, not an intraday one.
- **Market cap is measured AT THE PRE-MARKET PRINT** (share count recovered as
  `market_cap_basic / prev_close`, multiplied by the print) and the $200M floor is tested against
  that number, so the gate, the cap cell and the published price describe the same instant.
  `prev_close` is derived as `premarket_close / (1 + premarket_change/100)`, never read from the
  screener's `close` column, which carries the live intraday price once the session is open.
- **Delta pull at 09:24 ET** (about six minutes before the open, both sides complete: 195/195 and
  572/572) was used for movement flags only — it never changes a published number. The tables
  above carry the 09:04 ET pull.
- **What could not be verified:** six published names (HYMC, RZLV, SMHI, FTK, CNCK, SHAZ) had no
  catalyst confirmable from a named source, so their `Why` cell is blank and their CSV `catalyst`
  cell is empty — a blank means "not found", not "there wasn't one". The market cap on PARA is
  not trustworthy at all (see Flags). Yahoo's pre-market tape was not used for any price: it
  carries no pre-market volume and goes stale on thin names.
- The CSV companion carries all 693 names at 3% or more, including the sub-floor ones and
  every name the tables above do not publish; a row outside the published set carries
  `not published (not checked)` in its `catalyst` column, and a watchlist row carries
  `watchlist (not checked)`.
- **Audit:** `brief_rendered target=ntrt-premarket:2026-11-12 mode=B`.
- **degraded_sources: 1** — the pre-open catalyst tape (short, 47 rows). The ntrt backend's HTTP
  000 is the documented Mode B fallback and cost the run nothing: the screener pull was complete
  and every published number comes from it.
