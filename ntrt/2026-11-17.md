## Method, source health & caveats

- **Primary source**: TradingView public scanner (`POST scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`), pulled twice — one side each for gaps ≥3% and ≤-3%. Both pulls were COMPLETE: 506 rows against totalCount 506 (267 up, 239 down), one page each, no truncation. The scan carries no market-cap filter, so the $200M floor is applied afterwards and the names it excluded are published below.
- **ntrt backend probe**: `GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` → HTTP 000 (connection refused), which is why this is Mode B. **degraded_sources: 1**
- **MCP catalog probe**: `catalog_ok total=135 finance_tools=0`; the MCP-over-HTTP bridge answered (market-recap) and supplied the settled closes, futures, yields and the overnight backdrop. Yahoo JSON was not used for any published price.
- **Pre-open catalyst tape**: `preopen-feed/2026-11-17-0830.jsonl`, 159 rows (above the 120-row regression threshold). It supplied usable drivers for RCAT, GP, FRTT, BAER and CYAB and a low-confidence lead for AYTU; its prints are a second opinion, never the price of record.
- **Catalyst lookups**: one lookup per published name (34 names) plus the watchlist rows that carry a dated PR, via web_search and company/press wires. The `catalyst` column of the CSV is populated for the 25 names with a sourced driver and EMPTY for the 9 checked names with no confirmed catalyst — an empty cell means "not found", not "not looked up". Below-the-floor rows read `watchlist (not checked)` unless the tape or a dated PR supplied a driver.
- **Denominators**: `% of 30d ADV` is the RAW ratio (pre-market volume ÷ `average_volume_30d_calc`); the 4.8788% bar is the session-normalised gate threshold and is a constant, not a moving number. Market cap is measured AT the pre-market print (shares recovered as cap ÷ prior close, × the print), because the screener's own cap is a last-close figure and would let a sub-$200M shell sit in the tradeable table.
- **What could not be verified**: a driver for 9 published names (listed in Flags), the pre-market cause of the MFP, GDHG, PAVS and ABX prints, and any link between the tape's 08:30 prints and the published pull for the 21 names the tape did not quote.
