## Catalysts

**The dominant theme is guidance quality, not earnings.** Nine of the twelve deep-dive names
missed or met on the top line but were sold or bought on the **forward** number — the guidance
midpoint, the contract-value line, or the margin. AAPL, PLTR, ZBRA, DT and SHOP all beat or met
revenue and were still crushed. That is the tape's shape today, and it is worth carrying into the
open.

- **SHOP (-15.6%)** — the most consequential single print. Revenue and EPS were fine; **GMV missed
  and decelerated from 31.6% to 29.5% y/y**. Because merchant revenue is a take-rate on GMV, the
  market is pricing a slower top line into 2027. FY26 guide of only "low-30s%" revenue growth did
  not help.
- **PLTR (-5.9%)** — Q3 revenue +96% to $2.31B and an EPS beat, and both FY26 operating-profit and
  FCF guidance were *raised*. The sell-off is entirely on **total contract value growth decelerating
  to +6% from +49%** (US commercial +11%). This is a bookings-slowdown story, not a demand story.
- **AAPL (-4.3%)** — Q4 profit beat; the gross-margin outlook disappointed. ~$190B of market cap
  gone pre-open.
- **DT (-15.1%)** — revenue and ARR both beat and the ARR/revenue guidance was *raised*, but FY27
  adj EPS was cut to $1.88–1.90 against a $1.98 consensus and FCF to $600–605M from $610–615M.

## Method & caveats

- **Primary source:** TradingView public scanner, `POST https://scanner.tradingview.com/america/scan`,
  run **twice** — `premarket_change` in `[3,1000]` (desc) and `[-1000,-3]` (asc) — then merged and
  ranked by **absolute** `premarket_change`, so both the gain and the decline side are carried.
  210 gainers + 51 decliners = **261 names**.
- **Filters held exactly as specified:** `type = stock` (**REITs included — do not re-filter**),
  `exchange != OTC`, `market_cap_basic >= 200,000,000`, gap ≥ 3%.
- **Importance measure:** `premarket_volume ÷ average_volume_30d_calc` = % of 30-day ADV. This is
  the only column that separates a market from a quote, and it is why the two tables above differ
  from a naive gap ranking.
- **Prior-close verification:** every prior close used for the gap was re-checked against Yahoo
  daily bars for Nov 2 — BLZE, SHOP, ADEA, BRAI, AEHR, DT, ZBRA, GRAB, LSCC, FISV, EBS, PLTR and
  AAPL all matched to the cent. The gap base is sound.
- **Yahoo pre-market prices and ranges were deliberately NOT published.** Yahoo's pre-market tape
  goes stale on thin names and returns volume 0 on every pre-market bar, so it cannot source this
  brief's importance column at all. Where Yahoo was used, it was only for the *prior regular
  close*, which is a settled regular-session number.
- **Lag:** TradingView pre-market columns are live as of the scan; the screener holds only the
  most recent session, so this brief cannot be rebuilt for an older date from this source.
- **Catalyst sourcing:** each "why" was sourced from the issuer's own release/IR page or a
  wire/major outlet, never from memory. Where I could not confirm a catalyst, the row says so.
- **Not used:** the ntrt backend (`NTRT_BASE_URL` unset; `GET /health` on the default
  `http://127.0.0.1:8000` returned HTTP 000), the `mcp__*` finance tools (not registered here —
  0 finance tools in the catalog), and Finviz/Yahoo HTML scraping.
