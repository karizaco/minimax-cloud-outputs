## FOMC minutes — what actually landed (Wed 2:00 p.m. ET)

Source: Federal Reserve, *Minutes of the Federal Open Market Committee, October 27–28, 2026* (released 11/18/2026, 2:00 p.m. ET).

- **Decision:** target range held at **3.75%–4.00%**; ample-reserves policy reaffirmed.
- **Vote: 10–2.** Voting to hold: Warsh, Williams, Barr, Bowman, Jefferson, Kashkari, Logan, Paulson, Powell, Waller. **Dissenting: Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack, both preferring a 25bp increase to 4.00%–4.25%.**
- **Direction of travel is hawkish:** almost all participants judged that, under their baseline outlooks, **further rate increases would likely be appropriate** in coming months; several saw the hold as a close call, and some said an increase **larger than 25bp** could be considered at the next meeting if incoming inflation data were unfavourable.
- **Inflation:** staff estimated September **headline PCE 3.6%, core PCE 3.1%**, both above year-earlier levels (tariffs, energy, AI-driven goods demand). Participants expected inflation to decline toward 2% as tariff and energy effects waned — but the **vast majority saw upside risks**.
- **Labour market:** unemployment **4.2%** in September, payrolls averaging ~**50k/month** over the prior three months, average hourly earnings **+3.0% y/y**; described as stable but with unusually low dynamism (low hiring and low layoffs).
- **Read-through:** this is a committee whose default path is more tightening, not less — the risk framing into the **Dec 8–9** meeting is one of a higher-for-longer path, and the two dissents are the hawkish tail of the distribution.
