## Method & caveats

- **No FRED MCP is registered in this runtime** (`probe_mcp.py` → `catalog_ok total=135 finance_tools=0`), so the skill's preferred source was unavailable and the documented **web_fallback** path was used. The MCP-over-HTTP bridge answered (`bridge_ok packs=tradier,fmp,market-recap`) but carries no macro release schedule, so it was not used for the calendar.
- **Consensus source:** themarketmonitors.com/economic-calendar (fetched 2026-11-15). Their printed clock is **GMT despite the "ET" label** — every time was converted to ET by subtracting 5 hours; the conversion was validated against three independent anchors (Empire State and retail sales 13:30 → 08:30 ET; FOMC minutes 19:00 → 14:00 ET; Fed speeches vs the Fed's own calendar). MetaCap's tables carry the same 5-hour offset and were used only as a **cross-check on forecast/prior values**, not as the published clock.
- **Time disagreements across sources are real and unresolved for three items** ⚠: (1) *Empire State consensus* — 17.6 (themarketmonitors, MetaCap) vs 19.4 (ThriveInMarkets); published 17.6. (2) *Retail control group* — +0.4% (Newsquawk, MetaCap "core") vs +0.1% (themarketmonitors); published +0.4%. (3) *Housing starts* — 1.23M (themarketmonitors) vs 1.25M (MetaCap); published 1.23M. (4) *Initial claims* — 199K (MetaCap) vs 200K (themarketmonitors); published 199K. Each divergence is a different consensus survey, not a data error.
- **Waller on Tuesday** carries two published times: 09:20 ET (themarketmonitors) and 12:00 ET (the Fed's own calendar lists a Waller speech at the Clearing House Annual Conference on 17 Nov). Both are shown; the pair may be one event rescheduled or two separate appearances. The Fed's own calendar is the source of record for the Friday 08:25 ET Waller slot.
- **No separate consensus for the Friday Michigan final** was published — the 51.0 shown is the preliminary reading carried forward, not a new consensus estimate. Inflation-expectation sub-indices likewise carry preliminary values.
- **Auction sizes for the bills are not published in advance** (only prior yields); the 10-year TIPS reopening size is absent from the refunding statement and is left as `—` rather than estimated.
- **Auction schedule is taken from Treasury's own tentative Q4 2026 schedule** (PDF + XML), not from a calendar aggregator, because aggregators omitted the 6-week, 17-week and 2-year FRN/notes entries in this window.
- **Non-macro catalysts, listed for completeness only** (earnings are out of scope for this routine): Nvidia, Home Depot, Disney, Target, Lowe's, TJX, Palo Alto Networks, Walmart and Intuit report during the week, mostly after Tuesday's close; Anthropic is expected to IPO.
- **Not verified:** no source in this run confirmed a TIPS auction size, a Wednesday dollar-auction size, or a consensus for Atlanta Fed GDPNow beyond the previous print.
