## Beige Book, November 2026 (released 2:00 p.m. ET today)

Source: Federal Reserve, *Beige Book* (Nov 25, 2026) — national summary plus district reports.

- **Overall activity:** eight of twelve Districts (New York, Philadelphia, Cleveland, Richmond,
  Atlanta, St. Louis, Minneapolis, Dallas) reported **slight-to-moderate growth**; four (Boston,
  Chicago, Kansas City, San Francisco) reported **little or no change**.
- **Consumer:** spending **little changed** — households price-sensitive and more selective on
  discretionary purchases. Tourism and travel solid; entertainment and hospitality mixed; **auto
  sales softened** on higher fuel prices and interest rates.
- **Housing and construction:** residential real estate **weakened** on affordability; construction
  **declined** as borrowing and construction costs restrained activity.
- **Manufacturing:** grew modestly — strong in some industrial segments, weak in consumer-facing and
  housing-related goods. Transportation up modestly; **port volumes dipped on weather**, though
  data-centre-related imports increased.
- **Labour:** employment rose slightly; firms mostly replaced attrition, with only a minority hiring
  meaningfully. Workers generally easy to find, but skilled/technical roles remain persistently hard
  to fill.
- **Wages:** rose **moderately, accelerating from modest** in the previous report. Pay pressure
  concentrated in hard-to-fill positions and in cost-of-living adjustments.
- **Prices:** rose moderately overall, **unchanged** from the prior edition. Persistent cost pressure
  from energy, transportation, healthcare, insurance and **tariff-related** expenses. Critically:
  **input-cost growth generally exceeded selling-price growth** — margins, not prices, are being
  squeezed. Contacts expect both costs and prices to keep rising.
- **District colour:** Dallas — expansion slowed to a modest pace, wage growth accelerated, price
  growth elevated. Philadelphia — slight growth, wage inflation accelerated to moderate. St. Louis —
  modest activity, employment unchanged, **prices rose at a robust pace**. Kansas City — little
  changed; consumer-facing revenue lower as affordability strains bite.
- **Outlook:** most Districts expect slight-to-moderate growth. Cleveland and Dallas positive;
  Philadelphia positive but diminished across all firms; St. Louis neutral.
