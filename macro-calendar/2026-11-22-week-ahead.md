## Method & caveats

- **No FRED MCP is registered in this runtime** (`probe_mcp.py` → `catalog_ok total=135 finance_tools=0`), so the skill's preferred source was unavailable and the documented fallback path was used. The **MCP-over-HTTP bridge did carry live macro packs** this run — `us-econ-calendar` (release-schedule pack), `fed-comms` (FOMC calendar) and `fred` (release dates) — and those were used to **confirm** dates rather than to enumerate the week.
- **What the bridge confirmed:** the **jobless claims shift to Wed 25 Nov** (the pack's holiday-override logic: "Shifted from the usual Thursday (2026-11-26) because that day is a federal holiday"); the **Beige Book on Wed 25 Nov 14:00 ET**; the **2026 FOMC calendar** (8 meetings; the 27–28 Oct meeting's minutes were released 18 Nov, so **no minutes fall this week**, and the next decision is 8–9 Dec); the **H.6 money stock measures on Wed 25 Nov 13:00 ET**. The `fred` pack's `fred_release_dates` returns *series* release schedules (H.15, SOFR, CPI series), not the macro release calendar, so it was not used for the event list.
- **Event list sources (all public, all ET-labelled):** the Scotiabank Economics calendar of economic release dates for November 2026; the BEA release schedule (confirming GDP 2nd estimate and personal income and outlays both on 25 Nov at 08:30); the Census advance economic indicators schedule; the Federal Reserve's own November calendar page and `calendar.json`; Treasury's tentative auction schedule XML and the quarterly refunding statement; and three public economic calendars (nowflation.com, researchfrc.com, helious.io W48) for consensus and prior values. Full status table in **Source health**.
- **Prior values were taken from the agency figures quoted in the same week's previews** (BEA advance GDP 3.4%, September PCE +0.5% m/m / +3.6% y/y and core +0.3% / +3.1%, Census September durable goods +1.8%, DOL claims 203K, Census September new home sales 676K) rather than re-derived from data series.
- **One unresolved divergence ⚠:** the September durable-goods prior is **+1.8%** (Census, as quoted in the week-ahead preview) versus **+1.9%** (helious W48); **+1.8% is published**, and the consensus (+0.5%) is identical across the sources.
- **7-year auction time:** the XML confirms the **25 Nov auction date** but not the hour; the **11:30 ET** bids-due time (moved up from the usual 13:00 for the day before Thanksgiving) comes from a single week-ahead preview and is flagged ⚠ in the table.
- **Bill auction sizes** for the 4-week, 8-week and 17-week bills are not published in advance and are left as `—` rather than estimated.
- **Fed speakers:** the Board's calendar shows none for 23–27 Nov. **fedcal.io could not be scraped** (JS-rendered page, empty body) and other reserve banks' calendars were not checked exhaustively, so a regional-bank speech outside the Board's calendar could exist and is **not** ruled out. The NY Fed Bronx visit on 23 Nov is a regional visit, not a scheduled policy speech.
- **Fed funds target** and the 10–2 vote are from the Fed's own **28 Oct 2026 statement** (fed.gov), not carried forward from a prior run.
- **Market context** is the Friday 20 Nov close, from AP / CNBC / Investopedia / Dow Jones, cross-checked against the bridge's `market_snapshot` (as of 2026-11-22T20:03Z). Futures and the Asian indexes in that snapshot are Friday's settled prints, not a live Sunday session.
- **Out of scope / omitted deliberately:** earnings (handled by the earnings routines), CFTC Commitments of Traders (not present in any fetched source this week — **omitted rather than published from memory**), and international macro (ECB / BOJ).
- **Not verified:** no published consensus for the Richmond Fed index, the Chicago Fed NFCI, the Case-Shiller m/m (single source), the H.6, or the API crude build; and no auction size for the 4/8/17-week bills.
