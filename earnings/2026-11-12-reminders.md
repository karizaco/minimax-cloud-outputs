## Rules applied

- S&P 500 membership verified against the live Wikipedia constituent list (503 symbols, all five control symbols present) - never from memory.
- Every published report date confirmed on an issuer-owned page or a regulatory filing; the one name whose date only aggregators carried for Nov 12 (WSM) was excluded, and the names whose ET day could not be fixed (MUFG, SMFG, Mizuho) or falls outside the Nov 12 ET day (Alibaba, Tencent) were excluded and recorded.
- The ET day is decided by converting the issuer's own local release time with `zoneinfo` and comparing it with the 06:00 ET fire instant - never from the local calendar date and never against the wall clock.
- Reaction columns measured from Yahoo daily bars, Nasdaq past report dates and Cboe option chains; every value that could not be measured is published as n/a and counted, never estimated from market cap or a peer.
- One source per field, with the source named above; spreads beyond 5% on market cap and 2% on EPS are flagged in the notes.
- The roster is sorted by reaction score descending, and the score equals 0.6 x average absolute move + 0.4 x implied move, with a missing implied move substituted by the average absolute move.
- The three content tables do not overlap: the US roster, the non-US table and the band each carry their own names, and a name already in the US roster is never repeated in the non-US table.
