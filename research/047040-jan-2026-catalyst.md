# Daewoo E&C (KRX 047040) — the January 2026 rally: what actually drove it

Research note prepared 2026-10-31. Covers the Thu 2026-01 session, the catalyst chain, and the
link to the 2026-10-29 collapse that the cron #02 brief reported.

## Short answer

**Both, but in a specific order — and the nuclear leg was the one that mattered.**

- **Jan 16 (+13.35%):** Daewoo E&C's own **AI data-centre MOU with Jeollanam-do** (500MW across
  Jangseong 200MW + Gangjin 300MW, Daewoo as EPC prime) *plus* the **nuclear theme** (Presidential
  Policy Chief Kim Yong-beom's Jan 14 press remarks that new reactor construction is unavoidable
  because semiconductors/AI cannot get enough power).
- **Jan 20 (+11.31%, heaviest volume of the month):** **President Lee Jae-myung instructed
  discussions on building additional new reactors** and pushed for early Korea-US nuclear
  cooperation talks. Sector-wide limit-up rally.
- **Jan 21–23:** Mirae Asset lifted its target price ₩5,400 → ₩6,500 ("Ready for entry into the
  global nuclear market"); KHNP's stake in TerraPower confirmed. Stock broke above its ₩5,000 par
  value.
- **Jan 26 (−6.77%):** the actual policy decision (two new large reactors proceed) — sell the news.

**The data-centre link was real in January but it ran through AI *power demand*, not through
construction.** Daewoo's own data-centre construction business only became a driver in
July–August 2026 (Korea Herald Jul 9; "Korea Construction Stocks Surge 33% on Nuclear, AI Data
Center Orders", Seoul Economic Daily Aug 31). In January the market was trading **nuclear export
optionality** — specifically the expectation that Daewoo E&C would finally sign the **construction
("C") subcontract** for the Czech Dukovany project.

**That contract was never signed.** The Team Korea main contract with CEZ was signed **June 2025**;
KEPCO E&C took the engineering (E) scope and Doosan Enerbility the procurement (P) scope in
**December 2025**; the construction (C) scope — Daewoo E&C's — was still unsigned in January 2026
and was still unsigned on 2026-10-29, when KHNP ended the talks and the stock fell **18.57%**.
January 2026 was the same narrative that broke in October.

## Why the construction (C) contract never landed

- KHNP sought to cut the consortium's original bid by **more than 10% (~₩1trn)**; the dispute was
  the calculation of construction costs and who carries the risk from design changes that expanded
  the project from one unit to two.
- The consortium's position: it had offered the **same construction rates as its January 2024 bid**,
  and on the Oct 29 deadline filed a revised offer low enough to absorb the loss itself.
- Contract value reference: ~₩27trn (~$18–22.8bn), which KHNP said was **2.62x the UAE Barakah
  plant on a two-unit basis** — the source of the "low-bid" criticism.
- Timeline after the break: KHNP targets a new contractor by **July 2027**, with site preparation
  still due to start January 2028.
- Doosan Enerbility said it will **rebid**; Daewoo E&C — the consortium lead — called the breakdown
  "deeply regrettable" and asked KHNP to return to the table.

## Method and caveats

- Daily OHLCV for January 2026 from Yahoo Finance history (`web_fetch` on
  `finance.yahoo.com/quote/047040.KS/history/`, period-bounded). The Yahoo JSON chart API and
  stooq were both unavailable from this egress (429 / TLS failure), so the HTML history page was
  the working path.
- Brokers are Mirae Asset (Kim Ki-ryong), KB Securities (Jang Moon-jun), Kiwoom Securities,
  NH Investment, Hyundai Motor Securities, IBK, Daishin.
- The **Jan 16 catalyst is a single-day attribution** taken from Korean same-day "특징주"
  (featured-stock) summaries: the Jeollanam-do MOU and the nuclear theme. The MOU itself is
  company-disclosed; the market's causation of a 21.7m-share day to those two items is
  reconstruction from after-close summaries, not a company filing.
- One secondary Korean outlet's delayed-quote widget shows Oct 29 as ₩11,480 / −19.55% against a
  ₩14,270 previous close. That conflicts with the Korea Exchange figure reported by Maeil Business
  (₩11,620, −18.57%), the TradingView scanner and Yahoo history, all of which agree. I use
  ₩11,620 / −18.57%.
- Not verified: whether any part of the January move reflected index rebalancing, foreign flow or
  short-covering rather than news. Daily volumes (6–20x normal) are consistent with a genuine
  re-rating but do not distinguish the flow type.
