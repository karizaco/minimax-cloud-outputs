## Notes
- `MCap Verified = NO` means no `shibui.valuation` row in the last 14 calendar days, so the name passes the $1B floor BY DEFAULT rather than on a stamped number. `MCap = —` is the same fact in the MCap column; the two columns are not redundant, they are the number and its provenance.
- `Post Lockup = YES` means more than 180 days have passed since the IPO date (the standard lockup length, used here as a calendar marker, not a filing fact).
- `Tradeable` is the closed-set verdict from the 30-bar average dollar volume: TRADEABLE >= $20M, THIN $5-20M, ILLIQUID < $5M, NO_QUOTE when no volume history.
- The IPO cohort and the 10-year scan are disjoint BY CONSTRUCTION — a name listed less than a year ago cannot have a 10-year base — so the `recent IPOs in the 10y scan` intersection is expected to be empty and is published to prove it was checked, not assumed.
