## Method, source health & caveats

- **Session alignment — proof this scan is for today:** TV implied previous close vs shibui's latest settled close: **12/12 agree** (100.0%) → mode **LIVE**. The scanner's session is pinned before any gate is applied.
- **The signal is TODAY's live candle, not yesterday's.** The routine proves this at runtime (alignment above); it no longer resolves the screen date as "shibui's latest settled date", which was a one-session lag.
- **The candle is the 16:00 ET close.** The screen was re-executed at **16:02 ET**, after the closing print, so the close, the DCR and the stop published here are the settled values rather than a 15:55 read. The entry price in the table **is** today's settled close; it is actionable as a next-session entry at or above that level, not as a market-on-close fill.
- **The volume gate uses the full session's volume** (the run executed after the 16:00 close), so the 290,000 floor is applied to the day's final volume.
- **ADR(20) is computed over the 20 SETTLED sessions before today**, not over a window that includes the incomplete live bar.
- **`vol_vs_30d_avg_pct` compares the session's final volume with the full-day 30-session mean.** `capitulation = CAPITULATION` at ≥ 150% of that mean, `LOW_VOLUME` below it. All three candidates are `LOW_VOLUME` — no name had the volume conviction behind the reversal.
