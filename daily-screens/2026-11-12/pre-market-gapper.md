- **Denominator:** every gap uses the **2026-11-11 settled close** — the prior sweep's own `focus.csv` `today_close` column, confirmed by TradingView, Cboe and Nasdaq per-symbol history on 12/12 names. `screen_close_resolved` (the screen's T+1 data date) was **rejected** by the resolver as a denominator, and three names also carry a drifted Nasdaq `secondaryData` close — none of those was used.
- **Tape freshness:** 4 pulls per symbol across the window, the payload with the newest internal timestamp wins; the newest per-symbol tape timestamps run 08:11:07–08:21:35 ET. PEPG was the only symbol that returned two distinct vintages (08:19:13 and 08:21:03) and the newer one was used.
- **Margin against the trigger:** the widest absolute gap on any source is **5.74%** against a 4.00% trigger — a 0.70x margin, so the single flag decision is immune to which cached vintage a source returns. The borderline readings (GDYN's quote, the five faded intraday crosses) are *inside* the band and are published as borderline, not as flags.
- **Known Nasdaq defects checked on every fire** (`/info` `secondaryData.lastSalePrice` not always the settled close, the field flipping mid-session, `primaryData.lastSalePrice` being a trivial-size print, `primaryData` being the ask, the bridge's `market_state: pre` serving the prior regular close, and the TradingView `premarket_volume` undercount) — the published gap is the tape's last executed print in every row, and the live quote is a separate column.
- **Volume source:** all 12 rows report the Nasdaq extended-trading cumulative counter as `pm_volume_source`. No tape contradicted a counter on this run. TradingView's `premarket_volume` is undercounting (MARA 497,399 vs 732,319) and is published only as a cross-check.
- **What could not be verified:** the TradingView `earnings_release_date` field is published as-is and is sometimes stale for a name whose move is not an earnings move (NBIS carries 08-12). Where no same-session catalyst was confirmed on the wire or in an issuer release, this artifact says so rather than inventing one.
