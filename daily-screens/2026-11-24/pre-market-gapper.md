- **Margin against the trigger:** the widest absolute gap on any source is **6.20%** against a 4.00% trigger — a 0.64x margin, so the flag decision is immune to which cached vintage a source returns.
- **Denominator:** every gap uses the **2026-11-23 settled close** — the prior sweep's own `focus.csv` close column, chosen BY DATE, with `screen_close_resolved` (the screen's T+1 data date) rejected as a denominator.
- **Tape freshness:** the newest print was 15-60 min behind the wall clock at the time of the pull. A stable cache returns the SAME vintage however many times it is asked, so the burst of pulls protects against a ROTATING cache but not against an old one — which is why `tape_lag_min` is published on every row.
- **Tape waves:** wave 1 at 08:35:52 ET (12 names); wave 2 at 08:38:03 ET (7 names). A wave that is spread across the run is what produces the divergence record; the re-pull wave only re-pulls the decision-relevant names (|gap| >= 2% or a stale tape), because a name below the near-miss band cannot change the flag decision.
- **Driver classification:** the gap is compared with the index proxy (SPY -1.31%), the sector proxy and bitcoin (-1.99% overnight from 11-23 16:00 ET) on the SAME TradingView snapshot as the name, so the two numbers are comparable. The published gap stays the Nasdaq tape's.
- **Known Nasdaq defects checked on every fire** (`/info` `secondaryData.lastSalePrice` not always the settled close, the field flipping mid-session, `primaryData.lastSalePrice` being a trivial-size print or the ask, the bridge's `market_state: pre` serving the prior regular close, and the TradingView `premarket_volume` undercount) — the published gap is the tape's last executed print in every row and the live quote is a separate column.
- **What could not be verified:** the TradingView `earnings_release_date` field is a *candidate* catalyst, not a confirmed one, and it is sometimes stale for a name whose move is not an earnings move. Where no same-session catalyst was confirmed on the wire or in an issuer release, this artifact says so rather than inventing one.
