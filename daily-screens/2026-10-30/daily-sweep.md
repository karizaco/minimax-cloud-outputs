Friday was an AI-led unwind: the S&P 500 lost 1.82% to 7,580.07, the Nasdaq-100 fell 3.46%, and VIX
jumped 12.9% to 19.42 after Alphabet's Thursday miss and then Amazon's and Intel's weak results
overnight. The important structural detail is that this was **not** a broad de-rating — SPY fell 1.77%
while equal-weight RSP fell only 0.79%, and 58.0% of the 1,001-name large-cap universe still closed
above its 20-DMA. Money left the AI complex and piled into energy, staples, utilities and health care.

That backdrop is what makes this sweep's numbers worth reading: the screens were computed on the
**10-29** bar, so the 10-30 column below is the scoreboard for what the setup actually did. Several
of the best names were still flat or down on the day the screen saw them, and moved the next session.

## Method and caveats

- **T+1 screens.** shibui's latest bar at fire time was 2026-10-29, so every stylist screen and every
  flag is computed on 10-29 data. The 10-30 column is the settled close applied afterwards.
- **CANSLIM is rebuilt on shibui.** TradingView's `earnings_growth_quarterly_yoy` and
  `sales_growth_quarterly_yoy` are null for every US name, so the Jeff Sun screen runs on shibui's
  quarterly `eps_growth_yoy` / `revenue_growth_yoy` (decimal fractions) with the RSI, SMA and market-cap
  gates from the spec.
- **The skill's published sweep SQL has five defects** (a missing CTE comma, a mismatched LAG alias
  family, mismatched UNION arity, a lookback shorter than the 252-day LAG it needs, and column names
  inherited from the first branch). All were repaired before running; the Qullamaggi relative-strength
  book returns 60 names instead of the empty result the published SQL produces.
- **The peoplewish book is truncated at 200 rows** by the shibui server's row cap. The sort is by
  14-day velocity descending, so the head of the book is intact but the tail is not visible.
- **The theme funnel selects leaders, not sectors.** The top-3 themes (XLE, XLV, IBB) map to two GICS
  sectors, and only the top-5 names by 3-month relative strength inside each sector that had already
  cleared another stylist screen are tagged.
- **Sub-procedures not run.** The 3:55 PM reversal-bullish scan (window 15:30-16:00 ET), the 09:00 ET
  pre-market gapper and the 09:45-11:15 ET intraday RVOL re-rank are all window-bound and outside an
  18:30 ET fire. The Sunday IPO and high-short-float refreshes do not apply to a Friday.
- **No tier-2 fallback was used or available.** Finviz is Cloudflare-403 from this egress and Yahoo
  JSON is rate-limited, so the market-cap floor was enforced strictly against shibui's own valuation
  table.
