Read the two float columns to attribute a divergence: if the FLOATS agree and the share counts do not, the short position itself is disputed; if the share counts agree and the FLOATS do not, only the float definition differs and the position is the same. ## Notes
- **Δ vs Prior Wk (pp)** is the change in `short_percent_float` against the 2026-11-14 state snapshot (written by the previous run to `pattern-study/cron06-state/`). `NO_PRIOR_SNAPSHOT` means the name was not in last week's snapshot, so there is no base to difference.
- `short_percent_float` is a fraction in shibui (`0.20` = 20%); values > 100% occur where disclosed short interest exceeds float — the `FLOAT_ARTIFACT` verdict marks those rows as noise, not squeeze fuel.
- `Tradeable` is the closed-set verdict: TRADEABLE >= $20M 30d ADVol, THIN $5-20M, ILLIQUID < $5M, NO_QUOTE when there is no volume history. `Squeeze = YES` requires short > 50% AND a TRADEABLE verdict.
- `30d Chg %` is the price change over the 30-bar window already pulled for the ADVol denominator — it costs no extra call.
