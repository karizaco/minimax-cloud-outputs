## Method, source health & caveats

| Source | Endpoint / query | Status | Role |

- **Margin against the trigger:** the widest absolute gap on any source is **6.90%** against a 4.00% trigger — a 0.58x margin, so the flag decision is immune to which cached vintage a source returns.
- **Denominator:** every gap uses the **2026-11-24 settled close** — the prior sweep's own `focus.csv` close column, chosen BY DATE, with `screen_close_resolved` (the screen's T+1 data date) rejected as a denominator.
- **Tape freshness:** the newest print was 15-20 min behind the wall clock at the time of the pull. A stable cache returns the SAME vintage however many times it is asked, so the burst of pulls protects against a ROTATING cache but not against an old one — which is why `tape_lag_min` is published on every row.
- **Tape waves:** wave 1 at 08:36:00 ET (12 names); wave 2 at 08:38:12 ET (11 names). A wave that is spread across the run is what produces the divergence record; the re-pull wave only re-pulls the decision-relevant names (|gap| >= 2% or a stale tape), because a name below the near-miss band cannot change the flag decision.
- **Known Nasdaq defects checked on every fire** (`/info` `secondaryData.lastSalePrice` not always the settled close, the field flipping mid-session, `primaryData.lastSalePrice` being a trivial-size print or the ask, the bridge's `market_state: pre` serving the prior regular close, and the TradingView `premarket_volume` undercount) — the published gap is the tape's last executed print in every row and the live quote is a separate column.
- **What could not be verified:** the TradingView `earnings_release_date` field is a *candidate* catalyst, not a confirmed one, and it is sometimes stale for a name whose move is not an earnings move. Where no same-session catalyst was confirmed on the wire or in an issuer release, this artifact says so rather than inventing one.
- The AI-hardware complex moved as ONE group on ONE story this morning, not name by name. NVDA, AXTI, APLD, ALAB, LITE, MXL, SMTC, SITM, CRWV, NBIS and DELL all gapped lower together as Monday's Jensen Huang reinstatement rally unwound, and Reuters reported the US trade representative was about to publish Section 301 'overcapacity' findings on 16 economies including Taiwan and South Korea, with semiconductors and electronics on the illustrative sector list.
