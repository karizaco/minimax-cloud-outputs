## Carry-over rows (the one material caveat)

**1,457 rows** (13.9% of the mapped universe) are
instruments that did not trade on 2026-10-26. For those, the screener row
carries the **last session's** close and change, dated with today's file.
That is true of the instrument but misleading as a statement about the day.

- The CSVs are left exactly as the source returned them. The schema is fixed
  at 14 columns by the skill, so the affected tickers are published in a
  companion index instead: `_carryover.json` (per region, per ticker, with
  the instrument's last trade date).
- 1,012 of the 1,457 are **confirmed** carry-overs: the screener's volume
  equals Yahoo's volume for Yahoo's last session, so both feeds are
  carrying the same earlier session. The remaining 445 have a non-zero
  screener volume but no Yahoo bar for today; for most, Yahoo's last trade
  is days old, so they are also carry-overs, but a few may be Yahoo
  coverage gaps rather than genuine non-trading.
- Headline movers above exclude carry-over rows entirely.
- The rate is a market-structure fact, not a feed defect: it is highest in
  Canada (939 rows, mostly TSXV/CSE
  venture names), Australia (235), Spain
  (73) and Italy
  (76), and near zero in the deep
  liquid markets (Japan 41,
  Germany 1,
  Finland 1).

## Other caveats

- **Illiquid tail.** The extreme-move counts across all rows
  (e.g. Canada 381 up / 660 down ≥3%) are dominated by sub-$100m
  micro-caps and single-print names. The liquidity filter above is what
  makes the headline tables readable.
- **Secondary listings survive.** A German company's London line, a Canadian
  company's CSE line, and class-share lines are distinct rows by design
  (the skill's known limitation). Cross-listed issuers appear in more than
  one region file with different change_pct.
- **No cross-file dedupe and no prior-day cache.** This sandbox was fresh:
  there was no universe cache and no 2026-10-23 CSV, so movers-mode
  forward-fill was impossible and every region is a full screener pull.
  First run of this cron that produced real regional data (earlier fires
  of cron #5 wrote only SKIPPED/STUB markers).
- **Sibling cron.** Cron #20 (`us-eod/`) wrote the same regions 25 minutes
  earlier from the same source. Row counts match; this cron is an
  independent re-pull, not a copy.
- **FX.** Market-cap floors use a fixed rate table (EUR 1.1178, JPY 0.006348,
  AUD 0.7019, CAD 1.4022, DKK 0.1495) taken from Yahoo at run time. They
  affect the liquidity filter only, never the written price fields.
- **No catalyst research was performed.** This cron is a data snapshot;
  movers are reported without news attribution, and no reason for a move is
  asserted here.
