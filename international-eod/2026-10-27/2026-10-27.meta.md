## Deviations and corrections this run

- **The skill's holiday calendar is wrong for Greece by one day, and Greece traded.** `holiday_calendar.py` lists Ochi Day 2026 as `2026-10-27`; the actual date is **Wednesday 2026-10-28** (timeanddate, holidays-info and the Athens Exchange 2026 calendar all agree, and the exchange's next closure is listed as 28 Oct). The ATHEX was open on 2026-10-27: Yahoo's daily bars for ALPHA.AT, EUROB.AT, TPEIR.AT, GEKTERNA.AT, MOH.AT and ETE.AT each carry a `10-27 08:30Z` session bar whose close matches the screener to the cent (4.353 / 4.379 / 9.792 / 40.74 / 69.30 / 15.63). The calendar's holiday skip exists to keep stale sessions out of the historical record, so applying it here would have dropped a real session rather than protected the record. **`eu-gr` is therefore included** and the calendar entry is the bug. The other 15 regions' open/closed status for 2026-10-27 matches the calendar (Taiwan, Austria and Ireland all reopen today after 2026-10-26 closures).
- **The FX table carried an inverted CAD rate.** TradingView's `market_cap_basic` is in **local currency** (verified by share-count reconciliation: SAP 1.163bn shares, Toyota 11.900bn, TSMC 25.770bn, BHP 4.875bn, Genmab 59.11m, DPM 221.8m — all match the issuers' real share counts), so it must be multiplied by USD-per-local-unit. The 2026-10-26 run used `CAD 1.402160`, which is CAD **per** USD, so Canadian market caps were converted in the wrong direction and inflated ~1.97x. Today's table uses `CAD 0.7117944` (1/1.4049). This changes Canada's liquidity filter and the mcap column only; no price field is affected, and yesterday's published files are left untouched.
- **The MCP layer is not bound in this session** (`mcp__tradingview_advanced__*` / `mcp__yahoo_finance__*` returned no tool definitions), so both upstreams were called directly over HTTPS. This is the one fallback in the run; it is not retried or chained.
- **Full pull, not movers mode.** The skill's default for Tue-Thu is movers mode (refresh only names that moved >3% on the prior session, forward-fill the rest). This run pulled the full universe for every region so that the Yahoo census could verify every written row rather than a sample. Cost: 16 screener pulls instead of 4; benefit: a complete full-universe cross-check.

## Carry-over rows (the one material caveat)

**1,486 rows** (11.5% of the written universe) are
instruments that did not trade on 2026-10-27. For those, the screener row
carries the **last session's** close and change, dated with today's file.
That is true of the instrument but misleading as a statement about the day.

- The CSVs are left exactly as the source returned them. The schema is fixed
  at 14 columns by the skill, so the affected tickers are published in a
  companion index instead: `_carryover.json` (per region, per ticker, with
  the instrument's last trade date).
- 1,043 of the 1,486 are **confirmed** carry-overs: the screener's volume
  equals Yahoo's volume for Yahoo's last session, so both feeds are
  carrying the same earlier session. The remainder have a non-zero screener
  volume but no Yahoo bar for today; for most, Yahoo's last trade is days
  old, so they are also carry-overs, but a few may be Yahoo coverage gaps
  rather than genuine non-trading.
- Headline movers above exclude carry-over rows entirely.
- The rate is a market-structure fact, not a feed defect. It is highest in
  - **ca** (868 rows),
  - **au** (256 rows),
  - **eu-es** (77 rows),
  - **eu-it** (74 rows),
  and near zero in the deep liquid markets (**eu-de** 0, **eu-ie** 1, **eu-fi** 1).

## Other caveats

- **Illiquid tail.** The extreme-move counts across all rows are dominated
  by sub-$100m micro-caps and single-print names. The liquidity filter above
  is what makes the headline tables readable.
- **Secondary listings survive.** A German company's London line, a Canadian
  company's CSE line, and class-share lines are distinct rows by design
  (the skill's known limitation). Cross-listed issuers appear in more than
  one region file with different change_pct.
- **No cross-file dedupe.** The same issuer listed in two regions appears in
  both files; this is the skill's documented known limitation, not a defect.
- **FX.** Market-cap floors use a rate table taken from Yahoo at run time
  (EUR 1.1130, JPY 0.0063312, AUD 0.7026913, CAD 0.7117944, DKK 0.1488804,
  TWD 0.0308604) — all USD per one unit of local currency. They affect the
  liquidity filter and the mcap column only, never the written price fields.
- **No catalyst research was performed.** This cron is a data snapshot;
  movers are reported without news attribution, and no reason for a move is
  asserted here.

- `international-eod/2026-10-27/<region>.csv` — 16 files, one per trading region
- `international-eod/2026-10-27/_carryover.json` — carry-over index
