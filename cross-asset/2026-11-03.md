The correlation structure is **stable but the tape underneath it is not**. The largest week-over-week pair move is **0.059** (IWM–UUP), well inside the 0.20 threshold, so there is **no regime shift** in the network itself. Equities remain one crowded bloc (SPY–QQQ +0.92, QQQ–EEM +0.88), implied vol still runs inversely to equities (SPY–^VIX -0.79), and the dollar is still the pivot proxy of the matrix (SPY–UUP -0.33, GLD–UUP -0.54).

What changed underneath is **growth**. The important structural consequence is **QQQ–TLT at +0.16**, the weakest pair in the matrix and the first genuinely useful long-duration hedge in this regime: growth and bonds have finally decoupled enough for duration to work again, even though the broad **stocks-bonds hedge is still broken** (SPY–TLT +0.30, IWM–TLT +0.47).

Today at ~10:15 ET the rotation continues with a **rates** driver: **QQQ +0.69% and IWM +0.41% while TLT -0.91%** — equities rising *with* bonds falling, which is the mirror image of the last week's growth-de-rating/bond-bid tape and would push SPY–TLT further positive if it persists. Gold is still the worst performer in the universe at **-14.54%** over six months, and the dollar is still the best equity proxy at **+5.65%**. Correlation is regime context only — not causation, not a forecast.
