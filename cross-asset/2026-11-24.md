**The equity complex is still one trade.** SPY–QQQ +0.94, SPY–IWM +0.84, SPY–EEM +0.83 and SPY–^VIX -0.89 leave no internal diversification; QQQ–TLT +0.02 is the only pair in the universe that is close to uncorrelated.
