Over the full six months **gold is still the worst performer at -16.02%** and the dollar the best equity proxy at +5.99% — and today's 10:05 ET partial bar (GLD +2.10%, EEM +1.52%, IWM +1.12% vs SPY +0.56%, QQQ +0.55%, UUP -0.28%) is the first meaningful challenge to that pattern.
