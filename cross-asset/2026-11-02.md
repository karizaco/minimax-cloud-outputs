## Deviations from the skill spec (read first)

- **The skill's named tool still does not exist.** `mcp__financekit__correlation_matrix` is not bound in this Cloud runtime, and no `mcp__*` finance tool is registered. This is a known, documented state of the environment — it is **not** counted as a source error.
- **What was used instead — and why it is not a fallback:** the matrix was computed **server-side by DuckDB `corr()`** through the MCP-over-HTTP bridge (`shibui.stock_data_query`, one round trip for the whole matrix), with the aligned price series injected as inline VALUES. The skill's intent — *one batched server-side call that returns the entire matrix* — is satisfied exactly. **No correlation arithmetic was performed in the agent.** The local Pearson computation the spec forbids does not exist in this run.
- **Price source:** Yahoo Finance Chart API (`/v8/finance/chart/{SYM}?range=1y&interval=1d`), short `User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0`. All 8 symbols returned HTTP 200 on the first attempt.
- **Independent cross-check:** the same `shibui` server's own raw `stock_quotes` table was queried over the same bridge for the 7 ETF proxies (VIX is not in shibui). It is used **only** to validate the Yahoo series, never to fill gaps. Maximum absolute difference vs the published matrix: **0.0214** (TLT–UUP) — within noise for a 127-observation correlation.
- **The 2026-11-02 bar is excluded from the matrix.** The session was still in progress at 10:05 ET (SPY volume 4.50M vs a normal full session in the tens of millions), so a partial bar is not a close. Its effect is quantified below rather than estimated: including it moves no pair by more than **0.0059**.
- **The WoW delta is a matched-length one-week roll.** Last week's window ended 2026-10-23 (128 sessions, 2026-04-23 → 2026-10-23); this one ends 2026-10-30 (128 sessions). Same length, shifted one week — not an overlapping comparison.
- Correlation is **regime context only** — not causation, not a forecast.
- The audit line the skill asks for was **not** written: the Cloud shared blocks instruct that audit writes are skipped in this runtime.

## TL;DR

The correlation structure is **quietly stable** — the largest week-over-week move is 0.06 absolute, a third of the 0.20 regime-shift threshold, so this is a genuine "no shift" reading rather than a noisy one. Equities remain one crowded bloc (SPY–QQQ +0.92, QQQ–EEM +0.88), implied vol still runs inversely to equities (SPY–^VIX -0.78), and the dollar is still the pivot proxy of the whole matrix (SPY–UUP -0.33, GLD–UUP -0.54). The one relationship that matters for hedging is **QQQ–TLT at +0.17** — the only pair in the matrix below the 0.20 "effectively uncorrelated" line, and it has drifted further *away* from equity-like correlation this week (from +0.20), so long duration is drifting back toward being useful insurance against growth equity. The stocks-bonds hedge itself remains **broken**: SPY–TLT +0.30 and IWM–TLT +0.48 are both clearly positive.

What moved was **price, not structure**, and it moved in the direction of broadening risk appetite. Over the trailing week small caps and EM led (IWM +1.30%, EEM -1.56% but +1.47% today) while QQQ lagged (-1.97%) and gold fell hardest (-2.95%). Over the full six months **gold is still the worst performer in the universe at -16.02%**, and the dollar is the best equity proxy at +5.99%. Today at 10:05 ET that leadership is repeating in real time: GLD +2.08%, EEM +1.47%, IWM +1.12% against SPY +0.56% and QQQ +0.56%, with UUP -0.31% as the dollar softens — a rotation toward beaten-down cyclicals and a bounce in gold, not a flight to quality.
