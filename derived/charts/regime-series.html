Two charts, not one chart with two axes. Breadth is a percentage and VIX is an index level. Putting them on one pair of y-axes would let whoever drew it choose where the two lines cross, and therefore choose the story. They share an x-axis instead, so they can still be read together without the crossing point being an authored artifact.

The gaps are real and are drawn as gaps. 8 of 14 sessions publish a breadth figure and 12 publish a VIX. The line breaks where the routine published nothing rather than interpolating across a session it did not measure — 2026-10-19, 2026-10-22, 2026-10-23, 2026-10-26 carry no breadth value at all, and the files before 2026-11-05 predate the _schema: 2 regime file.

What this does not show. It is not a market index and not a return. Breadth here is the screen's own breadth input, which is a share of names above a moving average, so it says how many names participate — never how much anything moved. The two charts are regime context for reading a daily brief, and nothing in them states a direction.
