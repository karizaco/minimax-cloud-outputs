# CHRS (core) study result call — 2026-10-28

## TL;DR (max 3 bullets)
- Coherus reported compelling interim tagmokitug data in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC): first-line confirmed ORR was 75% at the October 14 cutoff and 89% after two post-cutoff scans, while PD-L1-positive ORR remained 100%; second-line data at the 700 mg dose showed a confirmed ORR of 16.7%, 33.3% clinical benefit rate, and 5.5-month median PFS.
- Management is prioritizing an integrated ESCC development plan: expand first-line cohorts to 30–40 patients, mature durability data, align on the 700 mg dose with the FDA in first-quarter 2027, and potentially pursue accelerated approval for first- and second-line indications.
- Activity was also observed in head-and-neck, upper-GI adenocarcinoma, and colorectal cancer, generally concentrated at 700 mg; management characterized safety as manageable, with no new safety signals or grade-five tagmokitug-related events.
